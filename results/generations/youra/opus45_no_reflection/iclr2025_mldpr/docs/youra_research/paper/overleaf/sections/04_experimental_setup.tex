\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{Research Questions}

\begin{description}
    \item[RQ1:] Does aggregate Gini exhibit structural breaks within 2019--2022?
    \item[RQ2:] Did foundation papers achieve exceptional citation impact?
    \item[RQ3:] Did emergent benchmark creation accelerate post-2020?
    \item[RQ4:] Did attention shift from traditional to emergent benchmarks?
    \item[RQ5:] Did modality dynamics diverge after foundation model emergence?
\end{description}

\subsection{Dataset}

Papers With Code benchmark data: January 2018 -- December 2024 (84 months).

\subsection{Baselines}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Monotonic Trend (Null):} Single trend without structural breaks
    \item \textbf{Koch et al.~\cite{koch2021reduced}:} Gini 0.6--0.7 for 2015--2020 as baseline concentration
\end{itemize}
