## Executive Summary

**GATE RESULT: PASS**

H-M3 validates that researcher attention shifted from traditional benchmarks to emergent-capability benchmarks post-2021. The emergent benchmark share increased from 7.53% (pre-2021) to 26.54% (post-2021), with highly significant chi-square test results (χ² = 1025.23, p < 10⁻²²⁴).

## 3. Ablation Analysis: Time Window Sensitivity

| Split Date | Pre-Split Emergent Share | Post-Split Emergent Share | Change | Chi² | p-value |
|------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|--------|------|---------|
| 2020-01 | 6.38% | 26.70% | +20.32% | 1147.93 | 1.26e-251 |
| **2021-01** | **7.53%** | **26.54%** | **+19.02%** | **1025.23** | **5.88e-225** |
| 2022-01 | 46.10% | 6.33% | -39.76% | 6482.98 | 0.0 |

**Interpretation:** The attention shift is robust to split date variations between 2020 and 2021. The 2022 split shows reversed trends because most emergent benchmarks were introduced between 2020-2022, so post-2022 data reflects matured usage patterns.
