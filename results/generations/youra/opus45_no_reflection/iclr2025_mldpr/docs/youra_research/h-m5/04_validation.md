# Validation Report: H-M5

**Hypothesis:** Modality Divergence (Phase Transition Effect)
**Statement:** Post-2021 Pearson correlation between CV and NLP Gini series drops below 0.4 from pre-2020 baseline of >0.6
**Gate Type:** SHOULD_WORK
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Result:** FAIL

---

## Executive Summary

H-M5 tested whether foundation model emergence caused benchmark concentration dynamics to fragment along modality lines. The hypothesis predicted that CV-NLP Gini correlation would be >0.6 pre-2020 (unified dynamics) and <0.4 post-2021 (diverged dynamics).

**Outcome:** The hypothesis is NOT supported. Pre-2020 CV-NLP correlation was -0.13 (not >0.6), indicating modalities never had unified concentration dynamics to begin with. The phase transition model assumes a baseline of coordinated behavior that does not exist in the data.

## Failure Analysis

### Why the Hypothesis Failed

1. **No baseline unified dynamics:** Pre-2020 CV-NLP correlation was negative (-0.13), not positive (>0.6). The assumption of unified concentration dynamics before foundation models does not hold.

2. **Correlation increased post-2021:** Contrary to the divergence hypothesis, CV-NLP correlation actually increased from -0.13 to +0.23. This suggests modalities became MORE correlated after foundation models, not less.

3. **Not statistically significant:** Even the direction of change (from negative to positive correlation) is not statistically significant (p=0.17).

### Failure Pivot Analysis (Other Modality Pairs)

|------|-------|--------|-------|

---

## Interpretation

The phase transition framework proposed unified benchmark concentration dynamics pre-2020 that fragmented post-foundation-models. The data shows:

1. **Modalities were always independent:** CV and NLP benchmark concentration trajectories were weakly negatively correlated even before 2020.

2. **Foundation models may have synchronized, not fragmented:** The slight positive correlation post-2021 could indicate that foundation models (which often bridge CV and NLP) may have introduced more cross-modality coherence.

3. **Alternative explanation:** Benchmark concentration within each modality may be driven by modality-specific factors (dataset availability, hardware requirements, research community size) rather than field-wide dynamics.

## Implications for Main Hypothesis

H-M5 was the final MECHANISM hypothesis (SHOULD_WORK gate). Its failure does not invalidate the main hypothesis H-BenchmarkPhaseTransition but indicates:

- The phase transition manifested differently than predicted
- Modality-specific dynamics were already present pre-2020
- The transition may have caused convergence rather than divergence

The main hypothesis chain remains valid through H-M4 (all MUST_WORK and prior SHOULD_WORK gates passed). H-M5 failure represents a refinement of understanding, not a rejection of the phase transition phenomenon.
