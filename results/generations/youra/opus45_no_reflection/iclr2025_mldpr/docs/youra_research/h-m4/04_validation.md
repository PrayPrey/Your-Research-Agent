1. **Share comparison**: Traditional vs emergent benchmark share of total papers
2. **Persistence check**: Absolute paper counts on traditional benchmarks

## Interpretation

1. **Reduced Dominance Confirmed**: Traditional benchmarks account for only 11.70% of total benchmark papers, well below the 50% dominance threshold.

2. **Persistence Confirmed**: With 47,068 papers, traditional benchmarks maintain substantial research presence.

3. **Quality vs Quantity Trade-off**: Traditional benchmarks have fewer datasets (67) but higher average papers per dataset (702.5) compared to emergent benchmarks (158 datasets, 102.0 avg papers). This suggests traditional benchmarks remain important reference points despite reduced share.

4. **Complementary to H-M3**: H-M3 confirmed attention shift toward emergent benchmarks (7.53% → 26.54% share post-2021). H-M4 confirms the complementary pattern: traditional benchmarks persist but with reduced dominance.
