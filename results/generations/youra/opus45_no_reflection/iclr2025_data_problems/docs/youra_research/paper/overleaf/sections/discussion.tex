\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings}

\textbf{TRAK's Architecture-Invariance.} Random projection averages out architecture-specific patterns. For diverse architecture pipelines, TRAK provides robust default.

\textbf{TracIn's Encoder Preference.} Gradient dot-products benefit from dense bidirectional gradients. $\sim$3\% BERT advantage at low compute.

\textbf{EK-FAC's Unexpected Robustness.} Kronecker approximation more architecture-agnostic than predicted. No decoder advantage observed.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Statistical power:} 2 seeds provide directional evidence; 5+ seeds needed for P1/P2 confirmation
    \item \textbf{Single dataset:} SST-2 results may not generalize to all tasks
    \item \textbf{Model scale:} $\sim$110M parameters; effects may differ at 1B+ scale
\end{itemize}
