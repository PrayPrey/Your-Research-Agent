\section{Discussion}

\textbf{Existence Validated.} Behavioral fingerprints---class-specific accuracy patterns---exist in model zoos. 67.6\% of variance is behavioral, not explained by overall accuracy.

\textbf{Mechanism Failed.} Simple weight statistics (25 features) do not capture this signal. Contributing factors:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Sample size: 193 models vs.\ recommended $\sim$30,000
    \item Feature expressivity: Scalar statistics may miss distributional/cross-layer information
    \item Model convergence: Mean accuracy 18.7\% suggests undertrained models
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Implications.} Learned representations (NF-Layers, SANE, behavioral autoencoders) warranted for behavioral prediction.

\textbf{Limitations.} Small sample, single feature set, single architecture.
