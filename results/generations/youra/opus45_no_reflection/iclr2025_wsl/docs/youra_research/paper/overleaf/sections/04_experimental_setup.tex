\section{Experimental Setup}

\textbf{Dataset.} Small CNN Zoo: 193 final-epoch models, 3-conv + 2-FC architecture, CIFAR-10.

\textbf{H-E1 Protocol.} Compute $193 \times 10$ class-wise accuracy matrix; fit per-class linear baselines; compute residual ratio.

\textbf{H-M1 Protocol.} Extract 25 weight features; 80/20 split; Ridge regression per class; compare $R^2$ against baseline.

\textbf{Threshold.} Residual ratio $> 0.05$ (existence); $\Delta R^2 > 0$ (mechanism).
