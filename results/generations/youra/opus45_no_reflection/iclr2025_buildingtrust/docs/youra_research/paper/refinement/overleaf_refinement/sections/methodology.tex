
We describe our approach to testing the calibration-mediation hypothesis. The methodology involves three phases: metric collection, correlation analysis, and mediation testing.

\subsubsection{Hypothesis Structure}

The core hypothesis decomposes into four testable sub-hypotheses:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
ID & Type & Statement & Gate \\
\midrule
H-E1 & Existence & Correlation r > 0.5 exists between MC1 and (1-ASR) & MUST\_WORK \\
H-M1 & Mechanism & ECE variance > 0.001 across models & MUST\_WORK \\
H-M2 & Mechanism & Lower ECE predicts higher MC1 & SHOULD\_WORK \\
H-M3 & Mechanism & ECE mediates $\geq 30$\% of correlation & SHOULD\_WORK \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}
\end{table}

\subsubsection{Testable Predictions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{P1}: Cross-model correlation r > 0.5 between MC1 and (1-ASR)
\item \textbf{P2}: ECE mediates $\geq 30$\% of the correlation
\item \textbf{P3}: Temperature scaling improves both metrics
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Metrics}

\textbf{Factuality: TruthfulQA MC1} --- Multiple-choice single-answer accuracy on 817 questions using lm-evaluation-harness. This benchmark specifically tests whether models generate truthful answers rather than plausible-sounding falsehoods.

\textbf{Robustness: TextFooler (1 - ASR)} --- TextFooler attack applied to SST-2 sentiment classification, with 500+ examples per model. Attack Success Rate (ASR) measures the proportion of correctly classified examples that can be flipped by word substitutions. Robustness is computed as 1 - ASR.

\textbf{Calibration: ECE} --- Expected Calibration Error with 10 equal-frequency bins computed from TruthfulQA logit predictions. ECE = $\Sigma$ (|accuracy\_bin - confidence\_bin|) weighted by bin size.

\subsubsection{Analysis Pipeline}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Correlation Analysis}: Pearson correlation between MC1 and (1-ASR) with bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals (1000 resamples).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Confound Control}: Partial correlation controlling for log(parameters) to isolate relationship from scale effects. Within-family analysis to control for training data confounds.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Mediation Analysis}: Baron-Kenny mediation framework testing MC1 $\rightarrow$ ECE $\rightarrow$ (1-ASR) pathway. Sobel test for indirect effect significance.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Intervention Validation}: Temperature scaling applied post-hoc, measuring before/after changes in MC1, ASR, and ECE.
\end{enumerate}
