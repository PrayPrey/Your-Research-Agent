\section{Experimental Setup}

We design three experiments to test the BiDPO causal chain:

\textbf{E1: Orthogonality Validation.} Compute Pearson correlation between collaboration scores and preference labels on 2000 response pairs. Success criterion: $|r| < 0.7$.

\textbf{E2: Training Stability.} Train BiDPO on 4000 samples for 250 steps. Monitor loss and numerical stability. Success criteria: training completes without NaN/Inf; final loss $<$ initial loss.

\textbf{E3: Generation Transfer.} Generate responses from BiDPO and DPO models on 500 held-out prompts. Perform one-sided t-test. Success criteria: $p < 0.05$, Cohen's $d \geq 0.2$.
