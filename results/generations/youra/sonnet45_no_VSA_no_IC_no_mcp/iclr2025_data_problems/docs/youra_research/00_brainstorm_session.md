## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we address persistent and emerging data-related challenges in foundation model development and deployment?

### Refined Question

What systematic approaches can address data curation, attribution, and evaluation challenges in foundation models while ensuring fairness, privacy, and copyright compliance?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Data Collection and Curation for Foundation Models**
   - What are practical strategies for curating data (filtering, mixing, repairing) tailored to FM training stages?
   - How can data curation techniques be extended to RAG, multimodal settings, and LLM agents?
   - What theoretical frameworks can guide data selection and scaling laws for foundation models?

2. **Data Attribution, Interpretability, and Data Marketplaces**
   - What efficient techniques exist for attributing model outputs to specific training data?
   - How can we evaluate and compare data attribution methods?
   - What economic models enable fair data pricing and marketplace design?

3. **Legal and Technical Solutions for Data Copyright Protection**
   - What mitigation strategies and mathematical frameworks address copyright issues in FM training data?
   - How do copyright, privacy, and fairness connect, including adaptations like machine unlearning?

4. **Synthetic Data and Model Collapse**
   - How can high-quality synthetic data generation impact FM performance, robustness, and safety?
   - What theoretical and empirical investigations help understand and mitigate model collapse?

5. **Data and Society (Safety, Privacy, Fairness, and Other Social Impacts)**
   - How can data-centric approaches improve AI safety, privacy, and fairness?
   - What are the side effects of data curation on fairness and ethics in FMs?

6. **Benchmarks and Evaluations**
   - How can we design evaluation metrics for data-centric techniques and create reliable dataset benchmarks?
   - What pitfalls exist in current dataset benchmarks (e.g., test data contamination)?
