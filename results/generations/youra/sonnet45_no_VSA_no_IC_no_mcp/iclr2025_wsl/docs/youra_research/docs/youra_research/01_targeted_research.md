# Targeted Research Report: How can we formulate testable research hypotheses that comply with strict feasibility constraints: existing datasets only, existing benchmarks only, no human evaluation, no synthetic data?

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Phase:** 1 - Targeted Research Gathering
**Phase Output:** Research data, gaps (Pre-hypothesis, targeted approach)

## Executive Summary

**Research Question:** How to formulate testable hypotheses under strict feasibility constraints (existing datasets/benchmarks only, no human eval, no synthetic data)

**Approach:** Targeted research via query-based investigation (MCP-based search unavailable in test environment)

**Key Finding:** Three critical gaps identified:
1. Dataset-Benchmark Compatibility Catalog
2. Automated Evaluation Methodology Taxonomy  
3. Constraint-Driven Research Design Patterns

**Status:** Phase 1 complete - ready for Phase 2A hypothesis generation
**Limitation:** No MCP servers in test environment - gaps identified from constraint analysis only

## 8. Research Gaps

### Gap 1: Dataset-Benchmark Compatibility Catalog Gap
**Current State:** Researchers must manually verify dataset-benchmark pairs compatibility before hypothesis formulation
**Missing Piece:** Systematic catalog mapping existing datasets to compatible existing benchmarks with automated evaluation criteria
**Potential Impact:** High - Directly blocks constraint-compliant hypothesis generation

### Gap 2: Automated Evaluation Methodology Taxonomy Gap
**Current State:** No comprehensive taxonomy of automated metrics that avoid human scoring exists
**Missing Piece:** Structured taxonomy categorizing automated evaluation methods by domain, metric type, and benchmark compatibility
**Potential Impact:** High - Essential for constraint-compliant experiment design

### Gap 3: Constraint-Driven Research Design Pattern Library Gap
**Current State:** Limited documented patterns for research design under strict resource constraints
**Missing Piece:** Pattern library of successful constraint-compliant research designs with reproducible templates
**Potential Impact:** Medium - Accelerates feasible hypothesis formulation
