\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

We design four research questions (RQs) that map directly to the claims in the Introduction.

\textbf{RQ1 (Main efficiency claim):} Does GNN-NFN achieve a sample efficiency ratio $\geq 2{\times}$ over flat-MLP on shared ModelZooDataset splits?

\textbf{RQ2 (Mechanistic grounding):} Is permutation equivariance verified to floating-point precision for GNN-NFN and DWSNets?

\textbf{RQ3 (Data-regime dependence):} Is PermAug always intermediate between plain and structural equivariance, or does the ordering depend on training set size?

\textbf{RQ4 (Full-scale convergence):} At full training scale, do all encoder types converge in performance---consistent with \citet{Dayan2026Expressive}?
