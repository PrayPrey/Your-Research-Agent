
\subsubsection{4.1 Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Does MBPP exhibit a non-degenerate binary execution reward variance distribution for DeepSeek-Coder-7B-Instruct? Is there a meaningful subset with variance > 0.1? \textit{(Experiment h-e1)}

\textbf{RQ2 (Selection Signal):} Does top-50 variance-guided selection achieve statistically significantly higher mean variance than random-50? \textit{(Experiment h-m1)}

\textbf{RQ3 (Gradient Concentration):} Does GRPO training on the variance-selected subset produce lower frac\_reward\_zero\_std than random-50 selection? \textit{(Experiments h-m2, h-m3)}

\subsubsection{4.2 Dataset and Model}

\textbf{Dataset:} MBPP training split \citep{Austin2021} --- 374 Python programming problems (\texttt{google-research-datasets/mbpp}, subset=full, split=train, task IDs 601--974). MBPP is the standard benchmark for binary execution reward RLEF \citep{Gehring2024}.

\textbf{Model:} DeepSeek-Coder-7B-Instruct (deepseek-ai/deepseek-coder-7b-instruct-v1.5) \citep{Shao2024DeepSeekMath} --- a 7B-parameter instruction-tuned code generation model, confirmed functional with TRL GRPOTrainer in this environment (torch 2.6.0+cu124, CUDA 12.4, NVIDIA H100 NVL).

\subsubsection{4.4 Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{count\textbackslash \_nonzero\textbackslash \_variance}: number of problems with v\_i > 0.1 (gate threshold: $\geq 15$ for RQ1)
\item \texttt{mean\textbackslash \_var\textbackslash \_selected} vs \texttt{mean\textbackslash \_var\textbackslash \_random}: Mann-Whitney U test, one-sided, $\alpha =0.05$ (RQ2)
\item \texttt{frac\textbackslash \_reward\textbackslash \_zero\textbackslash \_std} per training step: primary training diagnostic (RQ3)
\item HumanEval+ pass@1 via EvalPlus \citep{Liu2023}: planned; not conducted due to cold-start
\end{itemize}
