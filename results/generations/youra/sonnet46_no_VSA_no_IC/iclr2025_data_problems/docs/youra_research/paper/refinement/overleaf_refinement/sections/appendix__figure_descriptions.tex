
\textbf{Figure 1} (\texttt{fig1\textbackslash \_domain\textbackslash \_proxy\textbackslash \_comparison.png}): Bar chart showing mean entity density, narrative coherence, and formal syntax density for all 21 Pile domains (200 documents each, domain-representative generated texts). Wikipedia shows the highest entity density (0.2553); BookCorpus2 shows the highest narrative coherence (0.0190); GitHub shows the highest formal syntax density (0.1247). Error bars show 95\% confidence intervals. Source: h-m1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 2} (\texttt{fig2\textbackslash \_focal\textbackslash \_violins.png}): Violin plots comparing the three focal domains (Wikipedia, BookCorpus2, GitHub) across all three cognitive proxies. Distributions confirm the domain separation observed in the ANOVA. Source: h-m1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 3} (\texttt{fig3\textbackslash \_tukey\textbackslash \_heatmap.png}): 21 $\times$ 21 Tukey HSD rejection matrix for entity density. Dark cells indicate significant pairwise differences (p\_adj < 0.001). Wikipedia vs. all book domains and GitHub vs. all non-code domains are among the most significant comparisons. Source: h-m1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 4} (\texttt{fig4\textbackslash \_proxy\textbackslash \_scatter.png}): Scatter plot of narrative coherence vs. entity density for all documents, colored by domain. Clear domain clustering confirms that domains occupy distinct regions of the cognitive proxy space. Source: h-m1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 5} (\texttt{gate\textbackslash \_metrics.png}): Per-domain standard deviation of cumulative exposure fractions across 154 checkpoints. The horizontal dashed line marks the std > 0.001 gate threshold. Ten domains fall above the threshold; twelve (including Books3, GitHub, BookCorpus2) are at or near zero. Source: h-e1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 6} (\texttt{trajectories.png}): Cumulative domain exposure fraction trajectories for the top-5 and bottom-5 variance domains across all 154 training checkpoints. High-variance domains show monotonic but non-uniform growth; zero-variance domains are flat at 0.0. Source: h-e1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 7} (\texttt{variance\textbackslash \_heatmap.png}): Heatmap of per-domain trajectory standard deviation for all 22 domains $\times$ 3 model sizes (70M, 1B, 6.9B). Near-identical columns across model sizes confirm that domain exposure trajectories are a property of data ordering, not model-specific dynamics. Source: h-e1 analysis.

\textbf{Figure 8} (\texttt{spearman\textbackslash \_matrix.png}): Spearman correlation matrix between domain exposure trajectories across the three model sizes. Near-unity correlations ($\rho \approx$ 1.0 for all pairs) confirm cross-scale consistency. Source: h-e1 analysis.
