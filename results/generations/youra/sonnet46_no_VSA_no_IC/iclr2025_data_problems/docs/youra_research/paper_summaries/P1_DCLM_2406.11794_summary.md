# Paper Summary: DataComp-LM (DCLM)
**arXiv:** 2406.11794 | **Citations:** 398 | **Year:** 2024
**Authors:** Jeffrey Li et al.

### Key Contributions
- Benchmarks data curation (dedup, filtering, mixing) at 412M-7B scale
- DCLM-Baseline achieves 64% MMLU — best open dataset at time of publication
- Shows model-based filtering (FastText/BERT classifiers) > heuristic filtering by significant margin
- Provides 53-task evaluation suite covering commonsense, knowledge, reasoning

### Methodology
- Controlled ablation: fixed model architecture (decoder-only transformer), vary data pipeline
- Filters tested: URL blocklists, language detection, deduplication (exact + near-dup), quality classifiers
- Evaluation on MMLU, HellaSwag, ARC, WinoGrande, and 49 additional tasks
- Scale: 1B token to 7B parameter models

### Experiments & Results
- DCLM-Baseline (model-based filtering): 64.0% MMLU at 7B
- MAP-Neo baseline (heuristic filtering): ~57% MMLU — 6.6pp gap
- Deduplication alone: +2-3pp across benchmarks
- Quality classifier threshold is most sensitive hyperparameter

### Limitations & Gaps
- Reports aggregate/holistic scores — does NOT isolate which filter stage affects which benchmark
- No filter-by-filter × benchmark-by-benchmark correlation matrix
- Cannot determine whether dedup improvement comes from ARC vs HellaSwag vs MMLU

### Potential Relevance to Gap 1
HIGH — provides the evaluation framework and demonstrates filtering effects exist, but does not provide the per-filter × per-benchmark correlation analysis that Gap 1 targets.
