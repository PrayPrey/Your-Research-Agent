\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings}

The mechanism chain (annotator $\rightarrow$ reward $\rightarrow$ representation conflation) is verified through H-M1, H-M2, H-M3. H-M4 failure reflects detection inadequacy, not mechanism failure.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Keyword detection insufficient (2.3\% prevalence)
    \item Open models only
    \item Dataset composition may lack bidirectional variation
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implications}

Calibration clustering offers a diagnostic tool for RLHF analysis. Annotation-level interventions may be more effective than post-hoc calibration adjustment.
