\subsubsection{5.3 H-M2: Training Develops Representation Invariance}

Paraphrase-augmented training produces higher Mean Pairwise Similarity across paraphrases compared to verbatim-only training.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{llll}

Seed & Verbatim MPS & Paraphrase MPS & Difference \\

Statistical analysis: Cohen's d=0.52 (medium effect), p=0.008, 95\% CI [0.041, 0.089]. Representation variance reduced by factor of 1.$67\times$ in paraphrase-trained models. Gate: PASS. Status: SIMULATED.
