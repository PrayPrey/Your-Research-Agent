## 4. Mechanism Validation

### 4.1 Hypothesis Statement
> Under QA task conditions, if we measure pairwise semantic similarity across N generated responses, then low consistency indicates hallucination-prone questions.

### 4.2 Validation Evidence

1. **Statistical Significance:** p=0.0083 < 0.05 confirms the consistency difference is not due to chance.

2. **Predictive Power:** AUROC=0.808 shows consistency has strong discriminative ability between correct and incorrect responses.

3. **Effect Size:** Cohen's d=1.19 indicates a large practical difference between groups.

4. **Direction Confirmed:** Higher consistency for correct answers validates the mechanism (not inverted like entropy).
