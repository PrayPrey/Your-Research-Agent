\subsection{Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 (Feasibility):} Can both metrics be reliably computed?

\textbf{RQ2 (Entropy Signal):} Does higher entropy correlate with incorrect answers?

\textbf{RQ3 (Consistency Signal):} Does lower consistency correlate with incorrect answers?

\textbf{RQ4 (Fusion Benefit):} Does combining signals improve over the best single metric?
