\section{Experimental Setup}

We design experiments to answer three research questions:

\textbf{RQ1:} Do models trained on high-popularity datasets exhibit larger generalization gaps?

\textbf{RQ2:} Do popular benchmarks attract disproportionate research investment?

\textbf{RQ3:} Does texture bias explain the mechanism?

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Generalization Gap:} $\text{gap} = \text{acc}_{\text{in-domain}} - \text{acc}_{\text{held-out}}$
    \item \textbf{Paper Ratio:} High-use / low-use optimization paper counts
    \item \textbf{Texture Bias:} Proportion of texture-aligned predictions on conflict stimuli
\end{itemize}
