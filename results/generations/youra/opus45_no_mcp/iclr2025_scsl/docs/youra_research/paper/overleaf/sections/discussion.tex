\section{Discussion}

\textbf{Key Findings:} Crystallization is localized (15--40\% of training), detection is reliable on benchmarks with strong spurious correlations, and commitment is classifier-level (representations preserve both features).

\textbf{Limitations:} Architecture scope (ResNet-50 only), PoC validation level, timing hypothesis refinement required, domain scope (vision only). We did not compare our second derivative detection method against alternative detection metrics such as loss curvature or gradient norms; evaluating these alternatives remains future work. \textbf{Detection rate variability:} Our 100\% detection rate was validated primarily on Waterbirds; preliminary results suggest detection reliability depends on spurious correlation strength and may be lower on benchmarks with weaker or more complex spurious signals.

\textbf{Connection to Prior Work:} We extend simplicity bias \citep{shah2020simplicity} with temporal dimension, confirm gradient starvation causality \citep{pezeshki2021gradient}, and align with representation findings \citep{kirichenko2023last}.
