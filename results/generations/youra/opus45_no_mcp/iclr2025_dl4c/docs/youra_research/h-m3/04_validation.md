## Interpretation

The results confirm H-M3's mechanism hypothesis:

1. **Gradient Concentration Differs**: U_line errors show higher gradient concentration at ground-truth locations (mean=1.594) compared to U_ignore errors (mean=1.398).

2. **Statistical Significance**: The difference is highly significant (p < 1e-13), rejecting the null hypothesis that gradient concentration is equal.

3. **Noise Manifestation**: U_ignore samples show noise ratio > 1.0 on average (1.311), meaning gradients concentrate more at traceback locations than ground-truth locations when localization is unreliable.

4. **Mechanism Validated**: When fine-grained penalties target unreliable traceback locations (U_ignore errors where traceback ≠ ground truth 80% of time per H-M2), gradient signal becomes noise - it concentrates at wrong tokens rather than the actual error source.

---

## Limitations

1. **Synthetic Samples**: Used controlled synthetic samples rather than real APPS dataset samples for deterministic ground truth.

2. **Effect Size**: Cohen's d (0.477) is slightly below the 0.5 threshold for medium effect, though statistically significant.

3. **Model Scale**: Used CodeT5-small (60M params); effect may differ with larger models.
