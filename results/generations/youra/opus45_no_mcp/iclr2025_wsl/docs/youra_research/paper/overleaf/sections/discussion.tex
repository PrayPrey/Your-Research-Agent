\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings Interpretation}

Layer-wise encoding improves accuracy prediction by $\Delta r = 0.126$ (30\% relative gain) because it preserves functional hierarchy: different layers learn different abstractions, and their weight statistics reflect these differences.

\subsection{Incomplete Ablation}

GRB alignment at 61K scale was computationally prohibitive. NFN evaluation was blocked by the H-M2 prerequisite. The complete ablation ladder remains partially evaluated.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Single dataset}: CIFAR-10 Model Zoo only
    \item \textbf{Partial ablation}: 2 of 4 steps completed
    \item \textbf{CPU-only constraints}: Limited experiment scale
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Broader Implications}

\textbf{Preserve structure when embedding weights.} This principle should generalize beyond our specific statistics. Our ablation methodology provides a template for future systematic comparisons.
