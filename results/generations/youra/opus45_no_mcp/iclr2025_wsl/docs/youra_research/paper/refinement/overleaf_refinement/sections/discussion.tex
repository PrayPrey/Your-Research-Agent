
\subsubsection{Interpretation of Main Finding}

Layer-wise encoding improves accuracy prediction by $\Delta r$ = 0.126 compared to flattening. Each neural network layer learns different levels of abstraction. Early layers detect edges; deeper layers encode semantic features. Their weight distributions reflect these functional differences. Flattening destroys this hierarchical structure. Layer-wise encoding preserves the functional organization.

The improvement of $\Delta r$ = 0.126 exceeds the pre-specified threshold of $\Delta r$ > 0.1 by 26\%, suggesting the threshold was appropriately conservative.

\subsubsection{Incomplete Ablation}

The Git Re-Basin alignment step (H-M2) could not be completed due to computational constraints. The O($n^{2})$ complexity of alignment correlation computation at 61K model scale exceeded available CPU-only resources. The NFN evaluation was consequently not initiated, as it was planned to follow successful completion of the alignment experiment.

\subsubsection{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Single dataset}: All experiments conducted on CIFAR-10 Model Zoo only. Generalization to other architectures or domains was not evaluated.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Partial ablation}: Two of four planned ablation steps were completed. Claims about alignment preprocessing and equivariant architectures cannot be made.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{CPU-only constraints}: Hardware limitations prevented completion of computationally intensive experiments.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Architecture scope}: The Model Zoo contains only CNN checkpoints. Results may not extend to Vision Transformers or other architectures.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Single property}: Only accuracy prediction was evaluated. Other model properties (robustness, calibration) were not tested.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Implications}

The finding that layer-wise encoding substantially outperforms flattening suggests that structural preservation matters for weight embedding quality. This provides guidance for embedding method design: preserving layer boundaries should be considered a baseline requirement rather than an optional enhancement.
