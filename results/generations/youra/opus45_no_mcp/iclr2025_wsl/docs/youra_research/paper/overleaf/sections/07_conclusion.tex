\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether respecting neural network structure improves weight embedding quality. The answer is unambiguously yes.

Layer-wise encoding improves accuracy prediction correlation by $\Delta r = 0.126$ ($p < 0.001$) over na\"ive flattening. This 30\% relative improvement holds across all five random seeds.

Three implications:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Layer-wise encoding should be the default baseline, not flattening
    \item Structural preservation matters in weight space as in input space
    \item Controlled ablation enables fair cross-method comparison
\end{enumerate}

The goal is universal weight embeddings generalizing across architectures and properties. Our results suggest structural inductive biases---starting with layer boundaries---are essential ingredients.
