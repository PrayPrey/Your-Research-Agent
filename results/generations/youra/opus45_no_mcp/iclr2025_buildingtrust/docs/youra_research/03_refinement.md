## Final Hypothesis

### Title
CoT + Verbalized Confidence for Super-Additive Calibration

### Hypothesis ID
H-CoTCalibration-v1

### Core Claim
Under multiple-choice QA tasks (TruthfulQA, MMLU), if chain-of-thought prompting is combined with explicit confidence verbalization, then Expected Calibration Error (ECE) will be at least 0.03 lower than the better single intervention (CoT-only or confidence-only), because reasoning chain generation surfaces uncertainty signals that inform more calibrated confidence judgments.

### Mechanism
1. CoT prompting generates explicit reasoning chains
2. Reasoning chains contain uncertainty markers (hedging, alternatives, qualifications)
3. These markers are in-context when confidence is subsequently verbalized
4. The model's confidence estimate incorporates these uncertainty signals
5. Result: Better-calibrated confidence-accuracy relationship

## Experimental Design

**Models**: GPT-3.5-turbo, Llama-2-70B-chat (2 families)

**Datasets**: TruthfulQA (817 items), MMLU (1000 item sample)

**Conditions**:
1. Baseline (direct answer)
2. CoT-only (reasoning then answer)
3. Confidence-only (answer with confidence)
4. CoT+Confidence (reasoning, answer, confidence)
5. Token-padding control (filler + confidence)

**Metrics**: ECE, ACE, Brier Score, Accuracy, Extraction success rate

**Statistical Approach**: Bootstrap 95% CIs, 3 runs per condition

## Limitations

- Self-reading mechanism is interpretive correlation, not causal proof
- Two model families provide initial but not comprehensive generalization
- TruthfulQA is adversarial; results may differ on non-adversarial benchmarks (MMLU included for balance)
- Applies to multiple-choice QA; open-ended generation not covered
