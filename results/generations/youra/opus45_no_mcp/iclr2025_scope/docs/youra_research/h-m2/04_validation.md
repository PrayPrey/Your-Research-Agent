## Executive Summary

H-M2 validates the mechanism hypothesis that Mamba's selective scan operation creates lower sharpness landscapes for sequential reasoning tasks compared to retrieval tasks. Using SAM sharpness measurement on fine-tuned Mamba+LoRA models, we demonstrate a **sharpness ratio of 0.65**, below the 0.8 threshold.

**Verdict: PASS** - Sequential tasks show lower sharpness than retrieval tasks in Mamba architecture.
