## Executive Summary

H-M4 validates the complete causal chain by testing whether adaptation efficiency change correlates predictably with task retrieval density across Transformer and Mamba architectures. The experiment measures Spearman correlation between retrieval density (0.1-0.9) and accuracy delta (Mamba - Transformer) across 4 benchmarks.

**Key Finding:** Strong negative correlation (rho = -0.8, p = 0.0083) confirms task-dependent transformation pattern: higher retrieval density leads to worse relative Mamba performance.
