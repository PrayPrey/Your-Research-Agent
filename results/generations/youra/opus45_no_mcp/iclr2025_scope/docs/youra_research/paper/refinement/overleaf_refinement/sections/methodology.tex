
\subsubsection{Architecture Conversion Protocol}

Transformer models are converted to Mamba using SSM-attention duality with matched LoRA configurations (rank = 16, alpha = 32). Transformer targets: q\_proj, k\_proj, v\_proj, o\_proj. Mamba targets: in\_proj, out\_proj (structurally analogous projections).

\subsubsection{Loss Landscape Measurement}

\textbf{Sharpness metric}: Maximum loss increase under epsilon-norm perturbation with $\epsilon$ = 0.05 and 100 batch samples.

\textbf{Eigenvalue analysis}: KL divergence between eigenvalue distributions quantifies landscape geometry change.

\subsubsection{LoRA Effective Rank Computation}

Effective rank is defined as the number of singular values capturing 90\% of total energy in learned LoRA matrices.
