**Why This Matters:**

- In 4 key locations (Abstract, Intro, Results, Conclusion), paper uses 1.39%
- Only 04_validation.md uses the correct rounded value (1.39% rounds from 1.386%)

- Margin with actual value: (1.386 - 5.0) / 5.0 × 100 = -72.28%
- Margin with paper value: (1.39 - 5.0) / 5.0 × 100 = -72.2%
- **Impact:** 0.08% difference in margin calculation (negligible)

**Recommendation:**
Use **1.39%** throughout (correct 2-decimal rounding from 1.38565872%).

**Severity:** MINOR (does not affect conclusions, but should align with standard rounding)
