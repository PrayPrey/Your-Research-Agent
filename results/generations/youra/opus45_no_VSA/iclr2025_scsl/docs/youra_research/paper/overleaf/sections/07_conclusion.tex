\section{Conclusion}

We return to our opening question: \emph{When does training go wrong?} Our answer: 3--4 epochs before the symptom appears. Gradient ratio decay temporally precedes sharpness ratio divergence by $\tau = 3.67$ epochs.

We established: (1) SR$_0$ = 0.9999 confirms curvature disparity is training-induced, (2) $\tau > 0$ indicates gradient convergence precedes curvature divergence.

Understanding \emph{when} and \emph{why} the loss landscape becomes asymmetric is the foundation for principled intervention. We provide the first quantitative characterization of this temporal mechanism.
