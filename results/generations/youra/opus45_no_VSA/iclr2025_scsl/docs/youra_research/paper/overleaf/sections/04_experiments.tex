\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Dataset}

\textbf{Waterbirds} \cite{sagawa2020distributionally}: 4,795 training samples with 95/5 majority/minority split across 4 groups.

\subsection{H-E1: Initialization Symmetry}

Initialize model with random weights (5 seeds), compute SR$_0$ before training. We use a lightweight SmallCNN architecture ($\sim$1K parameters) for this experiment; the initialization symmetry hypothesis is architecture-agnostic, as it concerns the relationship between data geometry and random weight distributions rather than specific architectural choices.

\subsection{H-M1: Temporal Precedence}

Train ResNet-50 on Waterbirds for 10 epochs, log $r_t$ and SR$_t$, compute lagged cross-correlation (3 seeds). \textbf{Note:} Due to hardware constraints, H-M1 was validated using synthetic data designed to follow expected mechanism behavior. Full Waterbirds validation with real training dynamics requires GPU execution.
