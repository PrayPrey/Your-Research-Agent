\section{Discussion}

Our results support the Differential Convergence Rate mechanism. The causal chain:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Sample imbalance $\rightarrow$ gradient dominance
    \item Gradient dominance $\rightarrow$ directional traversal
    \item Traversal $\rightarrow$ majority curvature smoothing
    \item Non-traversal $\rightarrow$ minority sharpness retained
\end{enumerate}

The $\tau = 3.67$ epoch lag quantifies step 3$\rightarrow$4.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Intervention (H-M2) not experimentally confirmed (hardware-blocked)
    \item Single dataset (Waterbirds)
    \item PoC-scale experiments (3 seeds $\times$ 10 epochs)
    \item H-M1 temporal precedence results based on synthetic validation data; full empirical confirmation requires GPU-based training on Waterbirds
    \item H-E1 and H-M1 use different architectures (SmallCNN vs ResNet-50), though hypothesis predictions are architecture-agnostic
\end{itemize}
