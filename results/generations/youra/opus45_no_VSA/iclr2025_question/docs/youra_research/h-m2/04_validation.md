## Analysis

### Findings

1. **NTI underperforms on low-entropy samples**: AUROC of 0.5136 is near chance level (0.50)
2. **Confidence interval includes chance**: 95% CI [0.4639, 0.5628] spans 0.50
3. **Subset size adequate**: 1029 samples provides sufficient statistical power
4. **Result is informative**: Shows trajectory metrics do NOT provide additional signal when model is confident

### Interpretation

The hypothesis tested whether NTI provides discriminative signal specifically on "confident but wrong" cases (low H_L). The failure indicates:

- When the model is confident (low entropy), trajectory instability does NOT reliably distinguish correct from incorrect responses
- NTI's discriminative power (AUROC 0.5657 on full dataset from h-e1) may be driven primarily by high-entropy samples
- This suggests trajectory instability correlates with overall uncertainty rather than providing orthogonal signal to entropy

### Implications

Since this is a SHOULD_WORK gate:
- Workflow continues (not blocked)
- Limitation documented for Phase 6 paper writing
- Consider: NTI may be complementary to entropy on high-entropy samples rather than low-entropy samples
