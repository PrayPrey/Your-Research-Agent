\section{Conclusion}

We introduced CLTI for single-pass hallucination detection. NTI achieves AUROC 0.5657; combined with CMI and entropy, $+7.1\%$ improvement ($p = 1.15 \times 10^{-5}$). Trajectory metrics fail on confident predictions and RCI flip is architectural. The method complements entropy on uncertain cases; confident hallucinations remain an open problem.
