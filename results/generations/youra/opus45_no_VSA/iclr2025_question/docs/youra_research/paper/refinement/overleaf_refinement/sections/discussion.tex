
\subsubsection{Validated Claims}

NTI combined with CMI improves detection by +7.1\% AUROC over an entropy-only baseline (Fisher combined p = 1.15e-05). Trajectory instability correlates with hallucination on high-entropy samples where the model exhibits uncertainty.

\subsubsection{Refuted Claims}

\textbf{h-m2}: The signal collapses on confident predictions. NTI is coupled to entropy by construction; when final-layer entropy is low, trajectory variance is also constrained.

\textbf{h-m3}: RCI flip is architectural, not epistemic. Top-token changes between layers occur in >90\% of all responses regardless of correctness. This pattern reflects normal transformer processing rather than uncertainty-specific dynamics.

\subsubsection{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Entropy coupling}: The method cannot detect confident hallucinations---cases where the model is wrong but not uncertain.
\item \textbf{Single model}: Results are from LLaMA-2-7B only; generalization to other architectures and scales requires replication.
\item \textbf{Multiple-choice format}: TruthfulQA MC1 uses forced-choice evaluation; behavior on free-form generation may differ.
\item \textbf{Correlational evidence}: No causal intervention was performed; the observed patterns may not reflect the mechanism of hallucination.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Future Work}

\begin{itemize}
\item Develop entropy-orthogonal features using hidden state geometry
\item Validate on additional models (Mistral, LLaMA-3)
\item Investigate semantic RCI measuring distance between competing tokens rather than binary flips
\item Apply causal interventions through activation patching
\end{itemize}
