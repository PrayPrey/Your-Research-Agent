## Analysis

### Key Finding: Near-Universal Flip Pattern

The RCI flip pattern (top-1 token change between consecutive layers) occurs in nearly ALL samples regardless of hallucination status:
- 95.1% of hallucinated responses show flip
- 90.9% of correct responses show flip

This indicates the flip pattern is **not discriminative** for hallucination detection. The phenomenon appears to be a general characteristic of the model's layer-wise processing, not specific to hallucinations.

### Labeling Distribution

- Hallucinations: 795 (97.3%)
- Correct: 22 (2.7%)

The extremely high hallucination rate suggests either:
1. LLaMA-2-7B performs poorly on TruthfulQA MC1 with greedy decoding
2. The substring-match hallucination labeling is overly strict

### Mechanism Verification

- Hidden states extraction: PASS (33 layers)
- Layer logits shape: PASS [9, B, V]
- Flip detection: PASS (returns valid counts)

The mechanism implementation is correct; the hypothesis itself is falsified.
