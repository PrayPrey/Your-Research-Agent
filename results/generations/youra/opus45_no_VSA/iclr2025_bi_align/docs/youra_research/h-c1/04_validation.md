## Interpretation

The FAIL result indicates:

1. **High-BAI responses do NOT form semantically coherent agency clusters.** They cluster based on generic language patterns (common words, greeting phrases) rather than agency-preserving behaviors.

2. **Proxy signals may be noise.** The BAI proxy detectors (clarifying questions, hedging, deferral, option enumeration) may be picking up surface patterns (length, politeness markers) rather than genuine agency preservation.

3. **Alternative hypothesis:** The disagreement between BAI and reward scores may stem from:
   - **Length bias**: Longer responses score higher on both BAI proxies and reward model
   - **Politeness markers**: Generic conversational tokens ("welcome", "thanks") correlate with BAI but not with task helpfulness
   - **Dataset artifacts**: HH-RLHF may have systematic patterns unrelated to agency
