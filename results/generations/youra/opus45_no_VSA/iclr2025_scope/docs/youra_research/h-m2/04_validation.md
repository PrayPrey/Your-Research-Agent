# Validation Report: H-M2

**Hypothesis:** Routing is robust to paraphrase (cosine ≥0.90) and keyword masking (<10% absolute drop)
**Type:** MECHANISM
**Gate:** SHOULD_WORK
**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Result:** FAIL

---

## Executive Summary

The H-M2 robustness experiment evaluated whether the linear probe routing system (validated in H-E1) maintains stability under input perturbations. The hypothesis tested two conditions:
1. Paraphrase cosine similarity ≥ 0.90
2. Keyword masking accuracy drop < 10%

**Both conditions failed:**
- Cosine Mean: **0.7818** (threshold: 0.90, FAIL)
- Max Accuracy Drop: **44.4%** (threshold: 10%, FAIL)
- Routing Consistency: **76.1%** (threshold: 85%, FAIL)

---

## Methodology

### Data
- **Test Set:** 450 samples from FLAN dataset (same split as H-E1, seed=42)
- **Task Families:** 18 classes

### Perturbation Methods
1. **WordNet Paraphrases:** Synonym substitution (30% words swapped, 5 variants/sample)
2. **Embedding Paraphrases:** WordNet with cosine ≥0.80 filter (3 variants/sample)
3. **Keyword Masking (20%):** TF-IDF weighted word removal
4. **Keyword Masking (50%):** Aggressive keyword removal
5. **Random Masking (50%):** Control baseline

## Results

### Paraphrase Robustness

| Variant | Cosine Mean | Routing Consistency | Total Paraphrases |
|---------|-------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| WordNet | 0.720 | 72.5% | 2,250 |
| Embedding | 0.843 | 79.6% | 892 |
| **Combined** | **0.782** | **76.1%** | 3,142 |

**Finding:** WordNet paraphrases (synonym substitutions like "capital" → "Washington", "provide" → "allow") cause substantial embedding drift (cosine ~0.72), breaking the 0.90 threshold.

### Masking Robustness

| Mode | Ratio | Original Acc | Masked Acc | Accuracy Drop |
|------|-------|--------------|------------|---------------|
| Keyword | 20% | 74.9% | 48.9% | **26.0%** |
| Keyword | 50% | 74.9% | 30.4% | **44.4%** |
| Random | 50% | 74.9% | 22.7% | **52.2%** |

**Finding:** Even 20% keyword masking causes 26% accuracy drop—far exceeding the 10% threshold. The routing heavily depends on task-indicative keywords.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis is **falsified**. The linear probe routing is NOT robust to:
1. **Paraphrase variations:** Synonym substitutions cause embedding drift below the 0.90 cosine threshold
2. **Keyword removal:** The routing heavily relies on task-indicative keywords; removing them breaks classification

This suggests the MiniLM embeddings capture surface-level lexical features rather than deep semantic task structure. The routing mechanism is brittle to wording changes.

---

## Implications for Main Hypothesis

The H-M2 failure indicates a significant limitation:
- **Risk:** Real-world instruction variations will cause routing errors
- **Mitigation options:**
  1. Use a more robust encoder (e.g., larger models, contrastive fine-tuning)
  2. Add paraphrase augmentation during probe training
  3. Combine embedding routing with keyword-based fallback
