
Four research questions guide the experiments:

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Does a dominant latent factor persist after controlling for model scale and release date?

\textbf{RQ2 (Mechanism):} Does the latent factor correlate with behavioral stability?

\textbf{RQ3 (Intervention):} Does instruction-tuning increase both behavioral stability and factor scores?

\textbf{RQ4 (Generalizability):} Do frozen factor weights generalize to holdout benchmarks?
