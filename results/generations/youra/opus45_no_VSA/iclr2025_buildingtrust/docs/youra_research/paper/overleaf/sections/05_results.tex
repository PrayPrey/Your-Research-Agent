\section{Results}

\subsection{RQ1: Existence of Residual Factor}

PCA on the residualized matrix yields a dominant first eigenvalue:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{PCA eigenvalues for residualized benchmark scores.}
\label{tab:pca}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
Component & Eigenvalue & Variance Explained \\
\midrule
PC1 & \textbf{2.277} & \textbf{60.0\%} \\
PC2 & 0.423 & 11.1\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The 95th percentile of the permutation null is $\lambda_1 = 0.803$. Our observed $\lambda_1 = 2.277$ exceeds this by 184\% ($p = 0.001$).

All six benchmarks load positively and uniformly on PC1 (range: 0.35--0.44), supporting a general factor interpretation.
