\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Research Questions}

\begin{description}
  \item[RQ1:] Do trustworthiness dimensions exhibit significant pairwise partial correlations after controlling for scale and RLHF?
  \item[RQ2:] Is RLHF the mechanism behind joint safety-ethics co-optimization?
  \item[RQ3:] What is the cluster structure, and does it match the predicted RLHF-sensitive vs.\ RLHF-insensitive grouping?
  \item[RQ4:] What is the minimum sufficient evaluation set and its bootstrap stability?
\end{description}

\subsection{Dataset and Models}

TrustLLM published Table 1 scores (16 models $\times$ 6 dimensions). Models annotated with $\log_{10}(\text{parameter\_count})$ and binary is\_RLHF.

\subsection{Baselines}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Raw Spearman correlation} --- without confound control; expected positive bias
  \item \textbf{Scale-only control} --- partial correlation removing only $\log_{10}$\_params (not RLHF)
  \item \textbf{Epoch AI capability baseline} \cite{epochai2024} --- median $\rho = 0.73$ for general capability benchmarks (raw Spearman; reported here as a field reference point, not a methodologically equivalent comparison --- see Section~\ref{sec:related} for discussion of this distinction)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implementation}

Python 3.10; scipy 1.11, networkx 3.1, numpy 1.25, sklearn 1.3. Bootstrap: \texttt{np.random.default\_rng(seed=42)}, 1000 resamples of 14/16 models.
