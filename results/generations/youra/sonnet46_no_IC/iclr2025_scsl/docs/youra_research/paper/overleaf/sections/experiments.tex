\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1:} Does GroupDRO training create a group-reweighted gradient signal that differentially affects backbone weights compared to ERM? (H-M1/H-M2)

\textbf{RQ2:} Does DFR backbone encoding differ from ERM backbone encoding at matching seeds? (H-P0)

\textbf{RQ3:} Does GroupDRO layer4 background probe accuracy significantly lower than ERM (one-sided paired t-test, $p < 0.05$, $n = 3$ seeds)? (H-M3, primary)

\textbf{RQ4:} Does background probe accuracy correlate negatively with WGA across methods? (H-P2, exploratory)
