
\subsubsection{4.1 Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1:} Does GroupDRO training create a group-reweighted gradient signal that differentially modifies backbone weights compared to ERM? (H-M1/H-M2)

\textbf{RQ2:} Does DFR backbone encoding differ from ERM backbone encoding at matching seeds? (H-P0)

\textbf{RQ3:} Is GroupDRO layer4 background probe accuracy significantly lower than ERM (one-sided paired t-test, n = 3 seeds)? (H-M3, primary)

\textbf{RQ4:} Does background probe accuracy correlate negatively with WGA across methods? (H-P2, exploratory)
