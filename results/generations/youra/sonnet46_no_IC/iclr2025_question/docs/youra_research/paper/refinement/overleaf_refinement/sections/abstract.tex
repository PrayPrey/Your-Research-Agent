
Uncertainty statistics read from a language model's output distribution are inexpensive hallucination-detection signals, but they are architecture-fragile: the same statistic can separate hallucinations on one checkpoint and fail, with inverted score direction, on another. Existing methods that exploit the stronger intermediate-layer signal reintroduce the components that make detection expensive --- trained probes, repeated sampling, or geometric layer-selection machinery. This paper evaluates per-layer logit-lens uncertainty statistics --- Shannon entropy, max-token probability, and adjacent-layer KL divergence --- as training-free hallucination-detection scores read before final-layer calibration, with the readout layer, signal, and score direction selected per model on a held-out selection split. Across three 7--8B model families (LLaMA-2-7B, Mistral-7B-v0.1, LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct) on TriviaQA and TruthfulQA, at least one screened intermediate layer is class-separable in all six model $\times$ dataset cells (selection-split corrected AUROC 0.6092--0.7011 against a pre-registered 0.55 gate), and the best screened intermediate layer exceeds the same sweep's final-layer entropy readout in every cell as a point estimate (margins +0.0035 to +0.130; the thinnest margin is within plausible noise). Adjacent-layer KL divergence, to our knowledge previously unevaluated as a detection score, is the best signal in two of the six cells. Separately, a provenance audit showed that six protocol differences moved a reference final-layer baseline on the same model and dataset from AUROC 0.5186 to 0.5928, motivating the protocol-internal validity anchor adopted here. The method is training-free and sampling-free --- one greedy generation plus one teacher-forced re-forward per example. All reported values are selection-split point estimates from a single seed with no confidence intervals; the locked test splits were never evaluated, and the test-split confirmation, cross-dataset transfer, and signal-fusion tiers of the staged design remain unmeasured.
