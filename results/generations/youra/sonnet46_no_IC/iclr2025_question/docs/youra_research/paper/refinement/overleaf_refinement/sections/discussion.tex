
\textbf{What the results establish.} The existence premise of depth-resolved, training-free hallucination detection is confirmed universally within scope: in every model $\times$ dataset cell, at least one screened intermediate layer separates correct from hallucinated answers using only the model's own decoded distributions, and always better than the final layer's entropy readout in the same sweep. The calibration-suppression mechanism fares more modestly. The direction is right in 6/6 cells, and depth helps most where the final layer is weakest --- both consistent with suppression --- but the pre-registered CI test never ran, and the severity-ordering sub-claim was descriptively contradicted (the instruct model gained most, not least). ''Consistent with'' is where the mechanism claim ends. A complication worth noting: under the current protocol, LLaMA-2's final layer is not at chance (0.5928/0.6669), so any suppression is relative attenuation rather than destruction of signal. The third result was unplanned: the anchor forensics elevate measurement validity to a first-class finding. Six ordinary protocol choices moved a baseline AUROC by +0.074, which means weak-signal AUROC magnitudes are properties of protocols, not only of models --- and the protocol-internal anchor that replaced the broken one is a reusable design for iterated LLM evaluation.

\textbf{Limitations.} Four, stated plainly. \textit{First, all AUROCs are selection-split values; the locked test splits were never evaluated.} The numbers that selected the tuples also grade them, and they overestimate generalization by construction. The split stays sealed at publication for a scientific reason: because no analysis decision --- including any made in writing this paper --- has read it, the deferred evaluation retains the guarantees of a pre-registered confirmation. This paper is therefore, explicitly, the stage-one report of that staged design, and every artifact for stage two exists: freezing the six published tuples and evaluating the locked test splits with a paired bootstrap is a zero-GPU computation on the released caches. \textit{Second, one seed and no confidence intervals}; the +0.0035 LLaMA-3/TruthfulQA margin is within plausible noise. The binding gate quantity clears by +0.059 to +0.151 as point estimates --- no distributional claim is made for these margins either --- and the claim degrades gracefully (5/6 margins exceed 0.03); the bootstrap machinery is implemented and runs on cached data. \textit{Third, the original ''rescue from a documented 0.5186 failure'' framing did not survive its own provenance audit} --- that number is protocol-specific, and under the current protocol LLaMA-2's final layer sits near 0.59, weak but not at chance. The broken framing was converted into the measurement-validity contribution, and the depth advantage stands entirely on within-protocol comparisons; a factorial attribution of the six protocol factors is the natural follow-up. \textit{Fourth, scope}: three 7--8B families, two short-form QA datasets, greedy decoding, one instruct model, and an unaddressed familiarity-vs-truthfulness confound --- the scores demonstrably separate correct from incorrect answers under standard labels, but whether they track truthfulness or question familiarity is untested. This is deliberate proof-of-concept scoping, chosen for controlled lineage to the motivating failure record; base-vs-instruct pairs and frequency-stratified AUROC diagnostics are the corresponding mitigations. Cross-dataset transfer and KL--entropy fusion, like the test-split evaluation, were never measured (Section 5.6) and are staged as the next tier --- all zero-GPU from the released caches.

\textbf{Broader impact.} Inexpensive, training-free hallucination detection lowers the barrier to monitoring wherever open-weight models run with hidden-state access, at roughly one extra forward pass per monitored generation --- well below the roughly tenfold added cost of sampling-based detection. The corresponding risk is overtrust: these scores are uncertainty correlates under standard correctness labels, not truth oracles, and a deployment that treats a favorable AUROC --- especially a selection-split one --- as a guarantee would import exactly the miscalibration this line of work is meant to expose. Detection output should be treated as a triage signal, not a verdict.
