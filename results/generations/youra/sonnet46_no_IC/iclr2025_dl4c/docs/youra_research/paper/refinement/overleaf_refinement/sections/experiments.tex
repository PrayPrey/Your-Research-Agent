\subsubsection{4.3 H-E1: SFT Comparison Setup (Design Only)}

\textbf{Conditions:} (a) Unfiltered random subsample; (b) compile()-filtered subsample; both at
500M tokens from the target Python corpus.

\textbf{Model:} Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B. \textbf{Evaluation:} HumanEval pass@1 and MBPP pass@1 (greedy
decoding). \textbf{Baseline:} Unfiltered equal-token-budget (primary), Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B base
without SFT.

H-E1 is a complete experimental design provided for reproducibility. It has not been executed
at the time of writing.
