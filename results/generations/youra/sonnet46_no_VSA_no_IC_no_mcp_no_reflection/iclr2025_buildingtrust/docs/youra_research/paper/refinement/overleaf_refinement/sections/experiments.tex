
We structure our evaluation around two sequential research questions:

\textbf{RQ1 (h-e1 --- Fingerprint Existence):} Is the 4D trustworthiness benchmark profile
of DPO-aligned 7B models systematically separable from SFT-aligned models, at
statistically significant leave-one-out classification accuracy ($\geq 67$\%, permutation $p\leq 0.05)$?

\textbf{RQ2 (h-m1 --- Discriminative Dimensions):} Which benchmark dimension drives the
alignment fingerprint? Does DPO show a systematic fairness advantage on BBQ and WinoGender
($\geq 4/6$ pairs, one-sided binomial $p\leq 0.125)$?

These questions directly map to contributions C1 and C2.

\textbf{Evaluation protocol:} All models evaluated with \texttt{--batch\textbackslash \_size 8}, \texttt{--dtype bfloat16},
\texttt{--limit 100} samples per task on $5\times$ NVIDIA H100 NVL GPUs.

\textbf{Classification protocol (RQ1):} k-NN (k=1, Euclidean) LOO-CV over n=12 models;
permutation test (1000 shuffles, seed=42); significance threshold $p\leq 0.05$ one-tailed.

\textbf{Mechanism protocol (RQ2):} Fisher criterion per benchmark; paired sign test per
fairness benchmark; primary test k\_BBQ $\geq$ 4/6, p\_BBQ $\leq$ 0.125.
