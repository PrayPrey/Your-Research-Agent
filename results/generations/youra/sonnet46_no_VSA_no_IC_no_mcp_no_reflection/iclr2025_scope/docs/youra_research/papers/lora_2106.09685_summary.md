# Paper Summary: LoRA — Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models
**arXiv:** 2106.09685 | **Authors:** Edward Hu et al. (Microsoft Research) | **Year:** 2022

---

## Abstract
LoRA freezes pretrained weights and injects trainable rank-decomposition matrices into each layer. For a weight W ∈ ℝ^{d×k}, LoRA adds BA where B ∈ ℝ^{d×r}, A ∈ ℝ^{r×k}, r << min(d,k). Achieves comparable performance to full fine-tuning at 0.1-1% trainable parameters on GPT-3 and RoBERTa.

## Methodology
- **Update:** W' = W + (α/r)·BA; B initialized to zero, A to Gaussian; α is scaling hyperparameter
- **Target layers:** Original paper targets Q, V attention projection matrices; experiments show K, out_proj also help
- **Rank selection:** r=4-16 sufficient for most tasks; higher rank = more capacity but diminishing returns
- **No inference overhead:** BA merged into W at test time; zero latency cost
- **Architecture-agnostic:** Any nn.Linear layer is a valid LoRA target — validated on attention layers but generalizes

## Experiments & Results
- GPT-3 175B with r=4 on Q,V: matches full fine-tuning on WikiSQL and MNLI with 0.01% parameters
- RoBERTa-large: GLUE average within 0.5% of full fine-tuning at r=8
- Ablation: Q+V target > Q-only > V-only; all layers > Q,V only (marginal gain, not worth cost)
- Rank sensitivity: r=1 often sufficient; r>16 rarely helps

## Relevance to State-Aware PEFT
- **Baseline for comparison:** LoRA on Mamba in_proj+out_proj+x_proj = "naive LoRA" (projection-only)
- **Gap:** LoRA cannot target A_log/B/C directly since they are nn.Parameter (not nn.Linear) — this IS the state-aware PEFT gap
- **Rank guidance:** r=4-8 expected sufficient for Mamba projection layers given similar d_model sizes
- **The null hypothesis:** Naive LoRA (projection-only) on Mamba achieves task accuracy indistinguishable from state-aware PEFT — this is what we test against
- **dt_proj edge case:** dt_proj is nn.Linear and controls Δ (timescale) — including it may be a partial state-aware proxy since Δ modulates how much A contributes per step
