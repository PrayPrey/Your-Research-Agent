\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Our work sits at the intersection of three bodies of literature: parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods, Mamba and state-space sequence models, and the growing body of negative results in machine learning.

\subsection{LoRA and Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning}

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) \citep{hu2022lora} introduced the standard approach of decomposing weight updates into low-rank factors: for a frozen weight matrix $W$, the update is parameterized as $\Delta W = BA$ where $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r}$ and $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times k}$ with rank $r \ll \min(d, k)$. The method was validated on GPT-2, GPT-3, and RoBERTa across a range of NLP tasks. The original work explicitly targets attention matrices ($W_q$, $W_k$, $W_v$, $W_o$) --- all \texttt{nn.Linear} layers in transformer architectures where gradient flow from task loss to LoRA weights is unconditionally guaranteed.

Subsequent PEFT methods extended LoRA's ideas. AdaLoRA \citep{zhang2023adalora} introduces adaptive rank allocation across layers. DoRA \citep{liu2024dora} decomposes weight updates into magnitude and direction components. IA$^3$ \citep{liu2022ia3} applies multiplicative rescaling rather than additive low-rank updates. Each of these methods was designed for and validated on transformer architectures, where the critical assumption --- that gradient flows cleanly to any targeted \texttt{nn.Linear} layer --- is satisfied by construction.

The assumption is implicit, not stated. Because transformers always satisfy it, the PEFT literature has not needed to treat gradient path compatibility as a design criterion. This is the gap our work fills.

\subsection{Mamba and State-Space Sequence Models}

Structured state space models have evolved from S4 \citep{gu2022s4}, which demonstrated that SSMs could match recurrent and convolutional architectures on long-range dependency tasks. Mamba \citep{gu2023mamba} introduced \emph{selectivity}: rather than fixed state transition matrices, Mamba's selective SSM (S6) computes $A$, $B$, and $C$ as functions of the input. This selectivity is implemented via a custom CUDA parallel associative scan kernel. The Mamba paper characterizes this mechanism for its modeling capabilities; the gradient path properties of this kernel for classification tasks are not addressed.

Mamba-2 \citep{dao2024mamba2} reformulates the state space computation via the Structured State Space Duality (SSD) framework, unifying SSMs and attention under a common theoretical lens. The broader SSM family includes RWKV \citep{peng2023rwkv} and RetNet \citep{sun2023retnet}. None of these architectures have been systematically characterized for PEFT gradient compatibility.

\subsection{Mamba PEFT: The Prior Claim We Contrast Against}

The primary public reference for LoRA on Mamba is alxndrTL/mamba-peft \citep{alxndrtl2024mambapeft}, a community repository demonstrating SST-2 accuracy of approximately 90--92\% using LoRA rank 8 applied to \texttt{in\_proj}, \texttt{out\_proj}, and \texttt{x\_proj} on Mamba-130m. This is the claim that motivated our systematic investigation.

We do not claim that the mamba-peft result is wrong. We note that it is insufficiently documented to reproduce: the checkpoint used, the classification head design, the prompt format, and the complete learning rate schedule are not fully specified. The 40 percentage-point gap between our result and theirs is an open reproducibility problem. Our result may reflect the failure mode one encounters with the base pretrained checkpoint and a standard training recipe.

\subsection{Prefix Tuning and Bypass PEFT Strategies}

\citet{li2021prefix} introduced prefix tuning: rather than modifying model weights, the method prepends learned continuous token embeddings to the input. The key property for our discussion is that prefix tuning does not require gradients to flow through the model's internal computations. For Mamba, prefix tuning would steer the SSM's hidden state trajectory through learned input perturbations without requiring classification gradients to propagate backward through the SSM scan kernel --- making it a theoretically motivated bypass strategy.

\subsection{Negative Results in Machine Learning}

The machine learning community has increasingly recognized the value of documented negative results \citep{sinha2021reproducibility}. The principled documentation of a failure mode --- with a mechanistic interpretation, a diagnostic signature, and a forward direction --- is a scientific contribution distinct from performance improvements.

To our knowledge, no prior work provides a controlled, reproducible characterization of where and how projection-only LoRA fails on pure Mamba SSMs for classification tasks.
