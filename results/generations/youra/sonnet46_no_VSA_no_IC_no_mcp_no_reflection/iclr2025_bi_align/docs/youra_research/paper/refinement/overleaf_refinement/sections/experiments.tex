\subsubsection{Experiment Rounds}

Two rounds were executed. h-e1 used a word-count approximation (words $\times$ 1.3) for prompt token estimation and identified 6,769 returning users. h-e1-v2 used tiktoken cl100k\_base tokenization and identified 27,902 returning users. The difference in cohort sizes reflects the tokenization method: tiktoken counts subword tokens rather than whitespace-delimited words, affecting which users satisfy the $\geq 3$ monthly bin criterion. h-e1-v2 is the authoritative analysis.
