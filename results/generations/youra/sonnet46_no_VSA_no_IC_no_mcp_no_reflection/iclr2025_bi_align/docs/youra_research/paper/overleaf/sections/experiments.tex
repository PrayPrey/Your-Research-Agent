\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

We design experiments to answer three research questions:

\textbf{RQ1:} Does prompt complexity in returning WildChat-1M user cohorts show a statistically significant monotonic trend over 2023--2024?

\textbf{RQ2:} Does preference vote entropy in LMSYS Chatbot Arena show a statistically significant trend over 2023--2024?

\textbf{RQ3:} Does correction/negation frequency in returning WildChat-1M user cohorts show a statistically significant monotonic trend over 2023--2024?
