Our experimental setup is organized around four research questions that together enable clean attribution of the observed AUROC failure.

\textbf{Q1}: \emph{Do SMC methods achieve above-chance AUROC on Llama-3-8B-Instruct on HaluEval QA?} This is the primary empirical question. We evaluate both SMC-NLI and SMC-Embed against a random baseline (AUROC $= 0.5$) and report 95\% confidence intervals computed via bootstrap resampling ($n = 1000$ resamples).

\textbf{Q2}: \emph{Is the failure attributable to implementation error?} We address this through the 14-unit-test verification suite and mechanism verification step described in Section~3. If the NLI classifier correctly scores synthetic pairs and the aggregation formula is correct, then implementation error is ruled out.

\textbf{Q3}: \emph{Is the failure distributional?} We compare the mean and distribution of SMC scores for correctly-labeled vs.\ hallucinated-labeled questions. If the distributions overlap substantially, this confirms that the model produces similarly consistent outputs for both labels --- the hallmark of systematic confabulation.

\textbf{Q4}: \emph{Are SMC-NLI and SMC-Embed consistent with each other?} If both metrics fail in the same direction and their per-question scores are correlated, this suggests a shared underlying cause (model behavior) rather than metric-specific failure.

\subsection{Dataset Construction}

We use the HaluEval QA split \cite{Li2023HaluEval}, sampling 500 correct and 500 hallucinated question--answer pairs uniformly at random with seed 42. Each question is presented to Llama-3-8B-Instruct using the standard \texttt{<|start\_header\_id|>user<|end\_header\_id|>} chat template. The model's answer to each question is elicited $N = 10$ times with independent stochastic sampling.

\subsection{Baselines}

We compare against a random classifier baseline (AUROC $= 0.5$ by definition) and report whether our observed AUROCs are statistically distinguishable from chance using a one-sided bootstrap test ($H_0$: AUROC $\leq 0.5$; $H_1$: AUROC $> 0.5$). We do not compare against supervised baselines, as our focus is on the training-free regime.

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

Our primary metric is AUROC, which is appropriate for binary classification with balanced classes and is threshold-free. We additionally report: (1) the mean SMC score by label, (2) the Pearson correlation between SMC-NLI and SMC-Embed scores across questions, and (3) the ROC curve to visualize operating characteristics across all thresholds.

\subsection{Computational Resources}

Generating 10 samples for each of 1,000 questions (10,000 forward passes) on Llama-3-8B-Instruct requires approximately 4 hours on a single A100 80GB GPU with batch size 1. NLI scoring over 45 pairs per question (45,000 NLI forward passes) requires approximately 2 additional hours. Embedding scoring is negligible by comparison (\textless 10 minutes).
