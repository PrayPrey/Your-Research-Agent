\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We began by observing that domain mixing ratios can make or break LLM performance---yet optimizing them typically costs hundreds of GPU-hours in proxy model training. Our work takes the first step toward a training-free alternative.

Our main contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Pipeline Validation:} EDMP scores can be reliably computed for multi-domain corpora with perfect reproducibility.

    \item \textbf{Statistical Significance:} Domain scores are statistically distinguishable (ANOVA F=1242.59, p$<$0.001).

    \item \textbf{Data Quality Discovery:} Synthetic data produces insufficient variance; real domain data is necessary.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Future Directions:} Testing with real Pile domains, completing the causal chain (h-m1, h-m2), and comparing embedders.

The promise of training-free domain mixing optimization remains viable. Our work validates the computational pipeline and identifies precisely what is needed to complete the validation: real domain data with sufficient vocabulary diversity.
