\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

We design experiments to test whether hallucination detection methods generalize across benchmarks under matched computational budgets.

\textbf{Research Questions:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1:} Do semantic entropy and self-consistency detect hallucinations above random on each benchmark?
    \item \textbf{RQ2:} Does method ranking differ across benchmarks (benchmark sensitivity)?
    \item \textbf{RQ3:} Are observed differences statistically significant given our sample size?
\end{itemize}
