\subsection{Key Findings}

\textbf{Finding 1: Benchmark sensitivity exists.} Semantic entropy shows marginal detection on HaluEval but inverted results on TruthfulQA. Method performance depends on benchmark-specific factors.

\textbf{Finding 2: Self-consistency requires investigation.} BERTScore-based consistency performed near random, suggesting surface similarity may not capture hallucination-relevant uncertainty.

\textbf{Finding 3: Controlled comparison is essential.} Published results using different benchmarks are fundamentally incomparable.

\subsection{Limitations}

\textbf{L1: Insufficient sample size.} $N=20$ per dataset yields wide confidence intervals, preventing statistically powered conclusions. Full-scale evaluation required.

\textbf{L2: TruthfulQA labeling mismatch hypothesis.} The inverted results may stem from labeling methodology differences, requiring investigation.

\textbf{L3: Single model family.} Results may not generalize beyond Llama-3-8B.

\textbf{L4: Single seed.} Variance across random seeds not captured.

\subsection{Broader Impact}

Our work promotes rigorous evaluation practices. We emphasize these are pilot findings requiring validation, not definitive method assessments.
