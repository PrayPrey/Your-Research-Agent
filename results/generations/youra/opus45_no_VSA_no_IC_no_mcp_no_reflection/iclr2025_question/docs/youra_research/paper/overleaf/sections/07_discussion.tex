\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Format-Dependency as the Primary Result}

The 0.24 AUROC gap between max probability and semantic entropy stems from format mismatch. Semantic entropy assumes responses contain meaningful semantic content for NLI comparison. MC answers violate this assumption.

\textbf{Practical implication:} Method selection should match task format. For MC-format tasks, use simple confidence-based methods.

\subsection{Limitations}

\textbf{Sample Size:} 50 questions limits statistical power. Full validation requires 817 samples.

\textbf{Single Model:} Results may not generalize across architectures.

\textbf{MC Format Only:} Free-form generation may yield different rankings.

\subsection{Broader Impact}

Our work helps practitioners select appropriate UQ methods for their deployment context. Documentation of format-dependency contributes to more rigorous UQ method evaluation standards.
