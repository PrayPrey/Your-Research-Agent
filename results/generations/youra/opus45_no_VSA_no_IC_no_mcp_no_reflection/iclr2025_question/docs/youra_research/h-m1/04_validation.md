## Analysis

### Why Semantic Entropy Underperformed

1. **MC format mismatch:** Semantic entropy designed for free-form generation; MC answers are short letters with little semantic content for NLI clustering
2. **Entailment model granularity:** BART-mnli may not discriminate well between "A" vs "B" letter answers
3. **Sample diversity limited:** 5 samples may not capture semantic variance sufficient for entropy discrimination

### Mechanism Working Despite Low AUROC

The mechanism verification passes:
- Clustering reduces 5 samples to avg 4.92 clusters (some merging occurs)
- Entropy varies across questions (std > 0)
- This indicates semantic clustering is functioning, but the resulting entropy scores don't correlate well with hallucination labels on MC tasks
