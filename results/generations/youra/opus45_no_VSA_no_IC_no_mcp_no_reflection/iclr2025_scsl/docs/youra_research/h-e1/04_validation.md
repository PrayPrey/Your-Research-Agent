## Analysis

### Root Cause

Implementation accumulated only ONE gradient vector per epoch (last batch gradient). With 10 epochs, SVD operates on 10×num_params matrix yielding at most 10 principal components. Alignment measurement (projection energy) on 10-dimensional subspace in 25M-dimensional parameter space produces near-zero values regardless of direction.

### Expected vs Actual

- Expected: Subspace captures sufficient gradient variance to show differential alignment
- Actual: Subspace too low-rank to capture meaningful structure

### Fix Required

1. Accumulate gradients across ALL batches during epochs 1-10 (not just last batch)
2. Use streaming SVD or sample representative batches
3. Alternatively: accumulate epoch-averaged gradients with higher rank
