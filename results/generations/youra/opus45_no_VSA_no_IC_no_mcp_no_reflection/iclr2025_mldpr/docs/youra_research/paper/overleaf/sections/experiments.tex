\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

We design experiments to answer the following questions:

\textbf{RQ1:} Is the Kendall-$\tau$ correlation between ImageNet and ImageNet-V2 rankings below 0.90 (our threshold for ``significant ranking shift'')?

\textbf{RQ2:} What is the statistical confidence in our correlation estimate?

\textbf{RQ3:} What is the distribution of per-model rank changes, and what explains ranking preservation?

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Primary Metric:} Kendall-$\tau$ (tau-b) with pre-registered threshold $\tau < 0.90$
    \item \textbf{Secondary Metrics:} Spearman-$\rho$, mean accuracy drop, maximum rank change, Top-10 overlap
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Statistical Inference}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Bootstrap confidence intervals: 10,000 iterations, percentile method
    \item Significance level: $\alpha = 0.05$
\end{itemize}
