\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

We design experiments to answer two fundamental questions:

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Do duality equations produce valid, non-divergent SSM parameters?

\textbf{RQ2 (Mechanism):} Does duality-based initialization provide lower reconstruction error than random?

\subsection{Dataset}

WikiText-103 \cite{merity2017wikitext}: 100 samples (h-e1), 500 samples (h-m1), 64--512 tokens per sample.

\subsection{Models}

\textbf{Teacher:} BERT-base-uncased (12 layers, 768 hidden, 110M parameters) \cite{devlin2019bert}

\textbf{Student:} Mamba-12 with d\_state = 64
