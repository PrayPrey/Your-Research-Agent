
Experiments were designed to test the friction-completeness hypothesis through a hierarchical verification structure.

\subsubsection{4.1 Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 (Cross-Platform Optional Fields):} Do platforms with higher friction scores show higher optional metadata presence rates?

\textbf{RQ2 (Enforcement Mechanism Validation):} Do required fields show high presence regardless of friction score, validating enforcement as distinct mechanism?

\subsubsection{4.2 Sub-Hypothesis Structure}

Verification follows a 3-hypothesis structure:

\textbf{H-E1 (Existence):} Platform friction-reduction features are objectively measurable from public documentation, and 10,000+ metadata records are extractable via APIs/scraping.

\textbf{H-M2 (Mechanism):} Lower friction increases voluntary completion rates for optional fields (tested via cross-platform presence comparison).

\textbf{H-M3 (Mechanism):} Enforcement and friction-reduction operate as magnitude-differentiated mechanisms --- optional fields vary by friction, required fields remain high (~75-95\%) with weaker friction effects.

\subsubsection{4.4 Evaluation Metrics}

\textbf{Primary Metric:} Presence rate (0-100\%) = (datasets with field present) / (total datasets) $\times$ 100

\textbf{Statistical Tests:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cross-platform comparison: Chi-squared test (categorical: present/absent $\times$ platform), Cramér's V effect size
\item Significance threshold: $\alpha =0.05$ (two-sided tests)
\item Effect size thresholds: Small (Cohen's h=0.2), Medium (h=0.5), Large (h=0.8)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Secondary Metrics:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Gradient validation: Monotonic ordering (UCI < OpenML < HuggingFace) for optional fields
\item Coefficient of Variation (CV): Measure presence rate stability for required vs optional fields
\item Semantic accuracy: Manual validation of 100-sample subset (target >80\%)
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{4.5 Success Criteria}

\textbf{H-E1 Success:} Friction scores assigned (HF=3, OpenML=2, UCI=0) with objective binary criteria AND parsing accuracy >90\%.

\textbf{H-M2 Success:} HuggingFace shows higher presence for optional fields than UCI AND difference $\geq 30pp$ AND p<0.05.

\textbf{H-M3 Success:} All 3 platforms show high presence for license AND version fields AND weaker friction effects than optional fields.
