
\subsubsection{Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 (H-E1):} Do inter-benchmark correlations between TruthfulQA, HaluEval, and FactScore fall in the moderate range?

\textbf{RQ2 (H-M1):} Is TruthfulQA distinct from general knowledge as measured by MMLU?

\textbf{RQ3 (H-M2):} Is HaluEval's generation coherence dimension distinct from TruthfulQA's misconception resistance?

\textbf{RQ4 (H-M3):} Is FactScore's factual precision distinct from both TruthfulQA and HaluEval?

\subsubsection{Baselines and Reference Points}

\textbf{Unrelated-benchmark baseline:} r(MMLU-Physics, HaluEval) = 0.10 establishes a floor for meaningful correlation.

\textbf{Intra-benchmark reference:} Correlations among MMLU subjects establish a ceiling for single-construct measures.

\subsubsection{Evaluation Protocol}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Extract benchmark scores from Open LLM Leaderboard for all 50 models
\item Compute Spearman correlation for all benchmark pairs
\item Apply Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons
\item Generate 95\% confidence intervals via bootstrap
\item Apply PCA to standardized scores
\end{enumerate}
