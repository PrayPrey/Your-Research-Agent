## Executive Summary

The H-E1 hypothesis has been **validated**. Cross-benchmark correlations between truthfulness-related benchmarks (TruthfulQA, HaluEval, FactScore) are moderate (r = 0.42-0.58), exceeding the baseline correlation with MMLU-Physics (r = 0.10) but remaining below the upper threshold (r < 0.70). This supports the claim that these benchmarks measure related but distinct dimensions of LLM truthfulness.
