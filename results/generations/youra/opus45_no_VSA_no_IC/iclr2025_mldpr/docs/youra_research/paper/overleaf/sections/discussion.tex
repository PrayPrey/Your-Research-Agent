\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Interpreting the Existence Result}

The near-perfect fingerprint classification demonstrates that fine-tuning creates highly discriminative representation signatures. The asymmetric confusion matrix suggests different benchmarks create fingerprints of varying strength.

\subsection{Why BFS Failed to Predict Gap}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Ceiling effect:} All models achieved BFS $> 0.999$
    \item \textbf{Binary fingerprints:} May be presence/absence, not graded
    \item \textbf{Domain shift dominance:} Gap may reflect domain distance, not fingerprints
    \item \textbf{Sample size:} $n=6$ limits statistical power
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item 2 benchmarks (PoC) vs.\ planned 5
    \item BFS metric saturation
    \item H-M2 not executed with real data
    \item ResNet-50 only; Transformers may differ
\end{itemize}
