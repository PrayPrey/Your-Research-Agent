\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experiment 1: Existence (H-E1)}

Statistics baseline achieves $R^2 = 0.9995$ across all $N$, confirming learnable signal exists.

\subsection{Experiment 2: Equivariance Verification (H-M1)}

NFN passes 100\% of equivariance tests (2500/2500). Max error: $8.94 \times 10^{-8}$. Architecture is numerically equivariant.

\subsection{Experiment 3: Data Efficiency (H-M2)}

At $N=500$ (10 seeds):

\begin{table}[h]
\centering

\toprule

\midrule
NFN & 0.9985 & 0.0007 \\
MLP & $-1.50$ & 0.77 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

$\Delta R^2 = 2.50$, $p = 4.58 \times 10^{-6}$. Effect size 25$\times$ larger than predicted.

\subsection{Experiment 4: Convergence (H-C1)}

At $N=5000$: Statistics $R^2 = 0.9996$, NFN $R^2 = 0.9973$, MLP $R^2 = -1.08$. MLP still fails---methods do not converge.

\subsection{Experiment 5: Crossing Point (H-C2)}

No crossing point exists. NFN achieves $R^2 \approx 0.995$ at $N=100$; Statistics achieves $R^2 \approx 0.9995$ consistently.
