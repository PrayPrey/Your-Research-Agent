\section{Conclusion}

One in four preference battles hides a silent failure. Mode~3 (overconfident misalignment) constitutes \textbf{23.7\%} of Chatbot Arena battles---reward models confident where humans disagree.

\textbf{Contributions}: (1) First quantification of overconfident RM misalignment in large-scale preference data. (2) Mode decomposition framework as alignment diagnostic. (3) Negative results ruling out semantic divergence and prompt subjectivity as mechanisms.

The mechanism quest continues. Priority directions include: style-aware embeddings that capture presentation differences, reward model attention analysis to identify attended features, full ensemble validation across multiple reward models, and cross-dataset replication to establish generalizability.
