
Analysis of 57,477 Chatbot Arena battles reveals that Mode 3 (overconfident misalignment) constitutes 23.7\% of samples: reward models confident where humans disagree. Two candidate mechanisms---semantic divergence and prompt subjectivity---are not supported by the data.

\textbf{Contributions}: (1) First quantification of overconfident reward model misalignment at scale. (2) Mode decomposition framework (entropy $\times$ variance) as alignment diagnostic. (3) Negative results constraining the hypothesis space for future mechanistic investigation.

\textbf{Future work}: Full ensemble validation with multiple reward models; style-aware embedding analysis; cross-dataset replication; human annotation studies to identify features driving disagreement.
