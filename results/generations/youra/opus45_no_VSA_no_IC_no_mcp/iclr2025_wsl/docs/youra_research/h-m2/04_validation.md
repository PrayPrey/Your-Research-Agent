## Executive Summary

**Gate Result:** LIMITATION_RECORDED (SHOULD_WORK gate inconclusive)

The sample efficiency hypothesis (h-m2) could not be validated on the current synthetic MNIST-INR dataset. All three architectures (MLP, DWS, NFT) achieve 100% accuracy (AUC = 1.0) at all training data fractions (25%, 50%, 100%), indicating the dataset is too easy to differentiate sample complexity between architectures.

**Key Finding:** Dataset ceiling effect prevents sample efficiency measurement. The hypothesis remains plausible but requires a harder benchmark (TrojAI/ModelZoo) to test.
