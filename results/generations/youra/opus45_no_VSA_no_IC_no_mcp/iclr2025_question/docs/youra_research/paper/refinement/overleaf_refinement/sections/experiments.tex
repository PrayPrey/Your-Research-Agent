\subsubsection{4.3 Evaluation Metrics}

\textbf{Primary Metric:} AUROC for hallucination detection ranking quality.

\textbf{Effect Size:} Cohen's d (threshold: d > 0.2).

\textbf{Orthogonality:} Pearson r (threshold: r < 0.3).

\textbf{Complementarity:} Discordant proportion (threshold: >15\%) and subset AUROC (threshold: >0.6).
