
\subsubsection{6.1 Key Findings}

\textbf{Finding 1:} Orthogonality is confirmed. With approximately 5\% shared variance (r = 0.228), entropy and consistency measure different phenomena.

\textbf{Finding 2:} Consistency shows large discriminative power. Cohen's d = 1.068 indicates approximately one standard deviation of separation between correct and incorrect responses.

\textbf{Finding 3:} Discordant cases exhibit complementary predictive value. On 18.1\% of questions where methods disagree, each achieves AUROC > 0.75 on its winning subset.

\subsubsection{6.2 Mechanistic Interpretation}

The results suggest a dual-signal model of LLM uncertainty:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Entropy captures epistemic uncertainty:} When the model lacks knowledge, its next-token distribution becomes diffuse.
\item \textbf{Consistency captures generation stability:} When knowledge is unstable or fabricated, repeated sampling produces divergent outputs.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{6.3 Limitations}

\textbf{Single model.} Experiments used only LLaMA-2-7B. Results may not generalize to other architectures or scales.

\textbf{Single benchmark.} TruthfulQA's adversarial design may inflate effect sizes compared to naturally-occurring hallucinations.

\textbf{Hybrid detector not tested.} While orthogonality and complementarity are demonstrated, a combined detector has not been built or evaluated.

\textbf{Fixed parameters.} N=5 samples and temperature=1.0 were used without ablation of alternatives.
