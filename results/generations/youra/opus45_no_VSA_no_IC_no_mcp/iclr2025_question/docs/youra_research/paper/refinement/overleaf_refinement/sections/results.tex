
\subsubsection{5.1 Main Results: Orthogonality}

The correlation between entropy and consistency scores is low:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Metric & Value & p-value \\
\midrule
Pearson r & 0.228 & 4.11 $\times 10^{-11}$ \\
Spearman $\rho$ & 0.241 & 3.18 $\times 10^{-12}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}
\end{table}

With r = 0.228, entropy and consistency share approximately 5.2\% of variance ($r^2$ = 0.052).
