\section{Related Work}

\textbf{Preference Learning.} RLHF emerged through InstructGPT \citep{ouyang2022training} and Anthropic's work \citep{bai2022training}, establishing the SFT$\rightarrow$reward model$\rightarrow$PPO pipeline. \citet{rafailov2023direct} introduced DPO as an alternative bypassing explicit reward modeling. Prior comparisons focused on aggregate benchmark performance, finding similar results.

\textbf{Alignment Evaluation.} TruthfulQA \citep{lin2022truthfulqa} evaluates truthfulness; BIG-bench \citep{srivastava2023beyond} provides diverse tasks; HHH evaluates helpfulness and harmlessness. Our work examines cross-benchmark profiles rather than single-benchmark accuracy.

\textbf{Optimization Landscapes.} Work on neural network loss landscapes \citep{li2018visualizing} suggests different optimization paths can lead to qualitatively different solutions. We test whether this applies to preference learning.
