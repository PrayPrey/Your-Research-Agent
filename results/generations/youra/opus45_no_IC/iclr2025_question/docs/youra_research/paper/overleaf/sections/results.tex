\section{Results}

\subsection{Benchmark Clustering (H-E1)}

Hierarchical clustering on the JS-divergence matrix reveals two distinct benchmark families with silhouette score \textbf{0.8245}, substantially exceeding our 0.5 threshold.

\textbf{Cluster 1 (Factual Recall):} TriviaQA, Natural Questions, SQuAD

\textbf{Cluster 2 (Entity/Claim):} PopQA, HaluEval-QA, FEVER

Within-cluster JS-divergence averages 0.08 versus 0.48 cross-cluster---a 6$\times$ difference indicating genuine family structure.

Semantic entropy strongly separates correct from incorrect responses on TriviaQA:

Incorrect responses show 3$\times$ higher entropy than correct responses.

\subsection{Distribution Similarity by Family (H-M2)}

Same-family benchmark pairs show significantly lower JS-divergence than cross-family pairs:

\midrule

The perfect Cliff's delta indicates complete separation between family types.

\subsection{Within-Cluster Transfer Success (H-M3)}

Mean degradation of \textbf{0.032} is well below our 0.08 threshold, confirming that thresholds calibrated on one within-cluster benchmark transfer effectively to another.

\subsection{Cross-Cluster Transfer Failure (H-M4)}

Mean degradation of \textbf{0.223} substantially exceeds our 0.15 failure threshold. The ratio of cross-cluster to within-cluster degradation is \textbf{7$\times$}, demonstrating that cluster membership is the critical factor.
