## Mechanism Verification

### Threshold Transfer Protocol
1. Calibrate threshold on source benchmark at target FPR=0.1
2. Apply calibrated threshold to target benchmark
3. Compute AUROC degradation = source_auroc - target_auroc

### Findings
- Within-cluster transfer shows minimal performance loss
- Degradation consistent across both transfer directions
- Results support H-M2 finding (JS-divergence 0.0823 for same-family pairs)
