\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{Research Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Measurability}: Can we reliably quantify benchmark concentration across major ML venues?
    \item \textbf{Predictive Power}: Does concentration at the venue level predict individual paper adoption of standard benchmarks?
    \item \textbf{Temporal Dynamics}: Is there evidence for temporal lock-in?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Dataset}

Our corpus comprises papers from NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR spanning 2018--2024, yielding 21 venue-year observations. The dataset encompasses 10,636 papers with valid dataset annotations. Citation relationships were obtained from Semantic Scholar, providing 77,963 citing-cited paper pairs.

\subsection{Baselines}

We compare our findings against two baselines:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Null model (cross-sectional only)}: The hypothesis that concentration and adoption are unrelated across venue-years.
    \item \textbf{Citation-independent adoption}: The hypothesis that papers adopt benchmarks independent of what they cite.
\end{enumerate}
