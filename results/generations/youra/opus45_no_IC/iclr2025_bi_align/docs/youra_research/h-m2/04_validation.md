## Executive Summary

H-M2 tested whether AI formality varies as a function of human formality in immediate responses. The experiment found a **statistically significant positive correlation (r = 0.152, p < 0.001)** between human and AI formality scores, exceeding the gate threshold of |r| > 0.1.

## Implications for Main Hypothesis

H-M2 PASS confirms the bidirectional formality accommodation hypothesis from the AI side:
- **H-M1 (PASS):** Humans adapt to AI patterns (r = 0.0134)
- **H-M2 (PASS):** AI responses correlate with human formality (r = 0.152)

The AI-to-human correlation is notably stronger than human-to-AI, suggesting AI models may be more responsive to input formality than humans are to AI output patterns.
