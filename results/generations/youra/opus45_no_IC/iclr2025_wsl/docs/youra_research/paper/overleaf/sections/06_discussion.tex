\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Massive Sample Efficiency Gap:} NFN achieves $R^2=0.95$ at N=1K where MLP achieves $R^2=0.35$.

    \item \textbf{Perfect NFN Invariance:} Deviation $< 1.19 \times 10^{-7}$ confirms mathematical guarantee.

    \item \textbf{MLP Dissociation:} $R^2=0.986$ coexists with invariance$=0.63$ at N=40K.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item H-E1 used synthetic data (later validated on real Model Zoo)
    \item Single architecture family (CIFAR-10 CNNs)
    \item NFN-Scrambled baseline not implemented
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Broader Impact}

Architectural constraints are necessary, not merely convenient, for certain symmetries. Data quantity cannot substitute for structural inductive bias.
