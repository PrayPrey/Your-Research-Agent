
\textbf{Dataset:} LongBench with 21 datasets across 6 categories.

\textbf{Model:} Llama-2-7B (32 layers, 32 heads, 4096 context length, FP16 precision).

\textbf{Experimental Protocol:}
\begin{itemize}
\item H-E1 (Clustering): Gap statistic on $21\times 6$ response matrix with B = 500 bootstrap samples
\item H-M1 (Entropy Discrimination): One-way ANOVA on entropy across 6 domains, 30 samples per domain (180 total)
\item H-M2 (Entropy-Tolerance): Two-sample t-test comparing high/low entropy groups under 40\% eviction, 30 samples (5 per domain)
\end{itemize}
