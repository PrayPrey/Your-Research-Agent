\section{Conclusion}

We demonstrated that simplicity bias creates a detectable signature in training dynamics: minority-group samples exhibit 8$\times$ higher loss at 5\% of training epochs. This signal enables 6.6$\times$ improvement over random baseline for minority detection without group labels.

Through linear probe analysis, we established the mechanism: representations encode spurious features with $\sim$10 percentage points higher accuracy than core features throughout training---a magnitude gap rather than timing gap with pretrained features.

\textbf{The 8$\times$ loss difference is a window into how neural networks prioritize simpler patterns, creating systematic disadvantages for minority groups that can now be detected and addressed during training.}
