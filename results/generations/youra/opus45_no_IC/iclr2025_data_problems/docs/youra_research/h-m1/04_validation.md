**Hypothesis:** The Pile training corpus contains measurable 13-gram overlap (>1% of benchmark content) with standard benchmarks (MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag, WinoGrande).

## Executive Summary

The n-gram overlap detection pipeline executed successfully. While some contamination was detected (4 MMLU items with >17% overlap), no benchmark achieved >1% mean overlap required by the gate condition. This is attributed to using a 50k document subset (0.006%) of the full 825GB Pile corpus.

## Gate Assessment

### Primary Gate: SHOULD_WORK

**Condition:** At least one benchmark shows >1% mean overlap  
**Result:** FAIL

**Benchmarks above threshold:** None  
**Highest overlap benchmark:** MMLU (0.0035% mean)

### Limitation Note

The gate failure is attributable to **insufficient corpus coverage**, not mechanism failure:
- Used 50,000 documents (0.006%) of 825GB Pile
- Individual high-overlap items (17.4% max) confirm n-gram matching works
- Prior work (Yang et al.) on RedPajama with full corpus found 8-18% overlap

**Recommended Action:** Repeat with full Pile corpus or pre-computed ngram indices from EleutherAI.
