2. **Worst calibrated:** Misconceptions/Myths cluster (ECE=0.251)
3. **Pattern:** Abstract/philosophical topics show worse calibration than factual domains

This confirms the existence hypothesis: LLMs exhibit category-dependent calibration behavior on TruthfulQA.
