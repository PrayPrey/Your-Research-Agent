\section{Appendix}

\subsection{Category Clustering Details}

The 38 TruthfulQA categories were grouped into 7 semantic clusters based on thematic similarity. Full mapping available in supplementary materials.

\subsection{Implementation Details}

All experiments used PyTorch with the HuggingFace Transformers library. Temperature optimization used scipy.optimize.minimize\_scalar with Brent's method. Bootstrap confidence intervals computed with 100 resamples.
