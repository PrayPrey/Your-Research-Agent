\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Uniform Overconfidence Interpretation}

Our results reveal a two-layer structure: \textbf{Surface layer} --- category-specific ECE variation exists. \textbf{Deep layer} --- underlying miscalibration is uniform. All categories require maximum temperature smoothing.

We hypothesize RLHF training induces global overconfidence that dominates category effects.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Single model (Llama-2-7B only)
    \item Temperature bound saturation: T=10.0 is the upper bound; true optima may lie beyond, potentially revealing cluster differentiation at higher temperatures
    \item Simulated h-m1 data: due to GPU constraints, confidence distribution analysis used simulated data calibrated to ECE patterns, introducing circularity that limits the independence of the h-m1 finding
    \item NLL vs ECE optimization (alternative losses may differ)
\end{itemize}
