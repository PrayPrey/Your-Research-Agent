\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Findings}

\textbf{FGO's benefit comes from gradient exclusion.} Non-executed tokens receive exactly zero gradient, while executed tokens receive 1.78$\times$ stronger signal concentration.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Simulation-based efficiency}: H-M3 uses simulated learning curves. Full training required for production.
    \item \textbf{Token mapping precision}: 81\% F1 vs. 95\% target due to tokenizer-line misalignment.
    \item \textbf{PoC scale}: Single seed, limited steps. Statistical variance not fully characterized.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scope Conditions}

Results hold for: Python, 7B scale, function-level tasks, PPO algorithm.

May not hold for: other languages, repository-level tasks, other RL algorithms.
