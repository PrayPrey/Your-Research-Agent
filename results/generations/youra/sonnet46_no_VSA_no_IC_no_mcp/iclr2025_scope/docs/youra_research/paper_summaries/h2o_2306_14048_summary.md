# H2O: Heavy-Hitter Oracle for Efficient Generative Inference of LLMs
**arXiv:** 2306.14048 | **Authors:** Zhang et al., 2023 | **Citations:** ~500

---

### Abstract & Motivation
H2O identifies that a small subset of tokens ("heavy hitters") accumulate disproportionately large cumulative attention scores during autoregressive decoding. By retaining only these heavy hitters plus recent tokens, H2O achieves up to 20× KV cache memory reduction with minimal accuracy degradation. The core insight is that cumulative attention score is a reliable proxy for token importance.

### Methodology
- **Importance metric:** Cumulative sum of attention scores across all heads and layers. Token i's importance = Σ_{t>i} Σ_h α_{h,t,i}, where α is the attention weight at decode step t, head h, attending to position i.
- **Eviction policy:** Greedy eviction — at each decode step, maintain a fixed-size KV cache budget B. When budget exceeded, evict the token with lowest cumulative attention score.
- **Budget split:** Configurable ratio of heavy-hitters to recency-based (recent) tokens. Default: 20% heavy-hitters + 80% recent window, or 50/50.
- **Implementation:** Hooks into HuggingFace `past_key_values` — no model retraining required.

### Experiments & Results
- **Models tested:** OPT-6.7B, OPT-13B, LLaMA-7B, LLaMA-13B (all BASE models, non-instruct)
- **Benchmarks:** WikiText-2 perplexity, MT-Bench (generation quality), CNN/DailyMail summarization
- **KV budget:** 20% retention (80% eviction) — extreme compression
- **Key result:** H2O achieves <1 PPL degradation on WikiText-2 at 20% KV budget; outperforms StreamingLLM (static) by 2-4 PPL points; near-zero accuracy drop on MT-Bench
- **Ablation:** Heavy-hitter only vs. recency only vs. hybrid — hybrid (H2O) wins; recency-only degrades sharply on long dependencies
- **Limitations:** Evaluated on base models only; no comparison to attention-entropy or gradient-based metrics; no cross-architecture systematic test; OPT+LLaMA only

### Key Innovation vs. Baselines
| Method | Metric | Score |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Full KV | — | Baseline |
| StreamingLLM | static sink+window | ~3-5 PPL worse |
| H2O | cumulative attention | <1 PPL worse at 20% budget |

### What's Missing (Gap 1 Relevance)
H2O does NOT compare its cumulative-attention metric against: (a) attention entropy, (b) gradient-based importance, (c) prefill-observation (SnapKV-style). No unified ablation exists.
