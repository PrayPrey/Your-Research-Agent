
We organize five research questions corresponding to our contributions:

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Does SE outperform TE by $\geq$ 0.05 AUROC at 7B scale on TriviaQA?

\textbf{RQ2 (Mechanism):} Is the SE > TE gap explained by intra-cluster TE variance (paraphrase noise)?

\textbf{RQ3 (Scope):} Does the SE > TE advantage generalize to TruthfulQA?

\textbf{RQ4 (SCG Equivalence):} Is BERTScore-based SCG equivalent to SE on short factual QA (AUROC gap $\leq$ 0.03)?

\textbf{RQ5 (VC Calibration):} Is VC severely miscalibrated at 7B scale?

\subsubsection{4.3 Pre-Specified Gate Criteria}

All gates were pre-specified before running experiments:

\begin{itemize}
\item RQ1: SE $-$ TE AUROC $\geq$ 0.05; bootstrap CI on gap excludes zero (MUST\_WORK --- failure would terminate experiment line)
\item RQ2: Mean intra-cluster TE variance > 0.1 $nats^2$ on $\geq 15$ eligible questions (MUST\_WORK)
\item RQ3: SE AUROC > TE AUROC on TruthfulQA (SHOULD\_WORK)
\item RQ4: |SCG AUROC $-$ SE AUROC| $\leq$ 0.03 (SHOULD\_WORK)
\item RQ5: VC AUROC < TE AUROC (SHOULD\_WORK)
\end{itemize}
