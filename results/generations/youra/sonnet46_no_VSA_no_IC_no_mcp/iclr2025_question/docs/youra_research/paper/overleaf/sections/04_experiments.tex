\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

We design five research questions directly corresponding to our contributions:

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Does SE outperform TE by $\geq 0.05$ AUROC at 7B scale on TriviaQA?

\textbf{RQ2 (Mechanism):} Is the SE $>$ TE gap explained by intra-cluster TE variance?

\textbf{RQ3 (Scope):} Does the SE $>$ TE advantage generalize to TruthfulQA?

\textbf{RQ4 (SCG Equivalence):} Is BERTScore-based SCG equivalent to SE (AUROC gap $\leq 0.03$)?

\textbf{RQ5 (VC Calibration):} Is VC miscalibrated at 7B scale?

\subsection{Pre-specified Gate Criteria}

\begin{itemize}
\item RQ1: SE-TE gap $\geq 0.05$, bootstrap CI on gap excludes zero (MUST\_WORK)
\item RQ2: Mean intra-cluster TE variance $> 0.1$ nats$^2$ on $\geq$15 questions (MUST\_WORK)
\item RQ3: SE AUROC $>$ TE AUROC on TruthfulQA (SHOULD\_WORK)
\item RQ4: $|\text{SCG AUROC} - \text{SE AUROC}| \leq 0.03$ (SHOULD\_WORK)
\item RQ5: VC AUROC $<$ TE AUROC (SHOULD\_WORK)
\end{itemize}
