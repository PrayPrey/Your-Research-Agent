
\subsubsection{Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 (Existence):} Do synchronized multi-dimensional measurements produce sufficient variance for correlation analysis? Without adequate variance ($\sigma$>0.2) across reliability, robustness, and fairness, correlation estimates become unstable.

\textbf{RQ2 (Memorization):} Is reliability-robustness correlation positive (r>0.3) on factual content, consistent with shared memorization?

\textbf{RQ3 (Alignment Tax):} Is fairness-reliability correlation negative (r<-0.2) overall, consistent with RLHF safety-accuracy trade-off?

\textbf{RQ4 (Moderation):} Do correlation magnitudes differ significantly between factual versus misinformation prompt strata?

\subsubsection{Experimental Protocol}

\textbf{h-e1: Variance Validation.} Sample: 817 TruthfulQA prompts on Llama-2-7b. Procedure: (1) Generate outputs with synchronized parameters; (2) Score reliability (GPT-4), robustness (SBERT), fairness (HONEST + demographic augmentation); (3) Compute $\sigma$ for each dimension; (4) Validate $\sigma$ > 0.2 for all three dimensions. Success criteria: primary $\sigma _reliability$ > 0.2, $\sigma _robustness$ > 0.2, $\sigma _fairness$ > 0.2; secondary no floor/ceiling effects (mean $\in$ [0.3, 0.7]). Gate: MUST\_WORK.

\textbf{h-m1: Memorization-Driven Coupling.} Sample: 343 factual prompts (Science, Law, History, Geography) on Llama-2-7b. Procedure: (1) Subset TruthfulQA to factual stratum; (2) Compute Pearson r(reliability, robustness) with 95\% CI via Fisher z-transform; (3) Test against threshold r>0.3, p<0.05 (two-tailed); (4) Compare with misinformation stratum for mechanism specificity. Success criteria: primary r > 0.3, p < 0.05; secondary 95\% CI lower bound > 0.2; tertiary contrast with misinformation stratum |$\Delta r|$ > 0.1. Gate: MUST\_WORK.

\textbf{h-m2: Alignment Tax Quantification.} Sample: 817 TruthfulQA prompts on Llama-2-7b. Procedure: (1) Compute Pearson r(fairness, reliability) on full dataset; (2) Test against threshold r<-0.2, p<0.05 (two-tailed); (3) Verify 95\% CI upper bound <-0.1. Success criteria: primary r < -0.2, p < 0.05; secondary 95\% CI upper bound < -0.1. Gate: SHOULD\_WORK.

\textbf{h-m3: Prompt-Type Moderation.} Sample: 10 factual + 10 misinformation prompts (pilot) on Llama-2-7b. Procedure: (1) Compute r\_factual and r\_misinfo separately; (2) Fisher z-test for independent correlations; (3) Test significance p<0.05, effect size |$\Delta r|\geq 0.1$. Success criteria: primary Fisher z-test p < 0.05; secondary |r\_factual - r\_misinfo| $\geq$ 0.1; tertiary directional pattern (r\_factual > 0.4, r\_misinfo < 0.3). Gate: SHOULD\_WORK. Note: executed as underpowered pilot (n=10 per stratum); power analysis recommended $n\geq 85$ per stratum for 80\% power to detect r=0.3 at $\alpha =0.05$.
