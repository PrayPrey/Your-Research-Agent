## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we improve bidirectional human-AI alignment methods to better capture the dynamic, evolving interactions between humans and AI systems?

### Refined Question

Can existing alignment evaluation benchmarks be analyzed through a bidirectional lens to identify gaps between AI-to-human alignment (traditional RLHF, preference modeling) and human-to-AI alignment (user agency, critical evaluation, collaborative decision-making)?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Scope Analysis:** What are the current definitions and boundaries of alignment research, and how do they differ between unidirectional (AI-to-human) and bidirectional (mutual adaptation) frameworks?

2. **Specification Methods:** How are human values, behaviors, cognition, and societal norms currently represented in existing alignment benchmarks, and which representation approaches capture bidirectional dynamics?

3. **Evaluation Gaps:** What metrics and benchmarks exist for evaluating multi-objective AI alignment, and do they measure both AI adaptation to humans AND human agency preservation?

4. **Deployment Mechanisms:** How do current customizable alignment systems (steerability, interpretability, scalable oversight) balance AI-to-human alignment with preserving human critical evaluation capabilities?

5. **Interdisciplinary Integration:** What insights from HCI, social sciences, and cognitive science are missing from current ML-centric alignment benchmarks that could inform bidirectional alignment evaluation?
