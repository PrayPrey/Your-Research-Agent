
\textbf{Agreement Rate}: Percentage of responses within $\pm 1$ year of modal date. Target: >70\% for $\geq 2/3$ benchmarks.

\textbf{Fleiss' Kappa}: Inter-rater reliability. 0.21--0.40 = fair, 0.41--0.60 = moderate.

\textbf{Levene's Test}: Variance homogeneity test. p < 0.05 = significant variance shift pre/post convergence.

\textbf{Detection Rate}: Percentage of benchmarks where saturation signal detected.

\textbf{Lead Time}: Months between saturation and paradigm shift adoption. Positive = saturation precedes shift.

\textbf{Precision/Recall}: Citation velocity correlation accuracy. Target: precision 0.80, recall 0.80.
