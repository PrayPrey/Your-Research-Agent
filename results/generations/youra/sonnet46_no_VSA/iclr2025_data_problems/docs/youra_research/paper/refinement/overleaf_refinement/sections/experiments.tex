
\subsubsection{4.1 Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1:} Does global k-th percentile thresholding on ccnet\_perplexity produce statistically significant language-group retention disparity (Cramér's V > 0.10 with Holm p < 0.05)?

\textbf{RQ2:} Is the disparity consistent across the full practical range of threshold aggressiveness (k $\in$ {10, 20, 30, 40, 50})?

\textbf{RQ3:} Does the retention ordering align with the CCNet KenLM mechanism prediction --- Germanic (en, de) systematically below Romance (es, fr, it)?

\subsubsection{4.4 Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Cramér's V} (primary): language $\times$ retention association strength
\item \textbf{Per-language retention rates}: human-interpretable retention proportion per language per k
\item \textbf{Max--min retention gap}: worst-case disparity between any two language groups (percentage points)
\item \textbf{Statistical significance}: Holm-Bonferroni corrected chi-square p-values
\end{itemize}
