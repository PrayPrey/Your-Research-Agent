\subsection*{A. Additional Figures}

\textbf{Figure A1: Schoenfeld Residuals.} PH assumption diagnostic (\texttt{h-m1/figures/schoenfeld\_residuals.png}). Visual inspection showed no obvious violation. Automated quantitative test was not completed due to a string-type covariate encoding error (\texttt{task\_path}); fixing requires integer-encoding \texttt{task\_path} before calling \texttt{check\_assumptions()}.

\textbf{Figure A2: Coverage Heatmap.} Matched vs.\ unmatched h-e2 benchmarks in the pwc-archive/evaluation-tables fuzzy join (\texttt{h-e1/figures/coverage\_heatmap.png}). 75/87 benchmarks matched (86.2\%); 12 below token\_sort\_ratio=85 threshold.

\textbf{Figure A3: Predictor Distributions.} Histograms of \texttt{log\_unique\_paper\_count\_at\_intro} and \texttt{paper\_diversity\_ratio\_at\_intro} across 67 benchmarks with computed diversity statistics (\texttt{h-e1/figures/predictor\_distributions.png}).

\textbf{Figure A4: Correlation Matrix.} Pearson $r$ heatmap for all Cox covariates (\texttt{h-e1/figures/correlation\_matrix.png}). Confirms low inter-predictor correlations (max $r$ = $-0.324$ between diversity predictors).
