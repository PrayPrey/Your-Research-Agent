\textbf{Critical clarification:} These two runs test \emph{different constructs}. The prior result (HR = 0.871) is for \emph{score velocity} (how fast SOTA scores improved); the current result is for \emph{submission-count diversity} (how many distinct papers submitted). The current null does not supersede or invalidate the prior directional signal for score velocity --- that predictor has not been tested at adequate power. The two results are complementary: submission-count breadth is definitively null; score velocity remains an open question at full power.
