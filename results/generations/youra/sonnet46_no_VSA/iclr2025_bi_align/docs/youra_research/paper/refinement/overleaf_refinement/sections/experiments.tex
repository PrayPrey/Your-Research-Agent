
\subsubsection{4.1 Experimental Questions}

The experimental design is structured around four sequential sub-hypotheses:

\textbf{EQ1 (H-E1):} Is the data infrastructure valid for cross-dataset correlation analysis? Gate: N\_complete $\geq$ 30 after join.

\textbf{EQ2 (H-M1):} Does MMLU function as a substantive scale covariate for both alignment benchmarks? Gate: $R^{2}(MMLU \times$ TruthfulQA) > 0.05 AND $R^{2}(MMLU \times$ bias proxy) > 0.05.

\textbf{EQ3 (H-M2):} Is the reduction from raw to partial Spearman rho statistically significant? (Primary hypothesis.)

\textbf{EQ4 (H-M3):} Can the residual partial\_rho be assigned to a pre-specified structural scenario?

\subsubsection{4.2 Datasets}

\textbf{Open LLM Leaderboard v1.} 500 open-weight model records (TruthfulQA MC2, MMLU, ARC Challenge, HellaSwag). Sourced from \texttt{open-llm-leaderboard-old/results}.

\textbf{Analysis Dataset.} N=296 models after inner join and dropna on TruthfulQA, bias proxy, and MMLU.

\subsubsection{4.4 Implementation}

All analyses implemented in Python 3.11. Core libraries: scipy $\geq$ 1.10, pingouin 0.6.1, pandas $\geq$ 1.5, numpy $\geq$ 1.23, rapidfuzz. Total test cases: 57/57 pass (H-E1: 14, H-M1: 12, H-M2: 12, H-M3: 16 + internal tasks). A known pingouin 0.6.1 column naming discrepancy (the library uses \texttt{p\textbackslash \_val}, not \texttt{p-val}) was identified and corrected during the H-M2 coder-validator cycle.
