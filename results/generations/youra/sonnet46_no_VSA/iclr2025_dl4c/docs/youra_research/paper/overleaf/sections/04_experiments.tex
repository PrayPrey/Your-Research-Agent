\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

We design experiments to answer four research questions:

\textbf{RQ1:} Does SFT source identity produce statistically significant pass@1 differences at 1.3B scale under controlled conditions?

\textbf{RQ2:} Does same-source training produce symmetric benchmark specialization?

\textbf{RQ3:} Does code-embedding alignment predict pass@1 rank across conditions?

\textbf{RQ4:} Do source identity effects persist at 7B scale with reduced magnitude?

\subsection{Baselines}

The four SFT conditions (Table~\ref{tab:conditions}) plus the zero-shot base model serve as the control
baseline. Equal-mix is the structural falsifier for diversity over alignment.

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Primary:} pass@1 on HumanEval+ (complete across all conditions and scales)
\item \textbf{Secondary:} pass@1 on MBPP+ (available for HE-only vs MBPP-only; incomplete for full 4-condition analysis)
\item \textbf{Mechanistic:} Spearman $\rho$ with permutation test; dual-encoder concordance
\item \textbf{Scale:} Absolute condition spread and $\eta^2$ (reported together due to $\eta^2$ limitation at 7B)
\end{itemize}
