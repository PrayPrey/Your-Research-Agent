\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Sub-Quadratic Architecture Conversion}

\textbf{MOHAWK \citep{Bick2024MOHAWK}} establishes a three-stage distillation pipeline
(matrix alignment $\to$ hidden-state alignment $\to$ logit distillation) for converting
transformers to SSMs with SSD structured mixers. MOHAWK reports SSD Frobenius approximation
error $\approx$0.097 at $N=64/512$, but evaluates on short-context lm-eval tasks only, using
Phi-1.5 (1.3B) as the base model.

\textbf{LAWCAT \citep{Liu2025LAWCAT}} converts Mistral-7B to linear attention using Conv1D and
normalized gated linear attention, achieving $>$90\% passkey retrieval at 22K tokens with under
1B distillation tokens. Evaluated on synthetic needle tasks only; no cross-strategy comparison.

\textbf{Our work} fixes the base model (LLaMA-3-8B) across both strategies---eliminating the
capability confound that dominates cross-family comparisons---and separates the mechanism
measurement from behavioral evaluation.

\subsection{Long-Context Recurrent Model Evaluation}

\textbf{Overflow Prevention \citep{BenKish2025OverflowPrevention}} evaluates scratch-trained
SSMs (Falcon3-Mamba, RecurrentGemma, RWKV6) on LongBench v2 with category breakdowns, finding
systematic degradation on multi-document QA and synthetic tasks. This provides our external
scratch-trained SSM baseline.

\textbf{LongBench v2 \citep{Bai2024LongBenchV2}} provides the evaluation framework: 503
questions across 6 task categories (single-doc QA, multi-doc QA, summarization, few-shot,
synthetic, code) at 8K--2M context. \textbf{SSM Limits \citep{Zhan2025OvercomingSSM}} prove
SSMs cannot solve multi-query joint recall in sub-quadratic time, providing theoretical grounding
for expected retrieval degradation.

\subsection{Hybrid Architectures}

Retaining a fraction of attention layers substantially recovers long-context performance, as
shown in Falcon-H1 \citep{Zuo2025FalconH1}. MOHAWK's Hybrid-4 (4 middle attention layers
retained) motivates our control condition.

\subsection{Positioning}

No prior study: (a) compares MOHAWK-SSM and LAWCAT on the same base model and LongBench v2,
(b) separately measures SSD approximation quality before interpreting behavioral results, or
(c) uses mixed-effects task-type $\times$ strategy interaction testing for converted models.
