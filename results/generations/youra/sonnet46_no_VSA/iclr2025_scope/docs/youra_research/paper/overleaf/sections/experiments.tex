\section{Experimental Setup}

\textbf{RQ1 (Mechanism Gate):} Does SSD Frobenius error scale sub-linearly with $N$ for
LLaMA-3-8B?

\textbf{RQ2 (Behavioral):} Does conversion strategy produce a task-type $\times$ strategy
interaction on LongBench v2 $\Delta_\text{norm}$? \emph{(Results pending H-E1 completion.)}

\textbf{RQ3 (Depth-slope):} Does LAWCAT's Conv1D produce shallower depth degradation than
MOHAWK-SSM? \emph{(Infrastructure validated; requires H-E1 checkpoints.)}

\textbf{Hardware:} 4$\times$ H100 NVL 96GB.
\textbf{Training data:} \texttt{monology/pile-uncopyrighted} (C4 rate-limited).
\textbf{Evaluation:} THUDM/LongBench v2, 503 questions, logit-based MCQ scoring.
