\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Design: Mechanism Gate Before Behavioral Test}

We structure evaluation in two sequential parts. The \textbf{mechanism gate (H-M1)} asks: does
SSD approximate LLaMA-3-8B attention sub-linearly? The \textbf{behavioral test (H-E1)} asks:
does conversion strategy produce a task-type interaction on LongBench v2? This sequencing is
deliberate: if the mechanism gate fails, behavioral degradation reflects distillation failure;
if it passes, behavioral degradation reflects bounded-state architecture.

\subsection{Mechanism Gate: SSD Frobenius Scaling (H-M1)}

We load LLaMA-3-8B (bfloat16) and extract per-layer attention matrices at
$N \in \{512, 1024, 2048\}$ on 50 random inputs from \texttt{monology/pile-uncopyrighted}
(seed=42) at $N=512$ and 5 inputs at $N=1024, 2048$. For each of 32 layers, we materialize
the $N \times N$ attention and SSD transfer matrices using MOHAWK's official
\texttt{materialize\_mixer} implementation and fit by minimizing Frobenius distance:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \left\| \text{materialize\_mixer}(\theta, N) - M_{\text{attn}} \right\|_F
\end{equation}
using Adam (lr$=10^{-3}$, 500 steps, float32). \textbf{Normalized error} $= \text{raw\_error} / N$
enables fair comparison across lengths and matches MOHAWK Table~6 scale.

\textbf{Gate criteria:} (1) log-log slope $\beta \leq 0.5$; (2) 90th percentile error/$N \leq 0.3$
at max $N$.

\subsection{Controlled Conversion Comparison (H-E1)}

Four model variants, all using fixed LLaMA-3-8B:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Model variants for controlled comparison.}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Budget} \\
\midrule
Teacher    & Unconverted LLaMA-3-8B                        & ---         \\
MOHAWK-SSM & 3-stage distillation, SSD mixer               & $\sim$1B tokens \\
LAWCAT     & 2-phase, Conv1D + gated linear attention       & $\sim$1B tokens \\
Hybrid-4   & MOHAWK Stage 3 + 4 middle attention layers    & $\sim$1B tokens \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Primary metric:}
$\Delta_\text{norm} = (\text{Acc}_\text{teacher} - \text{Acc}_\text{student}) /
\text{Acc}_\text{teacher}$ per category.

\textbf{Statistical test:} Mixed-effects model
$\Delta_\text{norm} \sim \text{TaskType} \times \text{Strategy} + (1|\text{Task})$,
Holm correction. Gate: $\Delta_\text{norm}^\text{MOHAWK}(\text{retrieval}) /
\Delta_\text{norm}^\text{LAWCAT}(\text{retrieval}) \geq 2.0$, CI $> 1.0$, $p < 0.01$.

\subsection{Depth-Slope Analysis (H-M2)}

Needle depth percentile $=$ keyword\_start\_offset / context\_length. Logistic regression
$P(\text{correct}) \sim \text{DepthPercentile} + (1|\text{Task})$ per model. Gate:
$|\beta_\text{depth}^\text{MOHAWK}| \geq 2 \times |\beta_\text{depth}^\text{LAWCAT}|$,
non-overlapping CIs. \emph{Requires H-E1 checkpoints; statistical pipeline validated on
proxy data.}
