1. **Title**: LLM-Upgraded Graph Reinforcement Learning for Carbon-Aware Job Scheduling in Smart Manufacturing (arXiv:2512.06351)
   - **Authors**: Zhiying Yang, Fang Liu, Wei Zhang, Xin Lou, Malcolm Yoke Hean Low, Boon Ping Gan
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces LUCA, a framework that integrates large language models (LLMs) with graph reinforcement learning to optimize job scheduling in smart manufacturing. LUCA aims to minimize both makespan and carbon emissions by dynamically adjusting scheduling decisions based on real-time data. The framework demonstrates significant improvements over traditional algorithms, achieving up to 12.2% lower makespan while maintaining the same emission levels.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Balancing Multiple Objectives**: Developing reinforcement learning frameworks that effectively balance competing objectives, such as training efficiency, cost, and carbon emissions, remains a significant challenge.

2. **Real-Time Carbon Intensity Data Integration**: Accurately integrating real-time carbon intensity data into resource allocation decisions is complex, requiring robust data collection and processing mechanisms.

3. **Scalability of Carbon-Aware Scheduling**: Ensuring that carbon-aware scheduling algorithms scale effectively with the size and complexity of large language model training is a critical concern.

4. **Model Performance Trade-offs**: Minimizing carbon emissions without adversely affecting model performance and convergence rates presents a delicate trade-off that needs careful management.

5. **Generalization Across Diverse Environments**: Developing solutions that generalize across various data center configurations and geographic regions with differing carbon intensity profiles is challenging.
