1. **Title**: A System-Level Taxonomy of Failure Modes in Large Language Model Applications (arXiv:2511.19933)
   - **Authors**: Vaishali Vinay
   - **Summary**: This paper presents a taxonomy of fifteen hidden failure modes in large language model (LLM) applications, such as multi-step reasoning drift and context-boundary degradation. It highlights the gap in current evaluation practices and proposes design principles for building reliable LLM systems.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Faults in Deep Reinforcement Learning Programs: A Taxonomy and A Detection Approach (arXiv:2101.00135)
   - **Authors**: Amin Nikanjam, Mohammad Mehdi Morovati, Foutse Khomh, Houssem Ben Braiek
   - **Summary**: The authors analyze 761 artifacts from DRL programs to identify and categorize faults, resulting in a validated taxonomy. They also introduce DRLinter, a model-based fault detection tool that successfully identifies faults in synthetic DRL programs.
   - **Year**: 2021

3. **Title**: UDIS: Unsupervised Discovery of Bias in Deep Visual Recognition Models (arXiv:2110.15499)
   - **Authors**: Arvindkumar Krishnakumar, Viraj Prabhu, Sruthi Sudhakar, Judy Hoffman
   - **Summary**: This work introduces UDIS, an unsupervised algorithm that identifies subpopulations in datasets and surfaces systematic failure modes in deep visual recognition models, aiding in the detection of biases without the need for annotated attributes.
   - **Year**: 2021

4. **Title**: Not all Failure Modes are Created Equal: Training Deep Neural Networks for Explicable (Mis)Classification (arXiv:2006.14841)
   - **Authors**: Alberto Olmo, Sailik Sengupta, Subbarao Kambhampati
   - **Summary**: The paper discusses methods to obtain class-level semantics capturing human expectations regarding class similarities and proposes weighted loss functions to penalize inexplicable misclassifications, leading to more understandable failure modes in deep neural networks.
   - **Year**: 2020

5. **Title**: Tiny Machine Learning: Progress and Futures (arXiv:2403.19076)
   - **Authors**: Ji Lin
   - **Summary**: This survey addresses the challenges of deploying deep learning models on resource-constrained devices, highlighting issues like memory limitations and the need for co-design in TinyML applications.
   - **Year**: 2024

6. **Title**: Efficient Transformers: A Survey (arXiv:2009.06732)
   - **Authors**: Yi Tay, Mostafa Dehghani, Dara Bahri, Donald Metzler
   - **Summary**: The authors provide a comprehensive survey of efficient Transformer architectures, discussing various approaches to improve the efficiency and scalability of Transformers in different applications.
   - **Year**: 2020

7. **Title**: Deep Learning for Case-Based Reasoning through Prototypes: A Neural Network that Explains Its Predictions (arXiv:1710.04806)
   - **Authors**: Oscar Li, Hao Liu, Chaofan Chen, Cynthia Rudin
   - **Summary**: This work introduces a neural network architecture that explains its predictions by learning prototypes, facilitating case-based reasoning and enhancing interpretability in deep learning models.
   - **Year**: 2017

8. **Title**: SmartChoices: Augmenting Software with Learned Implementations (arXiv:2304.13033)
   - **Authors**: Eric Chen, Daniel Golovin, Gábor Bartók, Emily Donahue, Tzu-Kuo Huang, Efi Kokiopoulou, Ruoyan Qin, Nikhil Sarda, Justin Sybrandt, Vincent Tjeng
   - **Summary**: The paper presents SmartChoices, a framework that integrates machine learning models into software systems to provide learned implementations, aiming to reduce engineering effort and mitigate failure modes in production environments.
   - **Year**: 2023

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Lack of Cross-Domain Taxonomies**: The absence of a unified framework to categorize and compare deep learning failure modes across different domains hinders the identification of recurrent patterns and root causes.

2. **Data-Related Issues**: Challenges such as distribution shifts, biases, and label quality inconsistencies lead to performance degradation and unreliable model outputs.

3. **Model Limitations**: Issues related to ethics, fairness, interpretability, and scalability affect the robustness and trustworthiness of deep learning models.

4. **Deployment Challenges**: Computational demands, hardware constraints, and integration complexities pose significant obstacles to the successful deployment of deep learning systems in real-world applications.

5. **Evaluation and Monitoring Gaps**: Existing benchmarks often fail to provide insights into stability, reproducibility, and workflow integration, leading to unforeseen failures in production environments.
