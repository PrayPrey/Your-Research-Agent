**Key Challenges:**

1. **Scalability of Causal Graph Construction**: Building and maintaining causal provenance graphs for large-scale datasets is computationally intensive, requiring efficient algorithms to handle billion-scale data.

2. **Accurate Influence Tracking**: Developing methods to accurately track the influence of data combinations on model capabilities is complex, necessitating advanced techniques like gradient-based sketching.

3. **Interventional Analysis Complexity**: Systematically ablating data subgraphs to measure capability degradation involves intricate experimental designs and substantial computational resources.

4. **Validation in Controlled Settings**: Ensuring that the proposed methods work effectively in controlled environments with known data requirements is essential but challenging.

5. **Practical Tool Development**: Creating scalable and user-friendly tools for detecting contamination propagation and optimizing data mixtures requires bridging the gap between theoretical frameworks and practical applications.
