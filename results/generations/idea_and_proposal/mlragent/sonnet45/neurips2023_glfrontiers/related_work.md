1. **Title**: SSTAG: Structure-Aware Self-Supervised Learning Method for Text-Attributed Graphs (arXiv:2510.01248)

   - **Summary**: This paper introduces SSTAG, a self-supervised learning method for text-attributed graphs that integrates structural motifs and semantic features. 2. **Title**: UniGraph: Learning a Unified Cross-Domain Foundation Model for Text-Attributed Graphs (arXiv:2402.13630)
   - **Authors**: Yufei He, Yuan Sui, Xiaoxin He, Bryan Hooi
   - **Summary**: UniGraph proposes a foundation model for text-attributed graphs capable of generalizing to unseen graphs and tasks across various domains. It utilizes a cascaded architecture of language models and graph neural networks, employing masked graph modeling for self-supervised pre-training and instruction tuning for zero-shot predictions.
   - **Year**: 2024

3. **Title**: FedBook: A Unified Federated Graph Foundation Codebook with Intra-domain and Inter-domain Knowledge Modeling (arXiv:2510.07755)

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Domain Heterogeneity**: Graph data varies significantly across domains in terms of structure, semantics, and feature spaces, making it challenging to develop models that generalize well across diverse graph types.

2. **Data Scarcity in Scientific Domains**: Scientific fields often lack large annotated datasets, limiting the effectiveness of supervised learning approaches and necessitating the development of models capable of learning from limited labeled data.

3. **Scalability of Graph Models**: As graph sizes and complexities increase, ensuring that models remain computationally efficient and scalable becomes a significant challenge, especially when integrating with large language models.

4. **Effective Knowledge Transfer**: Transferring relational knowledge from data-rich domains to data-scarce scientific domains without losing critical information or introducing biases is complex and requires careful model design.

5. **Unified Representation Learning**: Developing a universal representation scheme that captures both structural motifs and domain-specific semantic features across various graph domains is essential but challenging due to the inherent diversity of graph data.
