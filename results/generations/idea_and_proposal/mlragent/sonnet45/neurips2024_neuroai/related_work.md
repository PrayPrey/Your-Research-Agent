1. **Title**: Meta-Representational Predictive Coding: Biomimetic Self-Supervised Learning (arXiv:2503.21796)
   - **Authors**: Alexander Ororbia, Karl Friston, Rajesh P. N. Rao
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces Meta-Representational Predictive Coding (MPC), a self-supervised learning framework inspired by neurobiological principles. MPC employs predictive coding mechanisms to learn representations by predicting sensory inputs across parallel streams, integrating active inference to guide learning through informative sampling.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Schema-based Active Inference Supports Rapid Generalization of Experience and Frontal Cortical Coding of Abstract Structure (arXiv:2601.18946)
   - **Authors**: Toon Van de Maele, Tim Verbelen, Dileep George, Giovanni Pezzulo
   - **Summary**: The authors propose a hierarchical active inference model that encodes abstract task structures (schemas) to facilitate rapid generalization in spatial navigation tasks. The model reproduces key behavioral and neural signatures observed in schema-based learning, providing insights into the computational principles underlying flexible behavior.
   - **Year**: 2026

3. **Title**: Divide-and-Conquer Predictive Coding: A Structured Bayesian Inference Algorithm (arXiv:2408.05834)
   - **Authors**: Eli Sennesh, Hao Wu, Tommaso Salvatori
   - **Summary**: This work presents a novel predictive coding algorithm tailored for structured generative models. The proposed divide-and-conquer predictive coding (DCPC) respects the correlation structure of the generative model and performs maximum-likelihood updates of model parameters, enhancing numerical performance and biological plausibility.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: Hierarchical Attention Network for Few-Shot Object Detection via Meta-Contrastive Learning (arXiv:2208.07039)
   - **Authors**: Dongwoo Park, Jong-Min Lee
   - **Summary**: The paper introduces a hierarchical attention network designed for few-shot object detection. By employing meta-contrastive learning, the model effectively exploits features between support and query images, achieving significant improvements in detection performance on the COCO dataset.
   - **Year**: 2022

5. **Title**: Hierarchically Structured Meta-Learning (arXiv:1905.05301)
   - **Authors**: Huaxiu Yao, Ying Wei, Junzhou Huang, Zhenhui Li
   - **Summary**: This study proposes a hierarchically structured meta-learning algorithm that customizes transferable knowledge to different clusters of tasks. By organizing tasks into a hierarchical structure, the approach addresses task uncertainty and heterogeneity, enhancing performance in few-shot image classification problems.
   - **Year**: 2019

6. **Title**: Meta-SGD: Learning to Learn Quickly (arXiv:1707.09835)
   - **Authors**: Chelsea Finn, Pieter Abbeel, Sergey Levine
   - **Summary**: The authors develop Meta-SGD, a meta-learning algorithm that learns an initialization, update direction, and learning rate for stochastic gradient descent. This approach enables rapid adaptation to new tasks with minimal data, achieving state-of-the-art results in few-shot learning scenarios.
   - **Year**: 2017

7. **Title**: Deep Learning for Case-Based Reasoning through Prototypes: A Neural Network that Explains Its Predictions (arXiv:1710.04806)
   - **Authors**: Been Kim, Rajiv Khanna, Oluwasanmi Koyejo
   - **Summary**: This paper presents a neural network architecture that enhances interpretability by reasoning through prototypes. The model classifies inputs based on their proximity to learned prototypical cases, providing explanations for its predictions and improving transparency in decision-making processes.
   - **Year**: 2017

8. **Title**: MM1: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Multimodal Language Models (arXiv:2403.09611)
   - **Authors**: B. McKinzie et al.
   - **Summary**: The authors study the development of multimodal language models (MLLMs) and identify key lessons that yield pre-trained models achieving state-of-the-art results on few-shot evaluations. The work emphasizes the importance of data and modeling choices in building effective MLLMs.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Integration Complexity**: Combining predictive coding, active inference, and meta-learning into a cohesive hierarchical neural architecture poses significant design and implementation challenges.

2. **Data Efficiency**: Achieving human-like reasoning and generalization from limited examples requires models to effectively leverage minimal data, which remains a substantial hurdle.

3. **Computational Resources**: Developing and training such complex models can be computationally intensive, potentially offsetting the benefits of data efficiency.

4. **Interpretability**: Ensuring that the model's decision-making processes are transparent and interpretable is crucial for trust and applicability in real-world scenarios.

5. **Generalization Across Domains**: Enabling the model to generalize effectively across diverse visual reasoning tasks and datasets remains a significant challenge.
