1. **Title**: From One Attack Domain to Another: Contrastive Transfer Learning with Siamese Networks for APT Detection (arXiv:2511.20500)
   - **Authors**: Sidahmed Benabderrahmane, Talal Rahwan

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Domain-Specific Constraints**: Healthcare and legal domains have unique terminologies, regulations, and decision-making processes, making it challenging to develop a unified XAI framework that effectively addresses the specific needs of both fields.

2. **Data Privacy and Security**: Both domains handle sensitive information, necessitating strict data privacy and security measures. Ensuring that XAI methods comply with these requirements while maintaining transparency is a significant challenge.

3. **Stakeholder Trust and Acceptance**: Building trust among diverse stakeholders, including medical professionals, legal practitioners, and patients or clients, is crucial. XAI systems must provide explanations that are understandable and actionable to gain widespread acceptance.

4. **Regulatory Compliance**: Both healthcare and legal sectors are heavily regulated. Developing XAI solutions that adhere to varying regulatory standards across jurisdictions adds complexity to cross-domain applications.

5. **Transferability of Explanation Patterns**: Identifying and transferring explanation patterns that are effective across domains is challenging due to differences in data structures, decision-making criteria, and contextual factors inherent to each domain.
