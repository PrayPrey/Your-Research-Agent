1. **Title**: Mind to Hand: Purposeful Robotic Control via Embodied Reasoning (arXiv:2512.08580)
   - **Authors**: Peijun Tang, Shangjin Xie, Binyan Sun, Baifu Huang, Kuncheng Luo, Haotian Yang, Weiqi Jin, Jianan Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces Lumo-1, a vision-language-action (VLA) model that integrates robot reasoning with action. It builds upon pre-trained vision-language models, extending them to embodied reasoning and action prediction through a three-stage pre-training pipeline. The model demonstrates significant improvements in embodied vision-language reasoning and generalization to novel tasks and environments.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: A Vision-Language-Action-Critic Model for Robotic Real-World Reinforcement Learning (arXiv:2509.15937)
   - **Authors**: Shaopeng Zhai, Qi Zhang, Tianyi Zhang, Fuxian Huang, Haoran Zhang, Ming Zhou, Shengzhe Zhang, Litao Liu, Sixu Lin, Jiangmiao Pang
   - **Summary**: The authors present VLAC, a vision-language-action-critic model designed to address challenges in robotic real-world reinforcement learning. VLAC generates dense progress signals and supports one-shot transfer to unseen tasks and environments, eliminating the need for task-specific reward engineering. The model achieves high success rates across various real-world manipulation tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Bi-VLA: Bilateral Control-Based Imitation Learning via Vision-Language Fusion for Action Generation (arXiv:2509.18865)
   - **Authors**: Masato Kobayashi, Thanpimon Buamanee
   - **Summary**: This work introduces Bi-VLA, a framework that extends bilateral control-based imitation learning to handle multiple tasks within a single model. By fusing robot joint data with visual features and natural language instructions, Bi-VLA improves task success rates and versatility in real-world robotic applications.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: LIV: Language-Image Representations and Rewards for Robotic Control (arXiv:2306.00958)
   - **Authors**: Yecheng Jason Ma, William Liang, Vaidehi Som, Vikash Kumar, Amy Zhang, Osbert Bastani, Dinesh Jayaraman
   - **Summary**: LIV proposes a unified objective for vision-language representation and reward learning from action-free videos with text annotations. The model assigns dense rewards to each frame in videos of unseen robots or humans attempting tasks, enhancing imitation learning and policy synthesis in robotic control.
   - **Year**: 2023

5. **Title**: OK-Robot: What Really Matters in Integrating Open-Knowledge Systems with Robotics (arXiv:2401.12202)
   - **Authors**: Mahi Shafiullah, et al.
   - **Summary**: OK-Robot integrates vision-language models with robotic primitives for navigation and grasping, enabling pick-and-drop operations without additional training. The system achieves a 58.5% success rate in open-ended tasks, highlighting the importance of nuanced integration of open-knowledge systems in robotics.
   - **Year**: 2024

6. **Title**: The Model Openness Framework: Promoting Completeness and Openness for Reproducibility, Transparency, and Usability in Artificial Intelligence (arXiv:2403.13784)
   - **Authors**: Ibrahim Haddad, et al.
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces the Model Openness Framework (MOF), a classification system that rates machine learning models based on their completeness and openness. The framework aims to enhance transparency, reproducibility, and usability in AI research and development.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Reasoning-to-Action Gap**: Bridging the disconnect between high-level reasoning capabilities of multi-modal foundation models and the fine-grained, low-level control required for embodied agents remains a significant challenge.

2. **Generalization to Unseen Environments**: Ensuring that models can adapt and perform effectively in novel and dynamic environments without extensive retraining is a persistent issue.

3. **Data Efficiency**: Developing models that can learn from limited data while maintaining high performance is crucial, especially in real-world applications where data collection is costly and time-consuming.

4. **Integration of Multi-Modal Inputs**: Effectively combining and processing diverse inputs such as vision, language, and sensory data to inform decision-making and control actions poses a complex challenge.

5. **Real-Time Processing and Adaptation**: Achieving real-time processing capabilities that allow for immediate adaptation to changing environments and tasks is essential for practical deployment of embodied AI systems.
