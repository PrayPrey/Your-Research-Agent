**Key Challenges**:

1. **Domain-Specific Representations**: Developing goal representations that are agnostic to domain-specific features remains challenging, hindering effective cross-domain transfer.

2. **Hierarchical Goal Decomposition**: Designing mechanisms to decompose complex goals into transferable sub-goals across diverse domains is complex and requires further research.

3. **Sample Efficiency**: Achieving sample-efficient learning in goal-conditioned reinforcement learning, especially when transferring policies across domains, is a significant hurdle.

4. **Inter-Level Coordination**: Ensuring effective communication and coordination between hierarchical levels in reinforcement learning architectures is essential for stable policy improvement.

5. **Representation Mismatch**: Addressing representation mismatches between source and target domains to facilitate seamless policy adaptation is a persistent challenge.
