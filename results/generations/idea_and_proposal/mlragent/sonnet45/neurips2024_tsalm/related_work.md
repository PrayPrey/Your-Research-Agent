1. **Title**: VisionTS++: Cross-Modal Time Series Foundation Model with Continual Pre-trained Visual Backbones (arXiv:2508.04379)
   - **Authors**: Lefei Shen, Mouxiang Chen, Xu Liu, Han Fu, Xiaoxue Ren, Jianling Sun, Zhuo Li, Chenghao Liu
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces VisionTS++, a time series foundation model that leverages pre-trained vision models to enhance time series forecasting. It addresses challenges in cross-modal transfer by implementing a vision-model-based filtering mechanism, a colorized multivariate conversion method, and a multi-quantile forecasting approach.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Context-Alignment: Activating and Enhancing LLM Capabilities in Time Series (arXiv:2501.03747)
   - **Authors**: Yuxiao Hu, Qian Li, Dongxiao Zhang, Jinyue Yan, Yuntian Chen
   - **Summary**: The authors propose Context-Alignment, a framework that aligns time series data with linguistic components to enable large language models (LLMs) to better understand and process time series information. This is achieved through structural and logical alignment using a Dual-Scale Context-Alignment GNNs (DSCA-GNNs) applied to time series-language multimodal inputs.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Semantic Gap Between Modalities**: Bridging the semantic gap between numerical time series data and natural language remains a significant challenge, as it requires effective methods to align and integrate these distinct data types.

2. **Limited Labeled Data**: The scarcity of labeled time series data hampers the development of robust models, necessitating the exploration of self-supervised and unsupervised learning techniques.

3. **Cross-Domain Transferability**: Ensuring that models trained on specific time series datasets generalize well across different domains is challenging due to the heterogeneity of time series data.

4. **Interpretability**: Developing models that not only perform well but also provide interpretable insights is crucial, especially in domains where understanding the reasoning behind predictions is essential.

5. **Integration of Linguistic Knowledge**: Effectively incorporating the rich world knowledge and reasoning capabilities of LLMs into time series models without extensive paired data is a complex task that requires innovative alignment strategies.
