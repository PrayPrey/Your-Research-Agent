1. **Title**: Causal pathway from AMOC to Southern Amazon rainforest indicates stabilising interaction between two climate tipping elements (arXiv:2501.14374)
   - **Authors**: Annika Högner, Giorgia Di Capua, Jonathan F. Donges, Reik V. Donner, Georg Feulner, Nico Wunderling
   - **Summary**: This study applies causal discovery methods to observational and reanalysis data from 1982-2022, identifying a stabilizing interaction pathway from the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) to the Southern Amazon Rainforest (AR). The findings suggest that AMOC weakening leads to increased dry season precipitation in the Southern AR, potentially offsetting some adverse effects of climate change.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: A Generative Adversarial Network for Climate Tipping Point Discovery (TIP-GAN) (arXiv:2302.10274)
   - **Authors**: Jennifer Sleeman, David Chung, Anand Gnanadesikan, Jay Brett, Yannis Kevrekidis, Marisa Hughes, Thomas Haine, Marie-Aude Pradal, Renske Gelderloos, Chace Ashcraft, Caroline Tang, Anshu Saksena, Larry White
   - **Summary**: The authors propose TIP-GAN, a generative adversarial network designed to explore parameter spaces of climate models to detect and understand tipping points. The model is demonstrated on the collapse of the AMOC, showing its potential to identify critical thresholds and underlying mechanisms in climate systems.
   - **Year**: 2023

3. **Title**: Probing forced responses and causal mechanisms in large-scale climate dynamics with reduced-order neural models (arXiv:2506.22552)
   - **Authors**: Fabrizio Falasca
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces reduced-order neural stochastic models to investigate forced responses and causal mechanisms in climate dynamics. By focusing on specific processes and employing response theory, the approach aims to enhance the understanding of climate feedbacks and improve the interpretability of climate models.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: Fourier Neural Operators for Arbitrary Resolution Climate Data Downscaling (arXiv:2305.14452)
   - **Authors**: Qidong Yang, Alex Hernandez-Garcia, Paula Harder, Venkatesh Ramesh, Prasanna Sattegeri, Daniela Szwarcman, Campbell D. Watson, David Rolnick
   - **Summary**: The authors present a downscaling method based on Fourier neural operators, capable of zero-shot downscaling climate data to arbitrary resolutions. This approach addresses the computational challenges of high-resolution climate simulations and offers improved accuracy over existing methods.
   - **Year**: 2023

5. **Title**: Conditional physics informed neural networks (arXiv:2104.02741)
   - **Authors**: Alexander Kovacs, Lukas Exl, Alexander Kornell, Johann Fischbacher, Markus Hovorka, Markus Gusenbauer, Leoni Breth, Harald Oezelt, Masao Yano, Noritsugu Sakuma, Akihito Kinoshita, Tetsuya Shoji, Akira Kato, Thomas Schrefl
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces conditional physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for estimating solutions to classes of eigenvalue problems. The approach incorporates physical laws into neural networks, enabling unsupervised training and efficient approximation of solutions across various scenarios.
   - **Year**: 2021

6. **Title**: Multi-Resolution Continuous Normalizing Flows (arXiv:2106.08462)
   - **Authors**: Vikram Voleti, Chris Finlay, Adam Oberman, Christopher Pal
   - **Summary**: The authors propose Multi-Resolution Continuous Normalizing Flows (MRCNF), a generative model that utilizes continuous normalizing flows for image generation at multiple resolutions. This method offers exact likelihood calculation and improved performance at higher resolutions with fewer parameters.
   - **Year**: 2021

7. **Title**: The Cost-Accuracy Trade-Off In Operator Learning With Neural Networks (arXiv:2203.13181)
   - **Authors**: Maarten V. de Hoop, Daniel Zhengyu Huang, Elizabeth Qian, Andrew M. Stuart
   - **Summary**: This study examines the cost-accuracy trade-offs in using neural networks for operator learning in the context of partial differential equations. The authors provide a numerical analysis comparing various neural network architectures, offering insights into their computational efficiency and accuracy.
   - **Year**: 2022

8. **Title**: Adaptive Deep Density Approximation for Stochastic Dynamical Systems (arXiv:2405.02810)
   - **Authors**: Junjie He, Qifeng Liao, Xiaoliang Wan
   - **Summary**: The paper presents an adaptive deep neural network approach for approximating probability density functions in stochastic dynamical systems. Utilizing the Liouville equation, the method addresses challenges such as high dimensionality and long-time integration, providing efficient solutions for complex systems.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Complexity of Climate Systems**: Climate systems involve numerous interacting components and feedback loops, making it challenging to accurately model and interpret causal relationships leading to tipping points.

2. **Data Limitations**: High-quality, high-resolution climate data are often scarce or incomplete, hindering the training and validation of complex models like neural differential equations.

3. **Model Interpretability**: While neural networks can capture complex patterns, their "black-box" nature makes it difficult to extract interpretable causal pathways, which is crucial for scientific understanding and policy-making.

4. **Computational Constraints**: Simulating climate systems at high resolutions is computationally intensive, posing challenges for real-time prediction and analysis.

5. **Validation Against Physical Laws**: Ensuring that machine learning models adhere to known physical laws and constraints is essential for their credibility but can be difficult to enforce in practice.
