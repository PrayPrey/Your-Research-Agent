1. **Title**: Explainable Verbal Reasoner Plus (EVR+): A Natural Language Reasoning Framework that Supports Diverse Compositional Reasoning (arXiv:2305.00061)
   - **Authors**: Zhengzhong Liang, Zeyu Zhang, Steven Bethard, Mihai Surdeanu
   - **Summary**: EVR+ enhances language models' compositional reasoning by enabling explicit generation and execution of symbolic operators and flexible decomposition of complex tasks into simpler ones. It supports diverse reasoning types, including nested loops and recursion, improving compositional generalization performance.
   - **Year**: 2023

2. **Title**: Select-Then-Decompose: From Empirical Analysis to Adaptive Selection Strategy for Task Decomposition in Large Language Models (arXiv:2510.17922)
   - **Authors**: Shuodi Liu, Yingzhuo Liu, Zi Wang, Yusheng Wang, Huijia Wu, Liuyu Xiang, Zhaofeng He
   - **Summary**: This study introduces a strategy that dynamically selects the most suitable decomposition approach based on task characteristics, enhancing reliability through a verification module. It achieves an optimal balance between performance and cost across multiple benchmarks.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: ExoViP: Step-by-step Verification and Exploration with Exoskeleton Modules for Compositional Visual Reasoning (arXiv:2408.02210)
   - **Authors**: Yuxuan Wang, Alan Yuille, Zhuowan Li, Zilong Zheng
   - **Summary**: ExoViP introduces verification modules as "exoskeletons" to correct errors in both planning and execution stages of compositional visual reasoning. It employs a mixture of sub-verifiers to validate predictions after each reasoning step, enhancing performance and generalization.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: CryptoX: Compositional Reasoning Evaluation of Large Language Models (arXiv:2502.07813)
   - **Authors**: Jiajun Shi, Chaoren Wei, Liqun Yang, Zekun Moore Wang, Chenghao Yang, Ge Zhang, Stephen Huang, Tao Peng, Jian Yang, Zhoufutu Wen
   - **Summary**: CryptoX introduces an evaluation framework combining existing benchmarks and cryptographic principles to quantify the compositional reasoning capacity of LLMs. It highlights the need to enhance LLMs' compositional reasoning capabilities.
   - **Year**: 2025

5. **Title**: Compositional Causal Reasoning Evaluation in Language Models (arXiv:2503.04556)
   - **Authors**: Jacqueline Maasch, Alihan Hüyük, Xinnuo Xu, Aditya V. Nori, Javier Gonzalez
   - **Summary**: This work presents an evaluation framework for compositional causal reasoning in language models, introducing a taxonomy for reasoning errors and providing insights into model performance on causal reasoning tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

6. **Title**: Towards System 2 Reasoning in LLMs: Learning How to Think With Meta Chain-of-Thought (arXiv:2501.04682)
   - **Authors**: Amrith Setlur, Chirag Nagpal, Adam Fisch, Xinyang Geng, Jacob Eisenstein, Rishabh Agarwal, Alekh Agarwal, Jonathan Berant, Aviral Kumar
   - **Summary**: This paper explores methods to imbue LLMs with System-2 reasoning capabilities, focusing on meta-learning approaches to enhance reasoning processes and improve generalization.
   - **Year**: 2025

7. **Title**: AI Reasoning in Deep Learning Era: From Symbolic AI to Neural–Symbolic AI (arXiv:2505.20313)
   - **Authors**: Baoyu Liang, Yuchen Wang, Chao Tong
   - **Summary**: The authors discuss the integration of symbolic reasoning with neural networks, highlighting the benefits of neural–symbolic AI in terms of interpretability, robustness, and generalizability.
   - **Year**: 2025

8. **Title**: Reasoning in Large Language Models: From Chain-of-Thought to Massively Decomposed Agentic Processes (arXiv:202512.2242)
   - **Authors**: Yiming Lei, Jiawei Xu, Chia Xin Liang, Ziqian Bi, Xiaoming Li, Danyang Zhang, Junhao Song, Zhenyu Yu
   - **Summary**: This survey synthesizes empirical findings on LLM reasoning, identifying challenges such as compositional generalization and error propagation, and discusses pathways toward solving complex reasoning tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

9. **Title**: Improved Logical Reasoning of Language Models via Differentiable Symbolic Programming (arXiv:2305.03742)
   - **Authors**: Hanlin Zhang, Jiani Huang, Ziyang Li, Mayur Naik, Eric Xing
   - **Summary**: The paper proposes a framework combining language models with differentiable symbolic reasoning, enhancing logical reasoning abilities and achieving significant accuracy improvements on deductive reasoning benchmarks.
   - **Year**: 2023

10. **Title**: Reasoning in Neurosymbolic AI (arXiv:2505.20313)
    - **Authors**: Aniruddha Chattopadhyay, Raj Dandekar, Kaushik Roy
    - **Summary**: This chapter describes an energy-based neurosymbolic AI system capable of representing and reasoning about propositional logic formulas, combining learning from data with logical reasoning.
    - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Compositional Generalization**: Ensuring models can generalize to novel combinations of known components remains a significant hurdle.

2. **Error Propagation**: Managing and mitigating errors that occur during multi-step reasoning processes is critical for maintaining accuracy.

3. **Interpretability**: Developing models whose reasoning processes are transparent and understandable to humans is essential for trust and verification.

4. **Integration of Symbolic and Neural Approaches**: Effectively combining symbolic reasoning with neural networks to leverage the strengths of both paradigms poses technical challenges.

5. **Benchmarking and Evaluation**: Creating robust benchmarks that accurately assess reasoning capabilities without data contamination is necessary for measuring progress.
