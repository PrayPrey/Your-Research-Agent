**1. Related Papers**

1. **Title**: Diffeomorphism Neural Operator for Various Domains and Parameters of Partial Differential Equations (arXiv:2402.12475)
   - **Authors**: Zhiwei Zhao, Changqing Liu, Yingguang Li, Zhibin Chen, Xu Liu
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces the Diffeomorphism Neural Operator (DNO), a framework designed to solve PDEs across various domains and parameters. By mapping diverse physical domains to a generic domain through diffeomorphisms, the DNO effectively learns operators on this unified space, demonstrating robust generalization across different geometries and conditions.
   - **Year**: 2024

2. **Title**: DyMixOp: Guiding Neural Operator Design for PDEs from a Complex Dynamics Perspective with Local-Global-Mixing (arXiv:2508.13490)
   - **Authors**: Pengyu Lai, Yixiao Chen, Hui Xu
   - **Summary**: DyMixOp presents a neural operator framework that integrates insights from complex dynamical systems, particularly inertial manifold theory. The Local-Global-Mixing transformation captures both fine-scale details and nonlinear interactions, addressing spectral bias and enhancing physical interpretability in modeling PDEs.
   - **Year**: 2025

**2. Key Challenges**

1. **Symmetry Preservation**: Ensuring that neural network architectures inherently respect physical symmetries (e.g., translation, rotation, scaling) to produce physically plausible and generalizable models.

2. **Topological Constraints**: Incorporating topological regularization to maintain invariant topological features, such as conservation laws and phase space structures, within learned models.

3. **Generalization Across Domains**: Developing models that can generalize across various domains and parameters, especially when dealing with complex and diverse physical systems.

4. **Computational Efficiency**: Balancing the computational cost and accuracy in operator learning, particularly when modeling high-dimensional and nonlinear dynamical systems.

5. **Integration of Theoretical Insights**: Effectively integrating insights from complex dynamical systems, such as inertial manifold theory, into neural operator frameworks to enhance physical interpretability and performance.
