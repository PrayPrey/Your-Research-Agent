1. **Title**: IB-UQ: Information Bottleneck Based Uncertainty Quantification for Neural Function Regression and Neural Operator Learning (arXiv:2302.03271)
   - **Authors**: Ling Guo, Hao Wu, Wenwen Zhou, Yan Wang, Tao Zhou
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces IB-UQ, a framework that employs the information bottleneck principle for uncertainty quantification in scientific machine learning tasks, including deep neural network regression and neural operator learning. The approach integrates a confidence-aware encoder and a Gaussian decoder to predict means and variances of outputs, enhancing the quantification of extrapolation uncertainty.
   - **Year**: 2023

2. **Title**: EQ-CBM: A Probabilistic Concept Bottleneck with Energy-Based Models and Quantized Vectors (arXiv:2409.14630)
   - **Authors**: Sangwon Kim, Dasom Ahn, Byoung Chul Ko, In-su Jang, Kwang-Ju Kim
   - **Summary**: EQ-CBM proposes a novel framework that enhances concept bottleneck models by incorporating probabilistic concept encoding using energy-based models with quantized concept activation vectors. This method effectively captures uncertainties, improving prediction reliability and accuracy, and facilitates higher levels of human intervention.
   - **Year**: 2024

3. **Title**: Causally Reliable Concept Bottleneck Models (arXiv:2503.04363)
   - **Authors**: Giovanni De Felice, Arianna Casanova Flores, Francesco De Santis, Silvia Santini, Johannes Schneider, Pietro Barbiero, Alberto Termine
   - **Summary**: This work introduces Causally Reliable Concept Bottleneck Models (C²BMs), which enforce reasoning through a bottleneck of concepts structured according to a model of real-world causal mechanisms. The approach aims to improve interpretability, causal reliability, and responsiveness to interventions while maintaining accuracy.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: Conformal Uncertainty Quantification to Evaluate Predictive Fairness of Foundation AI Model for Skin Lesion Classes Across Patient Demographics (arXiv:2503.23819)
   - **Authors**: Swarnava Bhattacharyya, Umapada Pal, Tapabrata Chakraborti
   - **Summary**: This study applies conformal analysis to quantify the predictive uncertainty of a vision transformer-based foundation model across patient demographics for skin lesion classification. The method provides coverage guarantees at the population level and individual uncertainty scores, advancing the trustworthiness and fairness of clinical AI.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Scalability of Concept Bottleneck Models**: Traditional concept bottleneck models often struggle to scale effectively when applied to large foundation models, limiting their applicability in complex tasks.

2. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Accurately quantifying uncertainty in both concept detection and final predictions remains a significant challenge, particularly in high-stakes applications where understanding model confidence is crucial.

3. **Integration with Foundation Models**: Incorporating interpretable components like concept bottleneck layers into existing foundation models without compromising performance or interpretability is complex and requires careful design.

4. **Human Intervention Mechanisms**: Developing effective methods for human experts to intervene and correct uncertain concepts during inference, guided by uncertainty scores, is essential but challenging to implement.

5. **Balancing Interpretability and Accuracy**: Ensuring that the addition of interpretable layers and uncertainty quantification mechanisms does not adversely affect the overall accuracy and efficiency of the model is a delicate balance to achieve.
