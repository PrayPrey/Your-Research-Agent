**2. Key Challenges**

1. **Data Heterogeneity**: Variations in data quality, formats, and distributions across different sources complicate the integration and analysis processes in federated learning systems.

2. **Privacy Preservation**: Ensuring robust privacy measures while maintaining model performance is challenging, especially when dealing with sensitive health data across multiple jurisdictions.

3. **Model Interpretability**: Balancing the complexity of federated learning models with the need for interpretability is crucial for gaining trust and facilitating decision-making in healthcare applications.

4. **Communication Efficiency**: Efficient communication protocols are necessary to manage the exchange of model updates without overwhelming network resources, particularly in resource-constrained environments.

5. **Real-Time Adaptation**: Developing federated learning systems capable of rapid adaptation to evolving data patterns during a pandemic is essential for timely and effective surveillance.
