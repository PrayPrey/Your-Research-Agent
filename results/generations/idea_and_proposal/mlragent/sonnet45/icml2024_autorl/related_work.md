1. **Title**: IRepair: An Intent-Aware Approach to Repair Data-Driven Errors in Large Language Models (arXiv:2502.07072)
   - **Authors**: Sayem Mohammad Imtiaz, Astha Singh, Fraol Batole, Hridesh Rajan
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces IRepair, a dynamic slicing-based intent-aware repair strategy for large language models (LLMs). By selectively targeting the most error-prone sections of the model, IRepair effectively mitigates data-driven errors such as biases and toxicity, enhancing model reliability without compromising overall performance.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: An Intelligent Fault Self-Healing Mechanism for Cloud AI Systems via Integration of Large Language Models and Deep Reinforcement Learning (arXiv:2506.07411)
   - **Authors**: Ze Yang, Yihong Jin, Juntian Liu, Xinhe Xu
   - **Summary**: The authors propose an Intelligent Fault Self-Healing Mechanism (IFSHM) that integrates LLMs with deep reinforcement learning to detect and adaptively recover from system faults in cloud AI systems. The framework enhances service reliability by dynamically extracting contextual semantics from system logs and optimizing recovery strategies through reinforcement learning.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Boosting Open-Source LLMs for Program Repair via Reasoning Transfer and LLM-Guided Reinforcement Learning (arXiv:2506.03921)
   - **Authors**: Xunzhu Tang, Jacques Klein, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé
   - **Summary**: This study presents Repairity, a methodology that enhances open-source LLMs' program repair capabilities by transferring reasoning knowledge from closed-source models and employing LLM-guided reinforcement learning. The approach significantly narrows the performance gap between open-source and closed-source models in program repair tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: MASteer: Multi-Agent Adaptive Steer Strategy for End-to-End LLM Trustworthiness Repair (arXiv:2508.06963)
   - **Authors**: Changqing Li, Tianlin Li, Xiaohan Zhang, Aishan Liu, Li Pan
   - **Summary**: MASteer introduces an end-to-end framework for enhancing LLM trustworthiness through representation engineering. It features AutoTester, a multi-agent system generating diverse steer samples, and AutoRepairer, which constructs adaptive steering strategies for context-aware inference, improving model reliability and generalization.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Complexity of Failure Diagnosis**: Identifying the root causes of RL training failures requires analyzing intricate interactions between various components, making automated diagnosis challenging.

2. **Dynamic Hyperparameter Tuning**: Hyperparameter landscapes in RL are non-static, necessitating adaptive tuning strategies to maintain optimal performance throughout training.

3. **Integration of LLMs with RL**: Effectively combining large language models with reinforcement learning systems poses challenges in ensuring seamless interaction and leveraging the strengths of both paradigms.

4. **Overfitting Risks**: Aggressive hyperparameter optimization can lead to overfitting, where models perform well on training data but fail to generalize to unseen scenarios.

5. **Interpretability of Automated Decisions**: Ensuring that automated failure diagnoses and hyperparameter adjustments are interpretable to practitioners is crucial for trust and adoption of AutoRL systems.
