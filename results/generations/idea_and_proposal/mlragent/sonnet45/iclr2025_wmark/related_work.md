3. **Title**: MT-Mark: Rethinking Image Watermarking via Mutual-Teacher Collaboration with Adaptive Feature Modulation (arXiv:2512.19438)
   - **Authors**: Fei Ge, Ying Huang, Jie Liu, Guixuan Zhang, Zhi Zeng, Shuwu Zhang, Hu Guan
   - **Summary**: MT-Mark introduces a collaborative interaction mechanism between the embedder and extractor in image watermarking, enabling mutual-teacher training. This approach enhances robustness and generalization by aligning embedding behavior with extraction objectives, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in extraction accuracy and perceptual quality.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Complex Real-World Transformations**: Existing watermarking methods often fail to withstand complex, cascaded transformations that content undergoes in real-world scenarios, such as those introduced by social media platforms and editing tools.

2. **Adversarial Attacks**: Watermarks are susceptible to adversarial manipulations designed to evade detection or remove the watermark without significant degradation of the content.

3. **Balancing Robustness and Imperceptibility**: Achieving a balance between embedding a robust watermark and maintaining the imperceptibility of the original content remains a significant challenge.

4. **Lack of Standardized Benchmarks**: The absence of comprehensive and standardized benchmarking frameworks hinders the consistent evaluation and comparison of watermarking techniques under real-world conditions.

5. **Cross-Modal and Cross-Lingual Robustness**: Ensuring that watermarks remain effective across different modalities (e.g., text, image, audio) and languages, especially under various manipulations, is a complex and unresolved issue.
