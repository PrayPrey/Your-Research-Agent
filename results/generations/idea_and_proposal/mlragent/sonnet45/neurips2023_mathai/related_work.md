**Key Challenges**:

1. **Defining Proof Complexity Metrics**: Establishing computational measures of proof complexity, such as proof depth and lemma applications, is challenging due to the abstract nature of mathematical reasoning.

2. **Adaptive Curriculum Design**: Creating dynamic curricula that adjust problem difficulty based on model performance requires sophisticated algorithms and continuous monitoring.

3. **Preventing Catastrophic Forgetting**: Ensuring that models retain previously learned concepts while acquiring new ones is a significant challenge in curriculum learning.

4. **Generalization Across Difficulty Levels**: Developing models that generalize effectively across varying levels of problem complexity remains a complex task.

5. **Sample Efficiency**: Enhancing the efficiency of training processes to reduce computational resources and time without compromising performance is a persistent challenge.
