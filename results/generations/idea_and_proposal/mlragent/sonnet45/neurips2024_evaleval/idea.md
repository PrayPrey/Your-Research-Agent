# Research Idea: Participatory Evaluation Toolkit for Generative AI Impact Assessment

## Title
**PETAL: Participatory Evaluation Toolkit for Assessing Local-to-Global Impacts of Generative AI**

## Motivation
Current generative AI evaluations are dominated by ML experts using technical metrics, missing critical insights from affected communities. Different stakeholder groups (artists, journalists, educators, content moderators) experience distinct impacts that technical evaluations overlook. Without structured frameworks for inclusive participation, evaluations remain incomplete and fail to capture real-world harms or benefits, leading to misaligned AI development and inadequate policy responses.

## Main Idea
Develop PETAL, a modular, open-source toolkit enabling structured participatory evaluation of generative AI systems across diverse stakeholder groups. The framework includes:

1. **Stakeholder Mapping Protocol**: Systematic methodology to identify affected communities and their evaluation priorities
2. **Multi-Modal Feedback Mechanisms**: Interfaces allowing non-technical participants to contribute qualitative and quantitative assessments (surveys, focus groups, experience sampling)
3. **Impact Translation Layer**: Methods to synthesize diverse stakeholder inputs into actionable technical and policy recommendations
4. **Standardized Documentation Schema**: Templates for reporting who participated, how concerns were weighted, and what trade-offs were made

Expected outcomes include validated case studies across 3-5 generative AI applications, demonstrating how participatory evaluations reveal blind spots in expert-only assessments. This would establish reproducible best practices and provide evidence for policymakers on investment priorities in inclusive evaluation infrastructure.
