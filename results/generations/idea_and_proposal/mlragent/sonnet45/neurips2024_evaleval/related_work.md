**Key Challenges:**

1. **Limited Stakeholder Inclusion**: Current AI evaluations often lack input from diverse stakeholder groups, leading to assessments that may not fully capture the real-world impacts of AI systems.

2. **Lack of Standardized Evaluation Frameworks**: The absence of standardized documentation and evaluation frameworks hinders transparency, reproducibility, and comparability across AI evaluations.

3. **Difficulty in Translating Qualitative Feedback into Actionable Insights**: Synthesizing qualitative feedback from non-technical participants into technical and policy recommendations remains a significant challenge.

4. **Addressing Sociotechnical Factors**: Evaluations often focus on technical aspects, neglecting the broader sociotechnical context, which is crucial for understanding the full impact of AI systems.

5. **Ensuring Ethical Participation**: Engaging marginalized communities in AI evaluations raises ethical concerns, including potential exploitation and unintended harms, necessitating careful consideration and mitigation strategies.
