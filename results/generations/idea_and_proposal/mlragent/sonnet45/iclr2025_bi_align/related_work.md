1. **Title**: In Pursuit of Predictive Models of Human Preferences Toward AI Teammates (arXiv:2503.15516)
   - **Authors**: Ho Chit Siu, Jaime D. Peña, Yutai Zhou, Ross E. Allen
   - **Summary**: This study evaluates AI agents in the cooperative card game Hanabi, correlating objective metrics with human subjective preferences. Findings indicate that traditional performance metrics are less predictive of human preferences than measures like AI action diversity and strategic dominance.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Co-Alignment: Rethinking Alignment as Bidirectional Human-AI Cognitive Adaptation (arXiv:2509.12179)
   - **Authors**: Yubo Li, Weiyi Song
   - **Summary**: The paper introduces Bidirectional Cognitive Alignment (BiCA), a framework where humans and AI mutually adapt through learnable protocols and representation mapping. In collaborative navigation tasks, BiCA achieved higher success rates and improved mutual adaptation compared to baselines.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Cooperation Through Indirect Reciprocity in Child-Robot Interactions (arXiv:2512.20621)
   - **Authors**: Isabel Neto, Alexandre S. Pires, Filipa Correia, Fernando P. Santos
   - **Summary**: This research explores indirect reciprocity in child-robot interactions, demonstrating that children and robots can sustain cooperation through reputation-based mechanisms. The study highlights the potential for AI systems to learn cooperative behaviors from human strategies.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: On the Algorithmic Bias of Aligning Large Language Models with RLHF: Preference Collapse and Matching Regularization (arXiv:2405.16455)
   - **Authors**: Jiancong Xiao, Ziniu Li, Xingyu Xie, Emily Getzen, Cong Fang, Qi Long, Weijie J. Su
   - **Summary**: The authors discuss the inherent algorithmic bias in Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), introducing a novel approach called preference matching RLHF to mitigate issues like preference collapse and improve alignment with human preferences.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Dynamic Preference Modeling**: Capturing and adapting to the evolving nature of human preferences over time remains a significant challenge, as static models may fail to reflect changes in user values and contexts.

2. **Bidirectional Adaptation Mechanisms**: Developing effective frameworks for mutual adaptation between humans and AI systems is complex, requiring mechanisms that facilitate reciprocal learning and adjustment.

3. **Balancing Performance and Alignment**: Ensuring that AI systems achieve high performance while aligning with human preferences and values can be difficult, as optimizing for one aspect may compromise the other.

4. **Addressing Algorithmic Bias**: Mitigating biases inherent in reinforcement learning from human feedback is crucial to prevent issues like preference collapse and to ensure fair representation of diverse human values.

5. **Scalability of Human Feedback Integration**: Integrating human feedback at scale poses challenges in terms of resource requirements and consistency, necessitating efficient methods to incorporate human inputs into AI training processes.
