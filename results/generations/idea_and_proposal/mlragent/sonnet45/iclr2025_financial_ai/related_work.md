1. **Title**: MARS: A Meta-Adaptive Reinforcement Learning Framework for Risk-Aware Multi-Agent Portfolio Management (arXiv:2508.01173)

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Effectively quantifying both epistemic and aleatoric uncertainties in multi-agent reinforcement learning systems remains a significant challenge, impacting the reliability of trading decisions.

2. **Dynamic Market Adaptation**: Developing models that can swiftly adapt to rapidly changing market conditions and unforeseen events is crucial for maintaining performance and mitigating risks.

3. **Risk-Return Trade-off**: Balancing the pursuit of high returns with risk management strategies is complex, especially in volatile markets where overconfidence can lead to substantial losses.

4. **Inter-Agent Coordination**: Ensuring effective coordination among multiple agents with diverse strategies and risk profiles is essential to prevent conflicting actions and optimize overall portfolio performance.

5. **Interpretability and Transparency**: Providing clear and interpretable insights into the decision-making processes of reinforcement learning models is vital for gaining trust and facilitating informed investment decisions.
