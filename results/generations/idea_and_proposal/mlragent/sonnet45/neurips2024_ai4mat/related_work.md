Here is a literature review on the topic of "Physics-Informed Multimodal Fusion Networks for Incomplete Materials Characterization Data," focusing on related works published between 2023 and 2025.

**1. Related Papers**

1. **Title**: A Multi-Agent Framework Integrating Large Language Models and Generative AI for Accelerated Metamaterial Design (arXiv:2503.19889)
   - **Authors**: Jie Tian, Martin Taylor Sobczak, Dhanush Patil, Jixin Hou, Lin Pang, Arunachalam Ramanathan, Libin Yang, Xianyan Chen, Yuval Golan, Hongyue Sun, Kenan Song, Xianqiao Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces CrossMatAgent, a multi-agent framework that combines large language models with generative AI to automate metamaterial design. The system utilizes hierarchical agents specializing in tasks like pattern analysis and architectural synthesis, leveraging multimodal reasoning to produce simulation- and 3D printing-ready metamaterial patterns.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Autonomous Inorganic Materials Discovery via Multi-Agent Physics-Aware Scientific Reasoning (arXiv:2508.02956)
   - **Authors**: Alireza Ghafarollahi, Markus J. Buehler
   - **Summary**: SparksMatter is presented as a multi-agent AI model designed for automated inorganic materials design. It autonomously executes the full materials discovery cycle, from ideation to experimentation, generating novel stable inorganic structures that meet target objectives through continuous evaluation and refinement.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Aethorix v1.0: AI-Driven Inverse Design of Inorganic Materials for Scalable Industrial Innovation (arXiv:2506.16609)
   - **Authors**: Yingjie Shi, Runtian Miao
   - **Summary**: Aethorix v1.0 integrates large language models, diffusion-based generative models, and machine-learned interatomic potentials to enhance the materials development cycle. It focuses on designing inorganic crystals and predicting properties with high accuracy, validated through real industrial use cases.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: AlphaMat: A Material Informatics Hub Connecting Data, Features, Models and Applications (arXiv:2303.11651)
   - **Authors**: Zhilong Wang, Junfei Cai, An Chen, Yanqiang Han, Kehao Tao, Simin Ye, Shiwei Wang, Imran Ali, Jinjin Li
   - **Summary**: AlphaMat is an AI platform that connects materials data with applications, capable of designing structural and component descriptors effective for various AI models. It has successfully discovered thousands of new materials in fields like photonics and catalysis.
   - **Year**: 2023

**2. Key Challenges**

1. **Handling Incomplete and Multimodal Data**: Materials characterization often results in datasets with missing modalities due to experimental constraints. Developing models that can effectively utilize incomplete, multimodal data remains a significant challenge.

2. **Integrating Physics-Based Constraints**: Incorporating known physical laws and constraints into AI models to ensure physically plausible predictions is complex and requires careful model design.

3. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Accurately quantifying the uncertainty in predictions, especially when dealing with incomplete data, is crucial for reliable materials design but remains difficult to achieve.

4. **Active Learning for Experimental Design**: Developing active learning strategies that can suggest the most informative experiments to reduce uncertainty and guide materials discovery is an ongoing challenge.

5. **Scalability and Generalization**: Ensuring that models are scalable and can generalize across different materials systems and characterization techniques is essential for practical applications but is challenging due to the diversity and complexity of materials data.
