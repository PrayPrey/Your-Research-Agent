# Title: Adaptive LLM-Based Conversational Agents for Early Detection of Childhood Anxiety Disorders

## Motivation
Childhood anxiety disorders affect approximately 7% of children globally, yet remain severely underdiagnosed due to limited access to mental health professionals, especially in low-resource settings. Children often struggle to articulate their emotional states in clinical settings, leading to delayed interventions. Current screening tools rely on standardized questionnaires that fail to capture the natural variability in children's expression patterns. An adaptive, conversational AI approach could provide accessible, engaging, and developmentally-appropriate screening while reducing the stigma associated with traditional assessments.

## Main Idea
We propose developing a child-friendly LLM-based conversational agent that conducts naturalistic dialogues through age-appropriate storytelling and role-playing scenarios to detect early signs of anxiety disorders. The system will:

1. **Methodology**: Fine-tune foundation models on child-psychologist conversation transcripts, incorporating developmental psychology principles to adapt language complexity based on age (5-12 years). The agent will present interactive narrative scenarios (e.g., "What would the character do if...") and analyze response patterns, hesitation markers, and emotional vocabulary.

2. **Multi-modal Analysis**: Integrate speech prosody and response latency features alongside text to improve detection accuracy.

3. **Expected Outcomes**: A validated screening tool achieving comparable sensitivity to clinician assessments, deployable via tablets in schools and primary care settings.

**Impact**: Democratize early mental health screening, enabling timely referrals and reducing long-term developmental consequences.
