1. **Title**: Chunked Data Shapley: A Scalable Dataset Quality Assessment for Machine Learning (arXiv:2508.16255)
   - **Authors**: Andreas Loizou, Dimitrios Tsoumakos
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces Chunked Data Shapley (C-DaSh), a method designed to assess dataset quality by estimating the contribution of data chunks using optimized subset selection and single-iteration stochastic gradient descent. C-DaSh significantly reduces computation time while maintaining accuracy in detecting low-quality data regions, making it practical for large-scale datasets.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Scalability of Data Quality Assessment**: Existing methods for evaluating dataset quality, such as Data Shapley, are computationally intensive and struggle to scale with large datasets. Developing efficient and scalable assessment techniques remains a significant challenge.

2. **Standardization of Quality Metrics**: The lack of standardized, measurable methods for evaluating data quality leads to inconsistencies in dataset assessments. Establishing universally accepted metrics is crucial for consistent evaluation.

3. **Automated Monitoring of Dataset Health**: Implementing automated systems to continuously monitor various health dimensions of datasets, including usage patterns, freshness, and ethical considerations, is complex and requires integration of multiple monitoring tools.

4. **Addressing Ethical and Bias Concerns**: Datasets may contain biases or become ethically problematic over time. Developing methods to detect and mitigate these issues is essential to maintain the integrity of ML models.

5. **Maintainer Engagement and Responsiveness**: Ensuring that dataset maintainers are actively engaged in addressing issues, updating documentation, and responding to user concerns is vital for dataset sustainability but can be challenging to enforce.
