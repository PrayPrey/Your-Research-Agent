   - **Authors**: Suzie Kim, Hye-Bin Shin, Seong-Whan Lee

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Ambiguity and Context-Dependence of Implicit Signals**: Implicit human feedback, such as facial expressions and gestures, can be ambiguous and context-dependent, making it challenging for agents to accurately interpret and respond to these signals.

2. **Lack of Predefined Reward Mappings**: Without predefined mappings between implicit signals and rewards, agents must autonomously discover and learn these associations, which can be complex and time-consuming.

3. **Variability in Human Communication Styles**: Individual differences in expressing implicit feedback necessitate personalized learning approaches, complicating the development of generalized models.

4. **Integration of Multimodal Feedback**: Effectively combining various forms of implicit feedback (e.g., facial expressions, gaze, speech tone) into a coherent reward signal poses significant technical challenges.

5. **Scalability and Real-Time Processing**: Developing systems that can process and learn from implicit feedback in real-time, while scaling to diverse tasks and environments, remains a substantial hurdle.
