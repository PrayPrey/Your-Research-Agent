1. **Title**: GraphAD: Interaction Scene Graph for End-to-end Autonomous Driving (arXiv:2403.19098)
   - **Authors**: Yunpeng Zhang, Deheng Qian, Ding Li, Yifeng Pan, Yong Chen, Zhenbao Liang, Zhiyao Zhang, Shurui Zhang, Hongxu Li, Maolei Fu, Yun Ye, Zhujin Liang, Yi Shan, Dalong Du
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces the Interaction Scene Graph (ISG) to model interactions among the ego-vehicle, road agents, and map elements. By focusing on essential connections, the ISG enhances efficiency and performance in perception, prediction, and planning tasks within end-to-end autonomous driving systems.
   - **Year**: 2024

2. **Title**: SUPER-AD: Semantic Uncertainty-aware Planning for End-to-End Robust Autonomous Driving (arXiv:2511.22865)
   - **Authors**: Wonjeong Ryu, Seungjun Yu, Seokha Moon, Hojun Choi, Junsung Park, Jinkyu Kim, Hyunjung Shim
   - **Summary**: The authors propose a camera-only end-to-end framework that estimates aleatoric uncertainty in bird's-eye view space and incorporates it into planning. The method produces an uncertainty-aware drivability map and introduces lane-following regularization to promote safe and rule-compliant behavior, achieving state-of-the-art performance on the NAVSIM benchmark.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: GEMINUS: Dual-aware Global and Scene-Adaptive Mixture-of-Experts for End-to-End Autonomous Driving (arXiv:2507.14456)
   - **Authors**: Chi Wan, Yixin Cui, Jiatong Du, Shuo Yang, Yulong Bai, Peng Yi, Nan Li, Yanjun Huang
   - **Summary**: GEMINUS presents a Mixture-of-Experts framework featuring a Global Expert and a Scene-Adaptive Experts Group, coordinated by a Dual-aware Router. This design enables adaptability and robustness across diverse driving scenarios, outperforming existing methods in the Bench2Drive closed-loop benchmark.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Effectively modeling and propagating uncertainty through neural networks to inform decision-making remains a significant challenge.

2. **Interpretability vs. Performance Trade-off**: Balancing the need for interpretable models with the requirement for high-performance, end-to-end trainable systems is complex.

3. **Dynamic Interaction Modeling**: Accurately capturing and updating the dynamic interactions among multiple agents and environmental elements in real-time is difficult.

4. **Generalization Across Scenarios**: Ensuring that models generalize well across diverse and unseen driving scenarios without extensive retraining is a persistent issue.

5. **Safety and Regulatory Compliance**: Developing systems that not only perform well but also meet safety standards and regulatory requirements for real-world deployment is a critical challenge.
