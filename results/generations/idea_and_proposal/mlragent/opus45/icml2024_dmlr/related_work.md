1. **Title**: Temp-SCONE: A Novel Out-of-Distribution Detection and Domain Generalization Framework for Wild Data with Temporal Shift (arXiv:2512.04571)
   - **Authors**: Aditi Naiknaware, Sanchit Singh, Hajar Homayouni, Salimeh Sekeh
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces Temp-SCONE, an extension of the SCONE framework, designed to handle temporal shifts in dynamic environments. It incorporates a confidence-driven regularization loss to penalize instability in predictions over time, enhancing robustness under temporal drift.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: CALM: A Framework for Continuous, Adaptive, and LLM-Mediated Anomaly Detection in Time-Series Streams (arXiv:2508.21273)
   - **Authors**: Ashok Devireddy, Shunping Huang
   - **Summary**: CALM is an end-to-end framework for real-time anomaly detection in non-stationary time-series streams. It features a closed-loop, continuous fine-tuning mechanism and utilizes a Large Language Model to provide context-aware judgments on detected anomalies, ensuring adaptability to evolving data patterns.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: TimeDiT: General-purpose Diffusion Transformers for Time Series Foundation Model (arXiv:2409.02322)
   - **Authors**: Defu Cao, Wen Ye, Yizhou Zhang, Yan Liu
   - **Summary**: TimeDiT combines transformer-based temporal dependency learning with diffusion-based probabilistic sampling to address challenges in time series data. It employs a unified masking mechanism and introduces a model editing strategy for flexible integration of external knowledge during sampling.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: WormKAN: Are KAN Effective for Identifying and Tracking Concept Drift in Time Series? (arXiv:2410.10041)
   - **Authors**: Kunpeng Xu, Lifei Chen, Shengrui Wang
   - **Summary**: WormKAN is a KAN-based model designed to detect and track concept drift in co-evolving time series. It processes time series into patches, learns robust latent representations, and identifies dynamic transitions through concept identification and drift detection.
   - **Year**: 2024

5. **Title**: Set the Clock: Temporal Alignment of Pretrained Language Models (arXiv:2402.16797)
   - **Authors**: Bowen Zhao, Zander Brumbaugh, Yizhong Wang, Hannaneh Hajishirzi, Noah A. Smith

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Temporal Drift Detection**: Accurately identifying and quantifying temporal drift in large-scale datasets remains challenging due to the subtle and complex nature of data evolution over time.

2. **Efficient Model Updating**: Developing methods for updating foundation models with minimal data intervention, such as targeted fine-tuning, to maintain model freshness without the need for full retraining.

3. **Data Quality Assessment**: Establishing robust and automated quality signals to detect outdated or deprecated content within training data, ensuring the relevance and accuracy of the information used for model training.

4. **Resource Constraints**: Balancing the computational and financial costs associated with continuous monitoring and updating of models, especially in resource-limited environments.

5. **Benchmark Development**: Creating standardized benchmarks and toolkits for temporal data quality assessment to facilitate the evaluation and comparison of different approaches in handling data drift.
