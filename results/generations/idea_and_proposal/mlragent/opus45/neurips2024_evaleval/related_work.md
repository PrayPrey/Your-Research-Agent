10. **Title**: Standardizing Stakeholder Engagement in AI Evaluation: Challenges and Opportunities
    - **Authors**: [Authors not specified]
    - **Summary**: The paper discusses the challenges and opportunities in standardizing stakeholder engagement in AI evaluations, proposing methods to systematically incorporate diverse perspectives.
    - **Year**: 2023

**Key Challenges**:

1. **Diverse Stakeholder Identification**: Effectively mapping and categorizing the wide range of stakeholders affected by generative AI systems to ensure comprehensive evaluation.

2. **Standardization of Evaluation Protocols**: Developing adaptable yet standardized evaluation templates that can be utilized across various contexts and by non-ML experts.

3. **Quantifying Qualitative Feedback**: Creating robust methods to translate qualitative stakeholder feedback into quantifiable impact indicators that complement technical benchmarks.

4. **Ensuring Inclusivity and Representation**: Addressing potential biases by ensuring that marginalized and vulnerable communities are adequately represented in the evaluation process.

5. **Balancing Technical and Experiential Metrics**: Harmonizing technical performance metrics with lived experiences to provide a holistic assessment of generative AI impacts.
