1. **Title**: Emergent Bias and Fairness in Multi-Agent Decision Systems (arXiv:2512.16433)
   - **Authors**: Maeve Madigan, Parameswaran Kamalaruban, Glenn Moynihan, Tom Kempton, David Sutton, Stuart Burrell
   - **Summary**: This paper examines fairness evaluation methodologies for multi-agent predictive systems in the financial domain. Through large-scale simulations, the authors reveal patterns of emergent bias in financial decision-making that cannot be traced to individual agents, indicating that multi-agent systems may exhibit collective behaviors leading to bias.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Structured Debate Improves Corporate Credit Reasoning in Financial AI (arXiv:2510.17108)
   - **Authors**: Yoonjin Lee, Munhee Kim, Hanbi Choi, Juhyeon Park, Seungho Lyoo, Woojin Park
   - **Summary**: The authors develop and evaluate two large language model-based systems for generating structured reasoning from non-financial evidence in corporate credit assessment. They introduce a debate-based multi-agent system that operationalizes adversarial verification through a structured interaction protocol, demonstrating superior reasoning quality and productivity gains compared to manual expert reporting.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: MARS: A Meta-Adaptive Reinforcement Learning Framework for Risk-Aware Multi-Agent Portfolio Management (arXiv:2508.01173)
   - **Authors**: Jiayi Chen, Jing Li, Guiling Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper proposes MARS, a novel reinforcement learning framework employing a heterogeneous agent ensemble with unique risk profiles. A high-level meta-adaptive controller orchestrates the ensemble to dynamically adjust reliance on conservative versus aggressive agents, effectively balancing risk and return in portfolio management.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: FinDebate: Multi-Agent Collaborative Intelligence for Financial Analysis (arXiv:2509.17395)
   - **Authors**: Tianshi Cai, Guanxu Li, Nijia Han, Ce Huang, Zimu Wang, Changyu Zeng, Yuqi Wang, Jingshi Zhou, Haiyang Zhang, Qi Chen, Yushan Pan, Shuihua Wang, Wei Wang
   - **Summary**: FinDebate introduces a multi-agent framework integrating collaborative debate with domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation. Specialized agents covering various financial aspects run in parallel to synthesize evidence into multi-dimensional insights, employing a safe debate protocol to challenge and refine conclusions, resulting in high-quality analysis with calibrated confidence levels.
   - **Year**: 2025

5. **Title**: Learning to Simulate Self-Driven Particles System with Coordinated Policy Optimization (arXiv:2110.13827)
   - **Authors**: Zhenghao Peng, Quanyi Li, Ka Ming Hui, Chunxiao Liu, Bolei Zhou
   - **Summary**: The authors develop a novel multi-agent reinforcement learning method called Coordinated Policy Optimization (CoPO) to simulate self-driven particle systems, such as traffic flows. CoPO incorporates social psychology principles to coordinate agents' behaviors while maximizing individual objectives, resulting in complex and diverse social behaviors that improve performance and safety.
   - **Year**: 2022

6. **Title**: Attention-based Fault-tolerant Approach for Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning Systems (arXiv:1910.02240)
   - **Authors**: Mingyang Geng, Kele Xu, Yiying Li, Shuqi Liu, Bo Ding, Huaimin Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper presents an attention-based fault-tolerant algorithm (FT-Attn) for multi-agent reinforcement learning systems, addressing security issues in the presence of agents with arbitrarily faulty or malicious behavior. The multi-head attention mechanism enables agents to learn effective communication policies concurrently with action policies, improving adaptability to noisy environments.
   - **Year**: 2019

7. **Title**: MetaDrive: Composing Diverse Driving Scenarios for Generalizable Reinforcement Learning (arXiv:2109.12674)
   - **Authors**: Zhenghao Peng, Quanyi Li, Ka Ming Hui, Chunxiao Liu, Bolei Zhou
   - **Summary**: MetaDrive is a compositional and extensible driving simulator designed to facilitate research in generalizable reinforcement learning. It allows for the generation of diverse driving scenarios through procedural generation and real traffic data replay, supporting both single-agent and multi-agent settings.
   - **Year**: 2021

8. **Title**: ScenarioNet: Open-Source Platform for Large-Scale Traffic Scenario Simulation and Modeling (arXiv:2306.12241)
   - **Authors**: Zhenghao Peng, Quanyi Li, Ka Ming Hui, Chunxiao Liu, Bolei Zhou
   - **Summary**: ScenarioNet is an open-source platform that enables large-scale traffic scenario simulation and modeling. It supports multi-agent cooperative learning and provides a bridge between multi-agent decision-making research and vast volumes of real-world data, facilitating the development of autonomous driving systems.
   - **Year**: 2023

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Emergent Bias in Multi-Agent Systems**: Multi-agent systems can exhibit collective behaviors leading to emergent biases that are not attributable to individual agents, posing challenges in ensuring fairness and mitigating systemic risks.

2. **Balancing Risk and Return in Dynamic Markets**: Developing frameworks that effectively balance risk and return while adapting to dynamically changing market conditions remains a significant challenge in financial applications.

3. **Ensuring Robustness Against Faulty or Malicious Agents**: Multi-agent systems must be designed to be fault-tolerant and secure, capable of handling scenarios where agents may behave arbitrarily or maliciously without compromising overall system performance.

4. **Generalizability Across Diverse Scenarios**: Creating models and systems that generalize well across a wide range of scenarios, especially in complex and dynamic environments like financial markets, is a persistent challenge.

5. **Addressing Hallucination in Large Language Models**: Mitigating the risk of hallucination, where models generate plausible but incorrect information, is crucial for ensuring the reliability and trustworthiness of AI systems in high-stakes domains such as finance.
