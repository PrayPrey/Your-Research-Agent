2. **Title**: LACS: Learning-Augmented Algorithms for Carbon-Aware Resource Scaling with Uncertain Demand (arXiv:2404.15211)
   - **Authors**: Roozbeh Bostandoost, Adam Lechowicz, Walid A. Hanafy, Noman Bashir, Prashant Shenoy, Mohammad Hajiesmaili

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Accurate Multi-Horizon Carbon Intensity Forecasting**: Developing models that can predict carbon intensity across various time horizons with high accuracy remains challenging due to the variability of renewable energy sources and grid dynamics.

2. **Uncertainty Management in Scheduling**: Effectively incorporating forecast uncertainties into scheduling decisions is complex, requiring robust mechanisms to handle variability without compromising performance.

3. **Balancing Performance and Carbon Reduction**: Achieving significant carbon reductions while maintaining acceptable performance levels involves trade-offs that are difficult to optimize, especially under strict service level objectives.

4. **Scalability of Carbon-Aware Scheduling Algorithms**: Ensuring that carbon-aware scheduling algorithms can scale efficiently with the size and complexity of modern data centers is a significant challenge.

5. **Integration with Existing Infrastructure**: Seamlessly integrating carbon-aware scheduling frameworks with existing data center management systems without causing disruptions or requiring extensive modifications is a critical hurdle.
