1. **Title**: VLA-Mark: A Cross-Modal Watermark for Large Vision-Language Alignment Models (arXiv:2507.14067)
   - **Authors**: Shuliang Liu, Qi Zheng, Jesse Jiaxi Xu, Yibo Yan, Junyan Zhang, He Geng, Aiwei Liu, Peijie Jiang, Jia Liu, Yik-Cheung Tam, Xuming Hu
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces VLA-Mark, a framework designed to embed detectable watermarks into vision-language models without compromising semantic fidelity. By integrating multiscale visual-textual alignment metrics and an entropy-sensitive mechanism, the approach ensures robust watermarking that preserves the coherence between visual and textual modalities.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: I2VWM: Robust Watermarking for Image to Video Generation (arXiv:2509.17773)
   - **Authors**: Guanjie Wang, Zehua Ma, Han Fang, Weiming Zhang
   - **Summary**: The authors propose I2VWM, a cross-modal watermarking framework tailored for image-to-video generation scenarios. By introducing the concept of Robust Diffusion Distance and employing a video-simulation noise layer during training, the method enhances watermark robustness across temporal transformations in generated videos.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: TAG-WM: Tamper-Aware Generative Image Watermarking via Diffusion Inversion Sensitivity (arXiv:2506.23484)
   - **Authors**: Yuzhuo Chen, Zehua Ma, Han Fang, Weiming Zhang, Nenghai Yu
   - **Summary**: TAG-WM presents a tamper-aware watermarking method for generative images, leveraging diffusion inversion sensitivity. The approach embeds dual watermarks into the latent space, enabling both copyright protection and tamper localization, while maintaining high generation quality and robustness against distortions.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: CAMME: Adaptive Deepfake Image Detection with Multi-Modal Cross-Attention (arXiv:2505.18035)

   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Modality Gap**: Different data modalities (e.g., text, image, audio) have distinct representations, making consistent watermark embedding across modalities challenging. Aligning watermarks that are interpretable across all domains requires sophisticated feature extraction and mapping techniques. ([tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/130097?utm_source=openai))

2. **Semantic Consistency**: Ensuring that watermarks maintain meaningful relationships between modalities is crucial. Alterations in one modality should not result in contradictions or distortions in another, preserving the semantic integrity of the content. ([tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/130097?utm_source=openai))

3. **Robustness to Transformations**: Cross-modal watermarks must withstand various transformations specific to each modality, such as compression, resizing, or paraphrasing, without degradation or loss of detectability. ([tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/130097?utm_source=openai))

4. **Imperceptibility vs. Extractability Trade-off**: Balancing the subtlety of watermarks to avoid degrading content quality while ensuring they remain detectable during extraction is a significant challenge, especially across multiple data types. ([tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/130097?utm_source=openai))

5. **Lack of Standardization**: The absence of unified standards in AI watermarking leads to interoperability issues and hinders cross-platform verification, complicating efforts to combat misinformation and deepfake content.
