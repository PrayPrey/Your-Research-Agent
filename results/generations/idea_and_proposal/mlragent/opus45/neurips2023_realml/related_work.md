**Key Challenges:**

1. **Balancing Fidelity and Cost**: Effectively integrating multiple fidelity levels in simulations and experiments to optimize computational resources without compromising accuracy remains a significant challenge.

2. **Incorporating Physics-Based Constraints**: Developing methods to seamlessly integrate known physical laws and constraints into machine learning models to guide the discovery process is complex and requires careful consideration.

3. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Accurately quantifying uncertainties in predictions, especially when combining data from various fidelity levels, is crucial for reliable materials design but remains difficult.

4. **Scalability of Active Learning Frameworks**: Ensuring that active learning approaches can efficiently handle the vast and high-dimensional search spaces inherent in materials discovery is a persistent challenge.

5. **Transferability Across Material Systems**: Developing models and frameworks that can generalize and transfer knowledge across different material systems and discovery tasks is essential for broader applicability but remains an open problem.
