**Key Challenges:**

1. **Data Incompleteness and Quality Variability**: Materials databases often have sparse measurements and varying data quality, complicating the development of accurate imputation models.

2. **Integration of Multi-Fidelity Data**: Combining high-fidelity experimental data with low-fidelity computational predictions requires sophisticated models to manage differing uncertainties and accuracies.

3. **Capturing Complex Property Correlations**: Effectively modeling the intricate relationships between various material properties is essential for accurate imputation but remains challenging.

4. **Scalability of Models**: Developing models that can efficiently handle large-scale, incomplete datasets without compromising performance is a significant hurdle.

5. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Providing calibrated uncertainty estimates for imputed values is crucial for guiding experimental prioritization and ensuring the reliability of predictions.
