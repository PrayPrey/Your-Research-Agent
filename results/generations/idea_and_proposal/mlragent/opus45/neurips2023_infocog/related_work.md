1. **Title**: InfoCom: Kilobyte-Scale Communication-Efficient Collaborative Perception with Information Bottleneck (arXiv:2512.10305)
   - **Authors**: Quanmin Wei, Penglin Dai, Wei Li, Bingyi Liu, Xiao Wu
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces InfoCom, an information-aware framework that applies the Information Bottleneck principle to collaborative perception in autonomous driving. It focuses on optimizing the extraction of minimal sufficient task-critical information, achieving significant reductions in communication overhead while maintaining near-lossless perception performance.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: On the Role of Emergent Communication for Social Learning in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (arXiv:2302.14276)
   - **Authors**: Seth Karten, Siva Kailas, Huao Li, Katia Sycara
   - **Summary**: This study explores emergent communication in multi-agent reinforcement learning, proposing an unsupervised method based on the information bottleneck to develop communication protocols that are both referentially complex and task-specific. The approach aims to align heterogeneous policies and reduce sample complexity in social learning scenarios.
   - **Year**: 2023

3. **Title**: Human-AI Copilot Optimization (HACO): A Human-in-the-Loop Reinforcement Learning Framework (arXiv:2202.10341)
   - **Authors**: Yunpeng Bai, Yifan Wu, Yuxuan Liu, et al.
   - **Summary**: HACO introduces a human-in-the-loop reinforcement learning framework where human interventions guide the learning process. The method explicitly considers human cognitive costs, aiming to minimize the reliance on human interventions over time and improve the learning agent's autonomy.
   - **Year**: 2022

4. **Title**: Learning to Simulate Self-Driven Particles System (arXiv:2110.13827)
   - **Authors**: Yifan Wu, Yunpeng Bai, et al.
   - **Summary**: This paper presents a multi-agent reinforcement learning method called Coordinated Policy Optimization (CoPO) to simulate self-driven particle systems. The approach facilitates coordination among self-interested agents, leading to the emergence of social behaviors such as cooperation and competition, which are essential for modeling complex human-AI interactions.
   - **Year**: 2021

5. **Title**: Entropy Minimization in Emergent Languages (arXiv:1905.13687)
   - **Authors**: Eugene Kharitonov, Rahma Chaabouni, Diane Bouchacourt, Marco Baroni
   - **Summary**: This research investigates the information-theoretic complexity of languages that emerge when neural agents are jointly trained to solve tasks requiring communication. It finds that emergent languages are subject to an entropy minimization pressure, balancing simplicity and task effectiveness, which is relevant for developing human-aligned communication protocols.
   - **Year**: 2019

6. **Title**: Emergent Communication for Understanding Human Language Evolution: What's Missing? (arXiv:2204.10590)
   - **Authors**: Lukas Galke, Yoav Ram, Limor Raviv
   - **Summary**: This paper discusses the discrepancies between emergent communication protocols in neural agents and human language evolution. It highlights the absence of key cognitive and communicative constraints, such as memory limitations and role alternation, suggesting that incorporating these constraints could lead to more human-like communication behaviors in AI agents.
   - **Year**: 2022

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Human Interpretability of Emergent Communication**: Ensuring that communication protocols developed by AI agents are interpretable and align with human semantic categories remains a significant challenge.

2. **Incorporating Human Cognitive Constraints**: Effectively integrating human cognitive limitations, such as working memory and attention bandwidth, into AI communication frameworks is complex and requires interdisciplinary approaches.

3. **Balancing Compression and Task Relevance**: Applying the Information Bottleneck principle necessitates a delicate balance between compressing information and retaining task-relevant details, which is critical for effective human-AI cooperation.

4. **Developing Efficient Training Methodologies**: Creating training methods that allow AI agents to develop human-aligned communication protocols without excessive reliance on human interventions poses a challenge in terms of scalability and practicality.

5. **Evaluating Human-AI Communication Effectiveness**: Establishing robust metrics and experimental designs to assess the effectiveness and interpretability of AI-generated communication in cooperative tasks is essential for advancing this field.
