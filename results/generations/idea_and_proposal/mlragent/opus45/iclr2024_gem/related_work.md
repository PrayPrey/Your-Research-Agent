1. **Title**: Adaptive Multimodal Protein Plug-and-Play with Diffusion-Based Priors (arXiv:2507.21260)
   - **Authors**: Amartya Banerjee, Xingyu Xu, Caroline Moosmüller, Harlin Lee
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces Adam-PnP, a framework that guides pre-trained protein diffusion models using gradients from multiple experimental sources. It features adaptive noise estimation and dynamic modality weighting, reducing manual hyperparameter tuning. The approach demonstrates improved accuracy in complex reconstruction tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: ProSpero: Active Learning for Robust Protein Design Beyond Wild-Type Neighborhoods (arXiv:2505.22494)
   - **Authors**: Michal Kmicikiewicz, Vincent Fortuin, Ewa Szczurek
   - **Summary**: ProSpero is an active learning framework that integrates a frozen pre-trained generative model with a surrogate updated from oracle feedback. It enables exploration beyond wild-type neighborhoods while preserving biological plausibility, consistently outperforming existing methods across diverse protein engineering tasks.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Generating Novel, Designable, and Diverse Protein Structures by Equivariantly Diffusing Oriented Residue Clouds (arXiv:2301.12485)
   - **Authors**: Yeqing Lin, Mohammed AlQuraishi
   - **Summary**: The authors present Genie, a generative model of protein structures that performs discrete-time diffusion using a cloud of oriented reference frames in 3D space. Genie generates protein backbones that are more designable, novel, and diverse than existing models, facilitating protein design with high success rates.
   - **Year**: 2023

4. **Title**: Generative De Novo Protein Design with Global Context (arXiv:2204.10673)
   - **Authors**: Cheng Tan, Zhangyang Gao, Jun Xia, Bozhen Hu, Stan Z. Li
   - **Summary**: This work proposes the Global-Context Aware (GCA) method for de novo protein design, which captures both local and global contexts in protein structures. GCA outperforms state-of-the-art methods in designing sequences for desired backbone structures.
   - **Year**: 2022

5. **Title**: Improving Compositionality of Neural Networks by Decoding Representations to Inputs (arXiv:2106.00769)
   - **Authors**: Mike Wu, Noah Goodman, Stefano Ermon
   - **Summary**: The paper introduces Decodable Neural Networks (DecNN), which jointly train a generative model to constrain neural network activations to decode back to inputs. This approach enhances compositionality, enabling applications like out-of-distribution detection and calibration while maintaining accuracy.
   - **Year**: 2021

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Uncertainty Quantification**: Accurately estimating uncertainty in generative models remains challenging, impacting the reliability of predictions and the effectiveness of active learning strategies.

2. **Integration with Experimental Feedback**: Developing frameworks that effectively incorporate experimental results to iteratively refine generative models is complex and requires careful design.

3. **Balancing Exploration and Exploitation**: Creating acquisition functions that appropriately balance the exploration of novel protein sequences with the exploitation of known high-fitness regions is a significant challenge.

4. **Computational Efficiency**: Ensuring that generative models and active learning frameworks are computationally efficient to handle large-scale protein design tasks is crucial.

5. **Biological Plausibility**: Maintaining the biological plausibility of generated protein sequences while exploring beyond wild-type neighborhoods is essential for practical applicability.
