1. **Title**: Towards Effective Federated Graph Foundation Model via Mitigating Knowledge Entanglement (arXiv:2505.12684)
   - **Authors**: Yinlin Zhu, Xunkai Li, Jishuo Jia, Miao Hu, Di Wu, Meikang Qiu
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces FedGFM+, a decentralized training paradigm for Graph Foundation Models (GFMs) that addresses knowledge entanglement in federated graph learning. The approach includes a domain-aware initialization strategy and an adaptive prompt pool to enhance domain-specific knowledge disentanglement, improving model adaptability across diverse datasets.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Provenance Networks: End-to-End Exemplar-Based Explainability (arXiv:2510.03361)
   - **Authors**: Ali Kayyam, Anusha Madan Gopal, M. Anthony Lewis
   - **Summary**: The authors propose provenance networks, a class of neural models that provide end-to-end, training-data-driven explainability. These models link each prediction directly to supporting training examples, embedding interpretability into the architecture and facilitating systematic investigations into model behavior.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: ZKPROV: A Zero-Knowledge Approach to Dataset Provenance for Large Language Models (arXiv:2506.20915)
   - **Authors**: Mina Namazi, Alexander Nemecek, Erman Ayday
   - **Summary**: ZKPROV introduces a cryptographic framework that allows users to verify that Large Language Models' responses are trained on certified datasets without revealing sensitive information. It ensures dataset relevance to user queries while preserving data confidentiality through zero-knowledge proofs.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: PROV-AGENT: Unified Provenance for Tracking AI Agent Interactions in Agentic Workflows (arXiv:2508.02866)
   - **Authors**: Renan Souza, Amal Gueroudji, Stephen DeWitt, Daniel Rosendo, Tirthankar Ghosal, Robert Ross, Prasanna Balaprakash, Rafael Ferreira da Silva
   - **Summary**: This paper presents PROV-AGENT, a provenance model that integrates AI agent interactions into end-to-end workflow provenance. It captures agent-centric metadata, linking decisions and their downstream impacts, thereby enhancing transparency and reproducibility in complex workflows.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Data Provenance and Transparency**: Ensuring comprehensive documentation of dataset origins, transformations, and usage is critical for accountability but remains a significant challenge due to the complexity and scale of data involved.

2. **Knowledge Entanglement**: In federated learning settings, disentangling domain-specific knowledge to prevent interference and enhance model adaptability across diverse datasets is a persistent issue.

3. **Explainability and Interpretability**: Developing models that provide clear, end-to-end explanations linking outputs to specific training data sources is essential for trustworthiness but is often difficult to achieve.

4. **Scalability and Efficiency**: Balancing the computational demands of training large-scale foundation models with the need for energy efficiency and scalability poses ongoing challenges.

5. **Legal and Ethical Considerations**: Addressing data copyright issues, ensuring compliance with regulations, and managing ethical concerns related to data usage are complex and evolving challenges in the development and deployment of foundation models.
