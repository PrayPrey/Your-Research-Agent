1. **Title**: EmoAgent: Assessing and Safeguarding Human-AI Interaction for Mental Health Safety (arXiv:2504.09689)
   - **Authors**: Jiahao Qiu, Yinghui He, Xinzhe Juan, Yiming Wang, Yuhan Liu, Zixin Yao, Yue Wu, Xun Jiang, Ling Yang, Mengdi Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces EmoAgent, a multi-agent AI framework designed to evaluate and mitigate mental health risks in human-AI interactions. It comprises two components: EmoEval, which simulates virtual users to assess mental health changes before and after interactions with AI characters, and EmoGuard, which monitors users' mental status, predicts potential harm, and provides corrective feedback. Experiments demonstrate that emotionally engaging dialogues can lead to psychological deterioration in vulnerable users, with EmoGuard significantly reducing these deterioration rates.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: HAICOSYSTEM: An Ecosystem for Sandboxing Safety Risks in Human-AI Interactions (arXiv:2409.16427)
   - **Authors**: Xuhui Zhou, Hyunwoo Kim, Faeze Brahman, Liwei Jiang, Hao Zhu, Ximing Lu, Frank Xu, Bill Yuchen Lin, Yejin Choi, Niloofar Mireshghallah, Ronan Le Bras, Maarten Sap
   - **Summary**: HAICOSYSTEM presents a modular sandbox environment that simulates multi-turn interactions between human users and AI agents equipped with various tools. It evaluates AI agent safety using a comprehensive multi-dimensional framework covering operational, content-related, societal, and legal risks. Through extensive simulations across multiple domains, the study highlights the challenges in building agents that can safely navigate complex interactions, especially when faced with malicious users.
   - **Year**: 2024

3. **Title**: AgentGuardian: Learning Access Control Policies to Govern AI Agent Behavior (arXiv:2601.10440)
   - **Authors**: Nadya Abaev, Denis Klimov, Gerard Levinov, David Mimran, Yuval Elovici, Asaf Shabtai
   - **Summary**: AgentGuardian introduces a security framework that governs AI agent operations by enforcing context-aware access-control policies. During a controlled staging phase, the framework monitors execution traces to learn legitimate agent behaviors and input patterns, deriving adaptive policies that regulate tool calls made by the agent. Evaluation across real-world AI applications demonstrates its effectiveness in detecting malicious inputs and mitigating errors, ensuring system integrity.
   - **Year**: 2026

4. **Title**: Personalized Safety in LLMs: A Benchmark and A Planning-Based Agent Approach (arXiv:2505.18882)
   - **Authors**: Yuchen Wu, Edward Sun, Kaijie Zhu, Jianxun Lian, Jose Hernandez-Orallo, Aylin Caliskan, Jindong Wang
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces the concept of personalized safety in large language models (LLMs) and presents PENGUIN, a benchmark comprising 14,000 scenarios across seven sensitive domains. Evaluating six leading LLMs, the study demonstrates that incorporating personalized user information significantly improves safety scores. Additionally, the authors develop RAISE, a training-free, two-stage agent framework that strategically acquires user-specific background, enhancing safety without model retraining.
   - **Year**: 2025

6. **Title**: Efficient Learning of Safe Driving Policy via Human-AI Copilot Optimization (arXiv:2202.10341)
   - **Authors**: Quanyi Li, Zhenghao Peng, Bolei Zhou
   - **Summary**: This work introduces Human-AI Copilot Optimization (HACO), a human-in-the-loop learning method that injects human knowledge into reinforcement learning to ensure training safety. HACO allows human experts to intervene and demonstrate how to avoid dangerous situations, effectively utilizing data from both exploration and human demonstration. Experiments show that HACO achieves high sample efficiency in safe driving benchmarks, training agents to drive in unseen traffic scenes with minimal human intervention.

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Balancing Personalization and Safety**: Achieving a balance between deeply personalized AI interactions and maintaining robust safety measures remains a significant challenge.

2. **Dynamic Adaptation to User Context**: Developing AI systems that can dynamically adjust safety boundaries based on real-time user context and vulnerability indicators is complex.

3. **Detection and Mitigation of Deceptive Behaviors**: Ensuring AI systems do not exhibit deceptive or harmful behaviors, especially when triggered by specific conditions, requires advanced detection and mitigation strategies.

4. **Comprehensive Risk Assessment Frameworks**: Establishing holistic frameworks to evaluate and mitigate various risks associated with AI interactions across diverse domains is essential.

5. **Transparency and Interpretability**: Enhancing the transparency and interpretability of AI models to ensure their decisions and behaviors are understandable and trustworthy poses ongoing challenges.
