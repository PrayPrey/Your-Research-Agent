10. **Title**: Adversarial Robustness in Multimodal Models: A Comprehensive Survey (arXiv:2501.04567)
    - **Authors**: [Authors not specified in the provided excerpt]
    - **Summary**: This survey examines the adversarial robustness of multimodal models, discussing various attack vectors, defense strategies, and evaluation metrics. It highlights the unique challenges posed by the integration of multiple modalities and suggests future research directions.
    - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges:**

1. **Cross-Modal Adversarial Transferability**: Understanding how adversarial perturbations in one modality (e.g., images) can affect another modality's output (e.g., text) remains a significant challenge due to the complex interactions within shared representation spaces.

2. **Detection and Mitigation of Cross-Modal Attacks**: Developing effective methods to detect and mitigate adversarial attacks that exploit cross-modal vulnerabilities is difficult, as traditional safeguards often fail to generalize across modalities.

3. **Robustness of Multimodal Alignment Mechanisms**: Ensuring the robustness of alignment mechanisms that integrate different modalities is crucial, as adversarial attacks can exploit weaknesses in these fusion processes.

4. **Evaluation Metrics for Multimodal Robustness**: Establishing standardized evaluation metrics to assess the robustness of multimodal models against adversarial attacks is challenging due to the diverse nature of multimodal data and tasks.

5. **Ethical and Safety Implications**: Addressing the ethical and safety concerns arising from adversarial vulnerabilities in multimodal models is essential, especially as these models are increasingly deployed in real-world, safety-critical applications.
