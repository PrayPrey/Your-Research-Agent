## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can moral philosophy and moral psychology theories be systematically applied to develop more ethically-informed and value-aligned AI systems?

### Refined Question

How can theories from moral philosophy and moral psychology be integrated into AI development practices to create systems that are trustworthy, transparent, and aligned with pluralistic human values rather than monolithic value structures?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. What are the most effective pathways for moral philosophers and psychologists to contribute to ethically-informed AI development?

2. How can findings from moral psychology inform the trustworthiness, transparency, and interpretability of AI decision-makers?

3. What human values are currently embedded in AI systems, and are they consistent with those in society at large?

4. What pluralistic values are missing from current-day AI systems, and how can they be incorporated?

5. Beyond RLHF, what are alternative methodological approaches for teaching AI systems human values that can accommodate diverse voices and views?
