## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we bridge the gap between machine learning research and regulatory policies to ensure ethical, legal, and technically sound algorithmic implementations?

### Refined Question

How can we operationalize regulatory principles (fairness, explainability, privacy, robustness) in machine learning systems while identifying and resolving tensions between different regulatory desiderata?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. What are the operational gaps between existing regulations (GDPR, AI Act, etc.) and state-of-the-art ML research?
2. How can we develop evaluation and auditing frameworks to ensure ML models comply with regulatory guidelines?
3. What tensions exist between different regulatory desiderata (e.g., fairness vs. privacy, explainability vs. performance)?
4. What novel algorithmic frameworks can operationalize the right to explanation, right to privacy, and right to be forgotten?
5. How can we ensure fairness and robustness of ML models in alignment with regulatory frameworks?
6. What new regulation challenges are posed by large generative models, especially in creative industries?
7. What regulation needs exist for preventing catastrophic risks from artificial general intelligence (AGI)?
