## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can foundation models be effectively developed, applied, and integrated into scientific discovery processes across diverse scientific domains while addressing unique challenges distinct from traditional computer vision or NLP applications?

### Refined Question

How can scientific foundation models be designed and deployed to advance scientific discovery across multiple domains (biomedicine, materials science, computational science, etc.), addressing domain-specific challenges in scalability, multi-modal understanding, hallucination prevention, and uncertainty quantification while maintaining compatibility with classical scientific tools and methods?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

**Progress-Oriented Questions:**
1. How do scaling laws and training strategies for scientific foundation models differ from those in NLP and computer vision?
2. Can scientific foundation models be trained once and effectively adopted across different scientific scenarios and domains?
3. Under what conditions can scientific foundation models consistently outperform specialized domain-specific models?

**Opportunity-Oriented Questions:**
4. What architectures and training approaches enable foundation models to understand multi-modal scientific inputs (molecular structures, spectra, simulation data, text) and solve multiple scientific problems?
5. How can foundation models accelerate scientific discovery, data collection, and data assimilation processes?
6. What frameworks enable seamless integration and compatibility between foundation models and classical scientific tools (simulation software, analysis pipelines, domain-specific methods)?

**Challenge-Oriented Questions:**
7. What methodologies can diagnose failure cases or operational modes where scientific foundation models perform poorly?
8. How can scientific foundation models be aligned with scientific facts and domain constraints to prevent hallucinations and maintain scientific validity?
9. What techniques can quantify and communicate the scientific uncertainty inherent in foundation model predictions?
