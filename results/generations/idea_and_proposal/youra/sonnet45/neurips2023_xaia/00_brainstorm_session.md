## Starting Context

**Background:** As AI models continue to advance in complexity and sophistication, understanding how they work and make decisions is becoming increasingly challenging. This challenge has prompted a surge of research into developing methods and tools that can enhance the transparency and explainability of these models. Nowadays, there are many such methods available, to the point that their specific applications have become somewhat unclear.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP (NeurIPS 2023 - XAI in Action)

**Problem Statement:** While many XAI methods exist, their specific applications and effectiveness across different domains have become unclear. There is a need to examine how XAI is applied in various fields, identify domain-specific challenges, understand methodological requirements, and explore transferability of insights across use cases.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How are explainable AI (XAI) methods currently being applied across different domains, what challenges are domain-specific versus universal, and what opportunities exist for advancing applied XAI through cross-domain insights?

### Refined Question

What are the domain-specific applications, challenges, and methodological requirements for explainable AI (XAI) across healthcare, natural science, auditing, fairness, NLP, and law domains, and how can insights from one application domain transfer to enhance XAI effectiveness in other domains?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Application Landscape**: What are the primary applications of XAI methods in each domain (healthcare, natural science, auditing, fairness, NLP, law), and what specific XAI techniques are most commonly used?

2. **Domain-Specific Challenges**: What obstacles hinder the effective deployment of XAI in each domain, and which challenges are domain-specific versus universal across all XAI applications?

3. **Methodological Requirements**: What are the necessary methodological requirements (e.g., interpretability metrics, validation approaches, stakeholder needs) for successfully applying XAI in different domains?

4. **Cross-Domain Transferability**: Can insights, techniques, or validation approaches developed for XAI in one domain (e.g., healthcare) be effectively transferred to other domains (e.g., law or fairness)?

5. **Future Opportunities**: What new domains could benefit from XAI applications, and what advancements in XAI methodology are needed to extend the frontiers of applied XAI?
