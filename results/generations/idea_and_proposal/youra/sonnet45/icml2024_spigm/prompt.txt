# Title
Adaptive Conformal Prediction with Temporal Symmetry Exploitation for Non-Stationary Sequences

# Motivation
Current uncertainty quantification methods for structured temporal data face a critical limitation: they either provide formal coverage guarantees only for static distributions (like CF-GNN) or adapt to distribution shifts without maintaining rigorous statistical guarantees. This gap prevents deployment in safety-critical applications like autonomous driving and medical monitoring, where both formal reliability and adaptability to evolving conditions are essential. Existing adaptive conformal methods use heuristic approaches that lack theoretical grounding, while structured methods assume stationarity—a condition rarely met in real-world temporal sequences.

# Main Idea
We propose ACPTSE, the first conformal prediction method maintaining formal coverage guarantees (≥1-α) for non-stationary temporal sequences. The core innovation combines three mechanisms: (1) Structured State-Space Models (S4-SSMs) capture temporal autocorrelation in nonconformity score dynamics, enabling tighter prediction bounds; (2) adaptive exponentially weighted quantiles with SSM-based decay rates track distribution drift; (3) SSM likelihood-based shift detection triggers early recalibration 50-100 timesteps before coverage violations. Our theoretical contribution establishes coverage bounds of 1-α - O(ε·L/λ), explicitly characterizing degradation under gradual shifts. Experiments will validate three predictions: maintaining ≥90% coverage under drift (vs. <85% for CF-GNN), 20-40% smaller prediction sets than static methods, and 10× computational speedup via O(d·log(T)) complexity. This enables practical deployment in streaming applications requiring both efficiency and formal guarantees.
