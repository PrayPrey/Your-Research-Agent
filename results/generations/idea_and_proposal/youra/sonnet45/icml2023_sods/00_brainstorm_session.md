## Starting Context

Sampling and optimization in discrete space are classical and important problems that arise in many applications, including physics, combinatorial optimization, compiler optimization, and many modern machine learning models like large language models and protein models. Examples include searching device placement strategies for distributed neural network training, or sampling from language model posteriors with arbitrary conditioning.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP / Research Proposal (ICML 2023 - Sampling and Optimization in Discrete Space)

**Key Context:** Discrete space sampling/optimization is challenging compared to continuous space. Recent research trends include: (1) gradient-based MCMC algorithms extending Langevin dynamics to discrete space, (2) embedding methods mapping discrete to continuous space, and (3) novel proposal strategies like Stein variational methods and GFlowNet.
