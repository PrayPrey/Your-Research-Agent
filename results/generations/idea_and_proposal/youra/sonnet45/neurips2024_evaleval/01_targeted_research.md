## 1. Research Questions

### Primary Research Question
What evaluation frameworks, participatory methodologies, and documentation standards are needed to systematically assess and document the broader societal impacts of generative AI systems (text, image, audio, video) in ways that integrate evaluation science expertise with stakeholder community knowledge and enable policy-relevant recommendations?

### Detailed Research Questions
1. What existing evaluation methodologies from evaluation science can be adapted or extended to assess generative AI's societal impacts, and what new methods are needed?

2. How can evaluation frameworks be designed to meaningfully incorporate expertise from affected communities, practitioners, and domain experts beyond ML/AI specialists?

3. What documentation and standardization practices would enable systematic comparison, replication, and aggregation of impact evaluations across different generative AI systems and deployment contexts?

4. What barriers currently prevent broader adoption of social impact evaluation for generative AI systems, and how can these be addressed through framework design, tooling, or policy?

5. How can evaluation findings be structured and communicated to inform investment priorities, regulatory approaches, and community-driven governance of generative AI systems?
