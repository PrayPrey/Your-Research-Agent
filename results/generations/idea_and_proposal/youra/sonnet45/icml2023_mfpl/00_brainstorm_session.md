## Starting Context

**Background:** Learning from human preferences, or preference-based learning, has been critical to major advances in AI and machine learning. It is based on the fact that humans are more reliable at providing relative feedback compared to numerical values. Therefore, preference feedback is usually easier to collect and less biased. A recent success story that showed the dormant potential of learning from preference feedback is fine-tuning of large language models with a reward function learned from human feedback and reinforcement learning to follow instructions in a dialogue context.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP (ICML 2023 - The Many Facets of Preference-based Learning)

**Context:** This workshop aims to bring together different communities where preference-based learning has played a major role and connect theory to practice by identifying real-world systems that can benefit from incorporating preference feedback.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question
How can preference-based learning be advanced and applied across diverse domains to bridge theory and practice in AI and machine learning?

### Refined Question
What are the fundamental methodologies, practical applications, and cross-domain connections of preference-based learning that can advance real-world AI systems while connecting theoretical foundations to practical implementations?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Preference Feedback Mechanisms:** How can preference feedback collection methods be improved across different domains (LLMs, robotics, recommender systems) to reduce bias and increase reliability?

2. **Theory-Practice Integration:** What are the key gaps between theoretical preference-based learning frameworks and their practical implementations in real-world systems?

3. **Cross-Domain Applications:** How can preference-based learning techniques be transferred and adapted across domains such as collaborative filtering, reinforcement learning, robotics, optimization, and healthcare?

4. **Reward Function Learning:** What are the most effective approaches for learning reward functions from human preferences, and how do they scale to complex domains like large language models and autonomous systems?

5. **Multi-Objective Preference Learning:** How can preference-based learning handle multi-objective optimization scenarios where preferences may be conflicting or context-dependent?
