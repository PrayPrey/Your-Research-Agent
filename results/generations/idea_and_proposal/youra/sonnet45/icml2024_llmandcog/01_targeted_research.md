## 9. Conclusion

### Key Findings

1. **Fragmented Cognitive Evaluation Landscape**
   - 83 resources collected reveal isolated evaluation of cognitive abilities
   - No unified benchmark exists for reasoning + navigation + planning + ToM
   - Current SOTA: MoMentS (ToM), CogEval (planning), NumericBench (reasoning), spatial cognition benchmarks (navigation) all separate
   - Gap impacts both measurement and development of cognitively integrated systems

2. **Architecture-Dependent Emergence**
   - Cognitive synergy emerges only in GPT-4, not GPT-3.5 or Llama2-13b (245 citations)
   - DeepSeek exhibits sophisticated ToM + forward planning; GPT-4 o1/o3-mini show trust collapse
   - "Window of maximal alignment" during training suggests developmental trajectory matters
   - Augmentation often necessary: Hybrid Mind (LLM+GIS) achieves 70.48% vs GPT-4's <25% on spatial tasks

3. **Documented Failure Modes Across Cognitive Tasks**
   - **Planning:** Hallucinations, loops, no emergent out-of-the-box planning (CogEval, 96 citations)
   - **Reasoning:** Numerical reasoning gaps persist in SOTA models (NumericBench, 9 citations)
   - **Navigation:** Spatial cognition: 24.95 vs 68.38 human baseline (Xu et al., 18 citations)
   - **ToM:** Commercial LLM 60% accuracy vs 75% analogical reasoning baseline (ToMCAT, 0 citations)
   - Performance drops 15% as task complexity increases (VideoCogQA)

4. **Three Implementation Paradigms Identified**
   - **Pure LLM:** Cognitive synergy via multi-persona (SPP), hypothesis-driven ToM (thought-tracing, AutoToM)
   - **Augmented LLM:** Tool integration (RAG, external modules), hybrid approaches (Hybrid Mind: LLM+GIS)
   - **Specialized Architectures:** Training innovations (GRPO for math, C-GRPO for multimodal)
   - Growing trend: Hybrid approaches combining LLM reasoning with symbolic/algorithmic components

5. **Mechanistic Interpretability Lags Behind Performance**
   - SemanticLens, Transformer Dynamics provide general interpretability
   - No tools specifically trace cognitive ability failures to internal mechanisms
   - Black box paradox persists: can measure failures, cannot explain why
   - Neuroscience-inspired frameworks proposed (ICML 2024) but not yet applied to cognitive tasks
