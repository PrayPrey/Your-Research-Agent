## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we develop language models that are both technically capable and socially responsible, addressing risks and harms from early development through deployment?

### Refined Question

What methodologies, frameworks, and technical approaches can enable the development and deployment of language models that proactively address security, privacy, bias, safety, and transparency concerns while maintaining effectiveness and promoting social good?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Security and Privacy:** How can we protect language models from adversarial attacks, data extraction, and privacy violations while maintaining their utility for legitimate users?

2. **Bias and Fairness:** What techniques can detect, measure, and mitigate bias and exclusion in language models across diverse populations and use cases?

3. **Safety and Alignment:** How can we ensure language models remain robust, aligned with human values, and safe across different deployment contexts?

4. **Transparency and Auditing:** What evaluation frameworks and red-teaming methodologies effectively audit language model behaviors and identify potential harms before deployment?

5. **Deployment Practices:** What development and deployment protocols minimize societal risks while maximizing beneficial applications, particularly for underserved communities and low-resource languages?

6. **Multimodal Considerations:** How do novel input/output modalities introduce new risks, and what safeguards are needed for multimodal language models?

7. **Interpretability:** What methods enable stakeholders to understand and explain language model decisions, particularly in high-stakes applications?
