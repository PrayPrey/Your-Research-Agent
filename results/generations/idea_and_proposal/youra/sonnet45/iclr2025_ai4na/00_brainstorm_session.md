## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI and machine learning methods advance nucleic acids research across structure prediction, functional analysis, and therapeutic design?

### Refined Question

How can novel AI methods and foundation models address key challenges in nucleic acid structure prediction, interaction understanding, and therapeutic molecule design?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Structure and Function:** How can AI improve RNA secondary and tertiary structure prediction, and what novel methods can better model nucleic acid interactions and functional analysis?

2. **Foundation and Generative Models:** What approaches can enable effective multimodal nucleic acid foundation models, and how can generative models be designed specifically for RNA/DNA sequences with desired properties?

3. **Therapeutic Applications:** How can AI accelerate nucleic acid drug design and discovery, and what methods can effectively model NA modifications and mutations for therapeutic purposes?

4. **Genomic Data Analysis:** What AI techniques can improve genome reconstruction, gene expression analysis, genetic variant calling, and single-cell transcriptomics/genomics analysis?
