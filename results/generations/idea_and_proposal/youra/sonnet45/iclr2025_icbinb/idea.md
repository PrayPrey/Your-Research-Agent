# Research Idea: IF-MCDA Pre-Deployment Risk Assessment for Deep Learning Systems

## Title
Intuitionistic Fuzzy Multi-Criteria Risk Assessment Framework for Predicting Deep Learning Deployment Failures

## Motivation
Deep learning models frequently fail in production despite strong benchmark performance, yet current pre-deployment assessment tools operate in silos—testing drift, fairness, robustness, or uncertainty independently. This fragmentation misses critical interaction effects: a model may pass drift tests but still fail due to fairness violations, or show good calibration yet be vulnerable to adversarial perturbations. No unified framework exists to predict deployment failures before they occur, forcing organizations into costly reactive firefighting. This research addresses the critical gap in integrated pre-deployment risk assessment by adapting proven multi-criteria decision analysis from financial forecasting to machine learning deployment prediction.

## Main Idea
We propose an Intuitionistic Fuzzy Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (IF-MCDA) framework that integrates four orthogonal risk dimensions—conformal prediction uncertainty, distribution drift (Jensen-Shannon divergence), adversarial robustness scores, and fairness violations—into a unified probabilistic risk score with 95% confidence intervals. The core hypothesis: entropy-weighted aggregation of these dimensions will achieve ≥70% accuracy in predicting 7-day deployment failures (>10% performance degradation or critical fairness violations) for production tabular ML systems in healthcare and finance, outperforming single-dimension baselines by ≥15 percentage points. The causal mechanism operates through multi-dimensional failure mode capture—different risks manifest independently (drift without unfairness, robustness without drift), requiring integrated assessment. We validate through retrospective analysis of 200 production models with documented outcomes, comparing against conformal prediction-only, drift-only, robustness-only, and fairness-only baselines. Expected impact: automated, uncertainty-aware pre-deployment gating that prevents high-risk models from reaching production, reducing deployment failures through proactive risk-informed decision-making.
