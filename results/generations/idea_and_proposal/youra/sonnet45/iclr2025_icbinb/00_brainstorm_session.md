## Starting Context

**Background:** In recent years, we have witnessed a remarkable rise of deep learning (DL), whose impressive performance on benchmark tasks has led to increasing ambitions to deploy DL in real-world applications across all fields and disciplines. However, despite its potential, DL still faces many challenges during deployment in dynamic, real-world conditions, exposing practical limitations that are often overlooked in controlled benchmarks.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP - ICLR 2025 I Can't Believe It's Not Better (ICBINB) Workshop

**Context:** This workshop focuses on understanding deep learning through negative results, failed experiments, and unexpected challenges encountered in applying deep learning to real-world problems across industry and science.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question
Why don't deep learning approaches always deliver as expected in the real world?

### Refined Question
What are the common patterns, underlying causes, and failure modes that prevent deep learning models from achieving expected performance when deployed in real-world applications across different domains?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Data-Related Challenges**: What data-related issues (distribution shift, bias, label quality, noisy measurements, quality of simulated data) commonly cause deep learning failures in real-world deployments?

2. **Model Limitations**: What model-specific limitations (assumption violations, robustness issues, interpretability constraints, scalability problems, representation misalignment) lead to suboptimal performance in practical applications?

3. **Deployment Challenges**: What practical deployment barriers (computational demands, hardware constraints, safety concerns, reliability issues) prevent successful real-world implementation?

4. **Cross-Domain Patterns**: What common failure patterns and underlying causes appear across different application domains (healthcare, robotics, scientific discovery, education, social sciences)?

5. **Ethics and Fairness**: What ethical and societal challenges emerge during deep learning deployment, and how do they manifest as failure modes in real-world systems?
