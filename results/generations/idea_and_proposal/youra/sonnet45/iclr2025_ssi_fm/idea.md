# Title
Stability-Guided Dual-Loop Self-Improvement: A Unified Framework for Autonomous Foundation Model Enhancement

# Motivation
Foundation models face an impending data bottleneck as internet-scale datasets become exhausted. While self-improvement through synthetic data offers a solution, existing approaches suffer from model collapse, safety degradation, or address only isolated components. Current state-of-the-art methods integrate at most 3 of 5 critical mechanisms (data accumulation, weak-to-strong bootstrapping, multi-agent debate, generator-verifier training, safety monitoring), leaving capability gaps. A unified framework with principled coordination is needed to enable sustained autonomous improvement without human supervision while simultaneously preventing collapse and maintaining alignment.

# Main Idea
We propose SG-DLSI, integrating five complementary mechanisms through dual coordinated loops: an exploration loop for capability growth and an exploitation loop for output refinement. Meta-learned dynamic ratio control prevents model collapse during data accumulation (target: <1.2× perplexity increase). One-shot weak-to-strong initialization bootstraps capabilities (+10% MMLU target). Adversarially-robust multi-agent debate refines outputs (≥0.65 win rate) while mitigating vulnerability amplification. Joint generator-verifier training exploits the verification-generation gap. Continuous safety monitoring maintains alignment (≥0.95 baseline). Control-theory-inspired adaptive switching and meta-learned arbitration coordinate competing objectives, achieving 15% efficiency gains over fixed schedules. Comprehensive experiments across 44 runs will validate simultaneous achievement of capability growth, collapse prevention, quality improvement, and safety maintenance—demonstrating the first complete autonomous self-improvement system.
