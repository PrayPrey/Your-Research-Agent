## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

What are the critical theoretical foundations and practical efficacy challenges that need to be addressed to advance deep generative models across diverse application domains?

### Refined Question

How can we advance the theoretical understanding of deep generative models (particularly in expressivity, optimization, and robustness) to enable more effective practical applications in high-dimensional, multimodal, and scientific domains?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Expressivity and Performance:** How does the expressivity of different deep generative model architectures vary across datasets, and what theoretical principles govern their performance variations?

2. **Optimization and Generalization:** What are the fundamental challenges in optimization and generalization of deep generative models, and how do implicit bias and regularization mechanisms affect their convergence and stability?

3. **Sampling and Efficiency:** What novel sampling methods and improved sampling schemes can enhance the efficiency and scalability of deep generative models in high-dimensional spaces?

4. **Robustness and Boundaries:** What are the robustness and generalization boundaries of generative models, particularly regarding adversarial attacks, and how can we develop effective defense mechanisms?

5. **Multimodal and Scientific Applications:** How can deep generative models be effectively adapted for multimodal data and structured scientific discovery (AI4Science) while maintaining theoretical guarantees on their behavior?
