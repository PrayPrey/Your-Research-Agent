## 1. Research Questions

### Primary Research Question
How can we develop AI systems that authentically represent, respect, and perform equitably across diverse global cultures, while preventing the universalization of western-centered values and minimizing unforeseen impacts on cultural production and consumption?

### Detailed Research Questions

1. **Conceptual and Theoretical Foundations**: What does global inclusion mean in the context of AI, and what are the possibilities and challenges of building culturally inclusive AI models?

2. **Scalable Cultural Representation Evaluations**: How do we build evaluation and development pipelines that can test cross-cultural performance via cultural metrics such as representation, quality, impact, and inclusion at scale?

3. **Culturally-Rich Training Datasets**: What are the features of a culturally representative training dataset, and what processes and conditions are needed to create or curate such training data?

4. **Methods to Study Cultural Values**: How can we recognize and account for the different cultural values embedded in our AI pipelines, and how do we bring our cultures of development in sync with our cultures of deployment?

5. **User Interactions in Support of Cultural Inclusion**: Are there creative strategies that can reparatively promote the inclusion of subjugated cultural values through UI, deployment, or public education/advocacy?

6. **Cultural Impacts of Generative AI**: How can we understand immediate and longer-term impacts of these technologies on the culture industries, and how does AI support or challenge existing dynamics in content creation and distribution?
