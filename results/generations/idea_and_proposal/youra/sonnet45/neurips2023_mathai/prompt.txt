# Title
Structural Invariance Testing: Measuring Compositional Abstraction in LLMs Through Performance Variance Analysis

# Motivation
Current mathematical reasoning benchmarks fail to distinguish genuine understanding from pattern memorization in LLMs. Models achieve >90% accuracy on standard benchmarks yet show dramatic performance drops (up to 19.6%) when problems are superficially varied while preserving deep mathematical structure. This saturation-without-understanding problem prevents researchers from tracking genuine reasoning progress and identifying brittleness in deployed systems. We need diagnostic tools that measure whether models extract compositional abstractions or merely match surface features to memorized patterns.

# Main Idea
We propose the Abstraction Index (AI), a variance-based metric quantifying performance consistency across structural equivalence classes—problem sets with identical deep structure but systematic surface variations (semantic context, numerical values, notation, linguistic expression). The core hypothesis: models with genuine compositional abstraction should demonstrate performance invariance across these variations (AI > 0.7), while memorization-dependent models show high accuracy but low AI (< 0.5). 

Our framework controls for confounds through expert difficulty validation (±1 point variance), contamination screening, and stochastic variance decomposition. We test across four mathematical domains (arithmetic, algebra, geometry, proofs) using existing benchmarks (GSM8K, MATH). Expected outcomes include a validated diagnostic framework classifying models into four patterns (strong abstraction, brittle memorization, capability gaps, inconsistent performance), enabling benchmark designers and model developers to measure understanding beyond accuracy metrics.

## Refined Hypothesis Statement

**Title:** Structural Invariance Testing (SIT): A Validated Compositional Abstraction Measurement Framework for Mathematical Reasoning in LLMs

**Core Hypothesis:** LLMs with genuine mathematical understanding should demonstrate performance invariance across validated structural equivalence classes—problem sets with identical deep structure but systematic surface variations. The Abstraction Index (AI) quantifies this invariance, where AI = 1 - (σ_systematic - σ_stochastic) / max_possible_σ. High accuracy with low AI indicates brittle memorization (Gap 1 concern); high accuracy with high AI indicates robust compositional abstraction.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H0):** Performance variance across structural equivalence classes is primarily driven by difficulty drift or stochastic variation rather than compositional abstraction capability.

## Causal Mechanism

**Proposed Mechanism:**
Compositional abstraction enables models to extract deep mathematical structure independent of surface presentation. Models with strong compositional abstraction:
1. Parse problem → Extract structural representation (mathematical relationships, operations, constraints)
2. Map to solution strategy based on deep structure
3. Execute strategy independent of surface features (context, wording, notation)

Result: Consistent performance across surface variations (high AI)

**Alternative Mechanism (Memorization):**
Models lacking compositional abstraction:
1. Match problem surface features to training exemplars
2. Retrieve solution pattern associated with surface match
3. Performance degrades when surface features change (mismatch to memorized patterns)

Result: Variable performance across surface variations (low AI)

**Evidence for Causal Links:**
- Putnam-AXIOM demonstrates 19.6% performance drop on variations (Gulati et al., 2025) → Surface dependency exists
- Systematic Diagnosis reveals perfect procedural but failing combinatorial reasoning (Parupudi, 2025) → Brittleness consistent with surface-dependent processing
- Cognitive science: Transfer performance validates understanding in humans (educational assessment literature) → Principle transfers to computational systems
- Relational reasoning in neural networks (Geiger et al., 2020) → Neural systems can develop compositional representations

**Key Tension:**
Understanding vs. memorization cannot be distinguished by accuracy alone. Current benchmarks saturate (>90% accuracy) while variation performance drops dramatically. This performance-variance dissociation requires new diagnostic tools.
