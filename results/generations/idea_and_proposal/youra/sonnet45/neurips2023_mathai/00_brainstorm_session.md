## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can large language models and deep learning systems achieve genuine mathematical reasoning capabilities, and what novel applications emerge from this capacity?

### Refined Question

To what extent can deep learning models comprehend mathematical concepts and reasoning processes, and how can we design systems that move beyond pattern matching toward genuine mathematical understanding with measurable real-world applications?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Human vs. Machine Reasoning:** How do current AI approaches to mathematical reasoning compare to human cognitive processes, and where do they fundamentally differ, complement, or intersect?

2. **Evaluation Framework:** How can we design benchmarks and evaluation methodologies that accurately assess mathematical reasoning abilities in large language models, accounting for memorization, test contamination, and the distinction between procedural competence and conceptual understanding?

3. **Capability Advancement:** What architectural innovations, training methodologies, or hybrid approaches could enable AI systems to move beyond current limitations toward more robust and general mathematical reasoning?

4. **Educational Applications:** What role can deep learning models play in mathematics education, particularly in contexts with limited educational resources, and how can we ensure these systems enhance rather than replace human mathematical development?

5. **Domain Applications:** What near-term and long-term applications could AI mathematical reasoning enable across software verification, scientific discovery, engineering, finance, and mathematical research itself?
