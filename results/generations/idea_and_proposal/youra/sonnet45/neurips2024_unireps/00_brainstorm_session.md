## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

When, how, and why do different neural models learn the same representations?

### Refined Question

What are the underlying mechanisms and theoretical principles that cause distinct neural models (both biological and artificial) to converge on similar internal representations when processing similar stimuli, and how can we leverage this phenomenon to unify representations across models?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Theoretical Understanding**: What learning dynamics and identifiability constraints in functional and parameter space lead to convergent representations across different neural architectures?

2. **Empirical Evidence**: Under what conditions (data distribution, architecture constraints, optimization objectives) do different models develop similar representations?

3. **Cross-Domain Analysis**: How do findings from neuroscience (biological neural networks) inform our understanding of representation convergence in artificial neural networks, and vice versa?

4. **Practical Applications**: How can understanding representation similarity enable practical applications like model merging, stitching, reuse, and multi-modal learning?

5. **Universal Features**: What invariances naturally emerge from learning processes across different models, and how can we enforce or leverage them?
