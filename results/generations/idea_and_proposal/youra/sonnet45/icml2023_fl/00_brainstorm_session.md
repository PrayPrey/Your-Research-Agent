## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical applications in federated learning and analytics?

### Refined Question

How can federated learning systems be designed and deployed to address real-world challenges across scalability, privacy, security, and fairness while bridging the theory-practice gap in production environments?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Scalable Systems**: How can we design scalable and robust federated machine learning systems for cross-device and cross-silo production applications?

2. **Privacy-Preserving Technologies**: What are effective approaches for implementing differential privacy and privacy-preserving technologies in federated settings, and how can we audit their empirical privacy guarantees?

3. **Foundation Model Adaptation**: How can foundation models be effectively trained, fine-tuned, and personalized in federated settings while addressing distribution shifts and continual learning challenges?

4. **Security and Trustworthiness**: What security attacks and defenses are most relevant in federated and decentralized settings, and how can we ensure trustworthy learning at scale?

5. **Fairness and Social Impact**: How can we develop and deploy fair and responsible models in federated settings while considering privacy policies and social impact?
