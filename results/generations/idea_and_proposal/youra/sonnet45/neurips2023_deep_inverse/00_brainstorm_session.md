## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can modern deep learning techniques, particularly diffusion models, advance the solution of inverse problems while addressing fundamental challenges like model uncertainty and partial information about system models?

### Refined Question

How can deep learning approaches address model uncertainty in inverse problem solutions when only partial information about the forward model is available, and what are the benefits, limitations, and optimal algorithms for using diffusion models as learned priors across diverse modalities (MRI, acoustics, graphs, proteins)?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Model Uncertainty Challenge:** What algorithms and analysis techniques are required for inverse problem applications where we only have access to partial information about the system model, rather than the complete forward model?

2. **Diffusion Models as Priors:** What are the benefits and limitations of using diffusion models as learned priors for solving inverse problems across diverse modalities (MRI, acoustics, graphs, proteins)?

3. **Optimal Algorithms:** What are the optimal algorithms for incorporating diffusion model priors into inverse problem solvers, and how do they perform under different types of distortion models?

4. **Beyond Gaussian Assumptions:** How can deep learning-based solutions move beyond simple distortion models (like additive Gaussian noise) to handle more complex, realistic noise and uncertainty patterns?

5. **Cross-Domain Applicability:** How do diffusion-based inverse problem solutions generalize across different scientific domains (medical imaging, seismic imaging, computational photography)?
