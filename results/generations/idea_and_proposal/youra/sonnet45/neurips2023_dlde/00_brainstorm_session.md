## Research Question Development

### Initial Question
How can we advance the symbiotic relationship between deep learning architectures and differential equation models?

### Refined Question
How can the integration of differential equation models into deep learning systems improve neural architectures, optimization algorithms, and theoretical understanding, while deep learning methods enhance the speed, flexibility, and realism of differential equation simulations?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Using differential equations to understand and improve deep learning:**
   - How can differential equation models be effectively incorporated into existing deep learning architectures (neural ODEs, diffusion models)?
   - What are the trade-offs and benchmarks for numerical methods when implementing DEs in DL models?
   - How can modeling training dynamics using DEs generate theoretical insights and novel algorithms?

2. **Using deep learning to create or solve differential equation models:**
   - What DL methods are most effective for solving high-dimensional, highly parameterized, or challenging DE models?
   - How can we develop learning-augmented numerical methods (hypersolvers, hybrid solvers) that leverage deep learning?
   - What specialized DL architectures (neural operators, PINNs) are optimal for specific classes of differential equations?

3. **Neural architectures leveraging classical mathematical models:**
   - How do normalizing flows, graph neural diffusion models, and Fourier neural operators incorporate classical mathematical principles?
   - What domain-specific equivariances can be built into architectures through understanding of differential equations and dynamical systems?
   - How can latent dynamical models (latent NDEs, H3, S4, Hyena) be improved through deeper integration of DE theory?
