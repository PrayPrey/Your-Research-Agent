## Executive Summary

**Initial Interest:** In-context learning (ICL) is an emerging capability of large-scale models, including large language models (LLMs) like GPT-3, to acquire new capabilities directly from the context of an input example without separate training or fine-tuning, enabling these models to adapt rapidly to new tasks, datasets, and domains. This research explores ICL from multiple perspectives including architectures, theory, evaluation, and safety.
