## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we address the safety challenges that emerge from five key trends in general-purpose AI: autonomous agents, multimodal systems, personalized interactions, sensitive domain applications, and dangerous capability emergence?

### Refined Question

What safety frameworks, mitigation strategies, and design principles are needed to ensure trustworthy deployment of next-generation AI systems across agentic autonomy, multimodal processing, personalized interactions, high-stakes sensitive applications, and dangerous capability management?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Agentic AI Safety**: How do we ensure autonomous AI agents respect privacy, adhere to safety protocols, and prevent unintended consequences or adversary exploitation as they become more autonomous?

2. **Multimodal Safety**: How do we craft robust guidelines and security measures to address content appropriateness, privacy, bias, and misinformation challenges in AI systems that process and generate diverse modalities (audio, video, images)?

3. **Personalized Interaction Safety**: How do we balance tailored conversational experiences with user safety, preventing data privacy breaches and echo chamber formation as AI agents evolve for social and personal interaction?

4. **Sensitive Application Safety**: How do we ensure that AI systems integrated into high-risk domains (legal, medical, mental health) enhance decision-making without compromising human expertise, causing overreliance on automation, or enabling catastrophic errors?

5. **Dangerous Capability Mitigation**: How do we design AI systems with safeguards to prevent misuse in extracting or generating information about harmful applications (bioweapons, cyber attacks) while still allowing beneficial research and innovation as AI's knowledge capabilities improve?
