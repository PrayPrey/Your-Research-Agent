## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

What are the security and trustworthiness challenges in building, deploying, and operating large language models across the full theory/algorithm/application stack?

### Refined Question

How can we address security and trustworthiness challenges in Large Language Models through novel approaches spanning reliability assurance, privacy protection, security defenses, and interpretability mechanisms?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Reliability & Assessment:** How can we ensure and assess the reliability of LLMs in production environments?

2. **Privacy & Copyright:** What mechanisms can protect against privacy leakage and copyright violations in LLM training and deployment?

3. **Security & Robustness:** How can we defend LLMs against adversarial attacks, backdoor attacks, and toxic speech?

4. **Interpretability & Trust:** What methods can improve LLM interpretability to build trustworthy AI systems?

5. **Emerging Paradigms:** What new security challenges arise from novel learning paradigms (prompt engineering, few-shot learning) and how do we address them?
