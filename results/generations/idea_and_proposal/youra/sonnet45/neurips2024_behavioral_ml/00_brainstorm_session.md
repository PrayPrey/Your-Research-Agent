## Starting Context

Across many application areas, machine learning systems rely on human data. Yet these systems often leave unmodelled the psychological processes that generate human data. Fortunately, there's a field full of insights about human behavior: the behavioral sciences. However, many of these insights are qualitative. Integrating them into machine learning systems requires converting them into computational models and designing machine learning systems to incorporate them.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP (NeurIPS 2024 - Behavioral Machine Learning)

**Workshop Goal:** Explore the incorporation of insights from the behavioral sciences into AI models/systems, bringing together computer scientists across many subfields with behavioral scientists.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can insights from behavioral sciences be systematically integrated into machine learning systems to better model the psychological processes underlying human-generated data?

### Refined Question

How can qualitative insights from behavioral sciences be converted into computational models and integrated into machine learning architectures to improve modeling of human psychological processes and behavior?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Alignment Challenge**: How can behavioral science models of human behavior be used to improve alignment of large language models and other generative AI systems?

2. **Evaluation Framework**: What evaluation methodologies can incorporate models of human interaction to better assess AI system performance in human-centered contexts?

3. **Cognitive Modeling Integration**: How can formal computational cognitive science models be effectively incorporated into modern AI architectures?

4. **Creativity Modeling**: How can psychological theories of creativity be operationalized as computational models for integration into generative AI systems?

5. **Human-Robot Interaction**: What behavioral models are most effective for enhancing natural and effective human-robot interaction?

6. **Interpretability Through Behavior**: How can behavioral models serve as interpretability tools to make AI system decisions more transparent and understandable?
