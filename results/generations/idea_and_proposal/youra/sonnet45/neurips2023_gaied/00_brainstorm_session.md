## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can generative AI models like ChatGPT be leveraged to enhance educational technology while addressing the challenges and safeguards needed for responsible deployment in educational settings?

### Refined Question

What are the opportunities and technical innovations needed to realize generative AI's potential in education (as personalized tutors, digital assistants, and collaborative learning peers) while developing safeguards against biases, misinformation, and authenticity concerns?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **GAI→ED Opportunities**: How can generative AI capabilities enable novel educational scenarios such as personalized content generation, grading, and human-machine collaborative systems with different AI roles (tutors, assistants, peers)?

2. **ED→GAI Safeguards**: What novel prompting, fine-tuning, and validation techniques are needed to safeguard generative AI outputs against biases and incorrect information in educational contexts?

3. **Authenticity Validation**: How can we develop effective techniques to validate content authenticity and determine whether assignments were generated by students or AI models?

4. **Policy and Real-World Deployment**: What are the unique challenges faced by educators and policymakers in using generative AI, and how can we design effective regulatory policies for educational institutions?

5. **Cross-Domain Integration**: How can we build a "multilingual" community that combines technical expertise in generative AI, classroom experience, and know-how for building/deploying educational technology at scale?
