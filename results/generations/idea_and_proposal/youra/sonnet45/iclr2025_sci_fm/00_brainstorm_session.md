## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we advance open science practices in foundation model research to improve transparency, reproducibility, and accessibility for the global research community?

### Refined Question

How can we systematically develop and evaluate open science methodologies across the foundation model lifecycle—from dataset curation and model pretraining to training protocols, evaluation benchmarks, and deployment—to enhance transparency, reproducibility, and broad accessibility in AI research?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Open Datasets**: What are the most effective methods for acquiring, curating, and synthesizing pretraining, instruction, and preference datasets through manual or algorithmic approaches? How can we ensure open access to alignment research datasets?

2. **Open Foundation Models & Training**: What pretraining strategies (data scaling, architecture design, multi-modal/multi-task learning) and learning algorithms (meta-learning, model fusion, continual learning) are most effective for developing scalable open foundation models? What training dynamics (scaling laws, emergent capabilities, interpretability) should guide open training protocols?

3. **Open Evaluation & Benchmarking**: How can we develop transparent evaluation protocols, metrics, and benchmark datasets that enable fair comparison across different foundation models? What standards should guide the open sharing of evaluation results?

4. **Open Compute Efficiency**: What techniques (model distillation, compression, quantization, attention/memory optimization) can make foundation models more computationally efficient while maintaining performance for broader accessibility?

5. **Open Multi-Modal & Interactive Systems**: How can we expand foundation models to underexplored modalities (chemistry, medicine, education) and develop open conversational AI, multi-agent systems, and tool integration frameworks?

6. **Open Replication**: What methodologies and resources are needed to successfully replicate and openly share previously proprietary foundation models and systems to ensure broader research transparency?
