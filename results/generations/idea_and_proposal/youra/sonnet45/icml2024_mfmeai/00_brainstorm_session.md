## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can Multi-modal Foundation Models (MFM) such as CLIP, ImageBind, GPT-4V, and Gemini empower embodied AI agents to operate effectively in open-ended environments?

### Refined Question

How can Multi-modal Foundation Models be adapted and optimized to enhance embodied AI agents' perception, decision-making, and control capabilities in open-ended environments, addressing challenges in training, evaluation, system architecture, and the balance between high-level reasoning and low-level control?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Training and Evaluation:** How can we train and evaluate Multi-modal Foundation Models in open-ended, dynamic environments that reflect the complexity of real-world embodied AI scenarios?

2. **System Architecture:** What constitutes an effective system architecture for MFM-based Embodied AI Agents that enables seamless integration of perception, reasoning, and action?

3. **Decision-Making and Control:** How can MFM augment both the high-level decision-making capabilities and the low-level control requirements of embodied agents, bridging the gap between abstract reasoning and precise physical manipulation?

4. **Data Collection:** What data collection strategies and methodologies are needed to effectively train both Embodied AI Agents and their underlying Multi-modal Foundation Models?

5. **Limitations and Challenges:** What are the fundamental limitations of MFM in empowering Embodied AI, and how can we address or mitigate these constraints?
