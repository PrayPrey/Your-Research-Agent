## Starting Context

**Background:** Large-scale foundation models are revolutionizing machine learning, particularly in vision and language domains. While model architecture received significant attention in the past, recent focus has shifted towards the importance of data quality, size, diversity, and provenance. This workshop aims to highlight cutting-edge advancements in data-centric approaches for large-scale foundation models in new domains, in addition to language and vision, and engage the vibrant interdisciplinary community of researchers, practitioners, and engineers who tackle practical data challenges related to foundation models.

**Source Type:** Workshop Call for Papers (ICML 2024 - Data-centric Machine Learning Research Workshop)

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question
How can data-centric methodologies advance the development of robust, versatile foundation models that work across diverse domains beyond language and vision?

### Refined Question
What are the key principles, methods, and best practices for constructing, curating, and evaluating large-scale datasets that enable foundation models to generalize effectively across diverse application domains?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Data Source Discovery & Construction**: What are effective methods for identifying and constructing large-scale datasets from unlabeled/uncurated data in new domains? How can models assist in this dataset construction process?

2. **Quality Signals & Curation**: What quality signals and curation techniques are most effective for large-scale datasets across different domains? How do data curation practices intersect with human-computer interaction principles?

3. **Evaluation & Benchmarking**: How should datasets for evaluation be designed to properly assess foundation model capabilities? What makes benchmarks like DataPerf, DynaBench, and DataComp effective?

4. **Dataset Drift & Distribution Shifts**: What is the impact of dataset drifts in large-scale models, and how can we detect and mitigate these shifts across different application domains?

5. **Ethics & Governance**: What ethical considerations and governance frameworks are necessary for large-scale datasets, particularly as foundation models expand into sensitive domains?
