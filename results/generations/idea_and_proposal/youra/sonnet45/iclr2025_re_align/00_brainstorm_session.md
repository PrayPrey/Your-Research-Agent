## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we better understand, measure, and intervene on representational alignment between biological and artificial intelligence systems?

### Refined Question

What are the most appropriate methods to compare and align representations of intelligent systems (both natural and artificial), and how can we systematically intervene on this alignment to achieve desired outcomes in computation, behavior, and values?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Computational Strategies:** To what extent does representational alignment indicate shared computational strategies among biological and artificial systems?

2. **Metric Advancement:** How have current alignment metrics advanced our understanding of computation, and what measurement approaches should we explore next?

3. **Robustness and Generalizability:** How can we develop more robust and generalizable measures of alignment that work across different domains and types of representations?

4. **Intervention Methods:** How can we systematically increase (or decrease) representational alignment among biological and artificial systems?

5. **Implications and Consequences:** What are the implications (positive and negative) of increasing or decreasing representational alignment between systems, on behavioral alignment, value alignment, and beyond?
