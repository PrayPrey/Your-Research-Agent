## 1. Research Questions

### Primary Research Question
What are the most appropriate methods to compare and align representations of intelligent systems (both natural and artificial), and how can we systematically intervene on this alignment to achieve desired outcomes in computation, behavior, and values?

### Detailed Research Questions
1. To what extent does representational alignment indicate shared computational strategies among biological and artificial systems?
2. How have current alignment metrics advanced our understanding of computation, and what measurement approaches should we explore next?
3. How can we develop more robust and generalizable measures of alignment that work across different domains and types of representations?
4. How can we systematically increase (or decrease) representational alignment among biological and artificial systems?
5. What are the implications (positive and negative) of increasing or decreasing representational alignment between systems, on behavioral alignment, value alignment, and beyond?

### Code Analysis

**Common Implementation Patterns:**

1. **CKA Computation Pattern:**
```python
# Typical CKA implementation structure
def linear_CKA(X, Y):
    # X, Y: [n_samples, n_features]
    # Center matrices
    X_c = X - X.mean(0)
    Y_c = Y - Y.mean(0)
    # Gram matrices
    K = X_c @ X_c.T
    L = Y_c @ Y_c.T
    # CKA score
    hsic = np.trace(K @ L)
    norm_K = np.linalg.norm(K, 'fro')
    norm_L = np.linalg.norm(L, 'fro')
    return hsic / (norm_K * norm_L)
```

2. **RSA RDM Pattern:**
```python
# Typical RSA RDM computation
from scipy.spatial.distance import pdist, squareform
# Compute pairwise distances
rdm_vector = pdist(representations, metric='correlation')
# Convert to matrix form
rdm = squareform(rdm_vector)
```
