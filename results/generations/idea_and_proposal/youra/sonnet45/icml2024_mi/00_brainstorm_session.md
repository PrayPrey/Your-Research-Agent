## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we better understand and model human feedback to improve Human-AI Alignment systems across different domains?

### Refined Question

How can we develop more accurate mathematical and computational models of human feedback that account for bounded rationality, bias, and individual differences, moving beyond simplistic assumptions in current RLHF and Learning from Demonstrations approaches?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Learning from Demonstrations**: What are the limitations of current Inverse Reinforcement Learning and Imitation Learning approaches in capturing the complexity of human decision-making, and how can we improve them?

2. **RLHF for LLMs**: How can we develop more sophisticated models of human feedback for fine-tuning Large Language Models that account for human biases, inconsistencies, and varying preferences?

3. **Cross-Domain Alignment**: What common patterns exist in human feedback across different domains (robotics, recommender systems, autonomous driving), and how can we leverage these insights for better AI alignment?

4. **Behavioral Economics Integration**: How can insights from bounded rationality and behavioral economics be systematically incorporated into AI alignment algorithms?

5. **Preference Learning and Aggregation**: How can we better model and aggregate diverse human preferences while respecting individual differences and avoiding oversimplification?
