## 6. Chain-of-Relations Analysis

### Research Evolution Path

**Foundation Era (2020-2022): Establishing XAI and Benchmarking Standards**

1. **Foundation:** Maree et al. (2020, 27 citations) introduced responsible AI principles framework with explainability, fairness, privacy, accountability, and transparency pillars for financial transactions
   - **Key Contribution:** SHAP-based explanations + robustness testing
   - **Implementation:** vickyzheng-fin/Explainable-AI-for-Financial-Fraud-Detection provides production framework

2. **Benchmarking:** Hu et al. (2020, 3281 citations) established Open Graph Benchmark with large-scale datasets (100M+ nodes, 1B+ edges)
   - **Impact:** Set standards for graph ML applicable to financial networks
   - **Connection:** Enables GNN-based fraud detection (awslabs/realtime-fraud-detection-with-gnn-on-dgl)

3. **Portfolio Optimization:** Jaeger et al. (2020, 34 citations) demonstrated XGBoost + SHAP for explainable portfolio construction
   - **Pattern:** Interpretable ML for high-stakes financial decisions
   - **Implementation:** SHAP tutorial ecosystem (shap.readthedocs.io)

**Multi-Agent Era (2022-2023): DRL and Foundation Models**

4. **Extension:** Shavandi & Khedmati (2022, 92 citations) applied multi-agent DRL for algorithmic trading
   - **Innovation:** First systematic multi-agent framework for financial markets
   - **Evolution:** Led to LLM-based agent systems (TradingAgents, Magents)

5. **Foundation Models:** Yin et al. (2023, FinPT, 23 citations) introduced Profile Tuning for LLMs on tabular financial data
   - **Breakthrough:** FinBench unified benchmark for default, fraud, and churn
   - **Implementation:** Bridges tabular data to NL templates for LLM fine-tuning

6. **XAI Consolidation:** Baker & Xiang (2023, 11 citations) proved XAI as foundation for all RAI pillars
   - **Theoretical:** Demonstrates SHAP/LIME as necessary for fairness, robustness, privacy
   - **Tutorial Resources:** 4 comprehensive SHAP/LIME tutorials found via Exa

**Hybrid Architecture Era (2024): Combining Multiple DL Components**

7. **Time-Series Hybrid:** Mahmoud & Mohammed (2024, 48 citations) achieved R²=0.976 with TCN-BiLSTM hybrid
   - **Pattern:** Combining TCNs for multi-scale patterns + BiLSTMs for context
   - **Implementation:** sktime/pytorch-forecasting (4.8k stars) provides production library

8. **Non-Stationary Handling:** Han & Liu et al. (2024, 45 citations) combined GARCH + CEEMDAN + GCN

   - **Adaptability:** Pattern applicable to regime-switching markets

    - **Paradigm Shift:** Zero-shot forecasting via transfer learning
    - **GitHub:** google-research/timesfm enables foundation model approach

**GenAI Surge Era (2025): Agentic AI and Advanced Multi-Agent Systems**

11. **Generative AI Applications:** Sai et al. (2025, 14 citations) first comprehensive study of GAI integration with finance
    - **Coverage:** Consultations, predictions, fraud detection, risk evaluation, unstructured data

    - **Implementation:** microsoft/MarS (1.6k stars) - market simulation engine with GenFoundation models
