**Key Challenges**

1. **Safe Trajectory Definition**: Formalizing "safe" vs. "harmful" trajectories across different high-stakes domains (healthcare, finance, criminal justice) with domain-specific harm definitions and baselines.

2. **Timescale Misalignment**: Ensuring harm patterns follow characteristic trajectory signatures and that detection timescales align correctly with checkpoint architecture (immediate/medium/long-term).

3. **Adversarial Gaming**: Mitigating strategic manipulation of checkpoint sensors through intentional gaming by human actors who understand the monitoring system.

4. **Cross-Domain Transfer**: Validating that immune checkpoint principles (biological molecular regulation) effectively transfer to HAIC governance (socio-technical systems with strategic human behavior).

5. **Bootstrap Problem**: Initializing safe trajectory baselines in novel applications without sufficient historical interaction data, requiring effective transfer learning and simulation approaches.

6. **Intervention Effectiveness**: Ensuring checkpoint-triggered interventions (throttle/audit/policy change) are timely enough to prevent harm before feedback loops amplify, with sufficient enforcement mechanisms.

7. **False Positive Management**: Balancing governance interventions to prevent harms while avoiding excessive over-constraint that limits beneficial human-AI coevolution (target: ≤15% FP rate).

8. **Multi-Stage Coordination**: Developing hierarchical consensus protocols that correctly aggregate signals from three distinct checkpoint stages with different timescales and detection mechanisms.
