## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How does continuous human-AI coadaptation create feedback loops that shape both human behavior and AI system evolution across multiple levels of analysis, from individual interactions to societal institutions?

### Refined Question

What are the mechanisms and dynamics of bidirectional learning and adaptation in Human-AI Coevolution (HAIC), and how can we develop frameworks beyond traditional performance metrics to understand, evaluate, and ensure beneficial long-term outcomes across social institutions?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. How do prolonged human-AI interactions shape human cognition, decision-making, and expectations, and what are the implications for human autonomy and social norms?

2. What technical frameworks and enhancements to existing methods (e.g., RLHF) are needed to improve AI adaptability to evolving human preferences while ensuring robustness, fairness, and bias reduction?

3. How can we revise AI evaluation metrics to assess systems through the lens of HAIC rather than static performance benchmarks, particularly for bidirectional learning scenarios?

4. What are the dynamics of feedback loops in high-stakes domains (healthcare, criminal justice, education), and how do they differ from low-stakes collaborative environments?

5. What design principles and governance frameworks are necessary to align AI systems with human values while accounting for dynamic human-AI interactions and preventing harmful societal bias perpetuation?
