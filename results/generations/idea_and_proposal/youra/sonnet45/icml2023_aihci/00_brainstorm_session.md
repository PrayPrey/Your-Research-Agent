## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI systems be designed to better integrate with human-computer interaction paradigms to create more effective, usable, and trustworthy tools?

### Refined Question

How can we bridge the gap between modern AI capabilities (particularly large-scale models with emergent behaviors) and human-centered design principles to develop AI-powered interfaces that are interpretable, controllable, and aligned with user needs?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Interface Understanding & Generation:** How can AI systems better understand and generate user interfaces that align with human mental models and usability principles?

2. **Human Feedback Integration:** What are effective methods for incorporating human feedback (both explicit and implicit) into AI model training and deployment to improve alignment with user intentions?

3. **Interpretability & Trust:** How can we design interaction paradigms that make AI decision-making transparent and build user trust in AI-powered tools?

4. **Personalization & Control:** What mechanisms enable users to personalize and correct AI behavior while maintaining system coherence and preventing negative adaptation?

5. **Evaluation Methods:** How should we evaluate AI systems from an HCI perspective, balancing technical performance metrics with human-centered measures like usability, satisfaction, and trust?
