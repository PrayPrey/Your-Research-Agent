## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we advance representation learning and generative models for structured/tabular data to enable better understanding, processing, and application of tables across multiple domains?

### Refined Question

What novel techniques in representation learning, generative AI, and multimodal learning can enhance the understanding, generation, and application of structured tabular data, particularly through LLM integration and domain-specific adaptations?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Representation Learning:** What new model architectures, encoding techniques, and pre-training methods can improve representation learning for semi-structured data (spreadsheets, tables, relational databases)?

2. **Generative Models & LLMs:** How can Large Language Models and diffusion models be specialized for structured data through prompt engineering, fine-tuning techniques, and multi-agent systems?

3. **Multimodal Integration:** How can structured data be effectively combined with other modalities (text, images, code, knowledge graphs) for enhanced learning and reasoning?

4. **Real-World Applications:** What techniques advance table representations for practical tasks like data preparation, retrieval, text-to-SQL, tabular ML, and query optimization?

5. **Production Challenges:** How can we address challenges in maintaining TRL models in production (data updating, error correction, privacy, domain-specific adaptations)?
