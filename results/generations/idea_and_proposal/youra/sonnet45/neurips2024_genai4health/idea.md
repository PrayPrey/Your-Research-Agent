# Compliance-as-Code for Healthcare GenAI: Automating Stakeholder Coordination Through Policy Enforcement

## Motivation
Healthcare GenAI systems handling Protected Health Information face a critical coordination bottleneck: policymakers, developers, and security experts struggle to align on HIPAA/GDPR compliance through manual meetings, emails, and periodic audits. While technical compliance mechanisms exist, translating policy into effective coordination remains underspecified. This creates deployment delays, late-stage violation detection, and incomplete audit trails—undermining trust and regulatory acceptance of GenAI in clinical settings.

## Main Idea
We propose a hybrid Compliance-as-Code framework that translates HIPAA/GDPR requirements into machine-executable policies (OPA Rego format) embedded at AI lifecycle gates (data collection, training, deployment, monitoring). The core mechanism operates through four steps: (1) policy formalization enables automated validation, (2) lifecycle gates generate real-time compliance feedback, (3) shared dashboards create asynchronous visibility replacing synchronous coordination, and (4) automated audit trails provide traceable evidence. We hypothesize this reduces stakeholder coordination time by ≥30%, detects violations 2 stages earlier (at data collection vs. deployment), and achieves ≥80% attestation completeness (vs. 40-60% baseline). Verification uses pre-post intervention design across ≥3 healthcare GenAI projects with controlled violation injection tests. Success demonstrates organizational automation—not just technical compliance—as the missing piece for trustworthy healthcare GenAI deployment.
