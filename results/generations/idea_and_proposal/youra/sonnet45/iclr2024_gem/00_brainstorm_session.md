## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we bridge the gap between generative machine learning methods and experimental biology for biomolecular design?

### Refined Question

How can generative machine learning be integrated with experimental biology to create biomolecular design systems that prioritize real-world applications over static benchmark performance?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **In-silico generative ML advancements:** What generative ML methods (inverse design, biomolecular data modeling, interpretability) can be developed to better align with experimental constraints and real-world applications?

2. **Experimental integration:** How can high-throughput data generation, adaptive experimental design, and wet lab validation be systematically integrated into generative ML workflows?

3. **Biological problem identification:** What biological problems are currently ready for ML applications, and how can we identify them systematically?

4. **Benchmarks and evaluation:** How can we develop benchmarks, datasets, and oracles that reflect real-world experimental conditions rather than just in-silico performance?

5. **Computational-experimental collaboration:** What frameworks and methodologies can facilitate effective collaboration between computationalists and experimentalists in biomolecular design?
