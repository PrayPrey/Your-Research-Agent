#### Gap 3: Interpretable Generative Models for Biomolecular Design Aligned with Biological Mechanisms

**Current State:**
- **Generative models:** Transformers (ProteinMPNN), diffusion models (RFdiffusion), GANs (MolGAN), VAEs (ChemVAE)
- **Black-box nature:** Models learn implicit representations, generate plausible structures, but reasoning is opaque
- **Validation approach:** Post-hoc analysis (attention weights, latent space visualization), not mechanistic understanding
- **Biological disconnect:** Models don't explicitly encode known biophysics (electrostatics, hydrophobic effect, steric clashes)

**Missing Piece:**
- **Mechanistic interpretability:** Why does the model generate this specific sequence/structure? What biological features drove the decision?
- **Constraint satisfaction guarantees:** Ensure generated molecules satisfy hard constraints (synthesizability, no reactive groups, stable folds)
- **Physics-informed architectures:** Integrate known biophysical principles (force fields, energy functions) into generative process
- **Failure mode diagnosis:** When a generated molecule fails experimentally, can we trace it back to model assumptions?

**Potential Impact:**
- **Scientific trust:** Biologists adopt ML tools when they understand the reasoning, not just the output
- **Failure debugging:** Rapidly identify why a design failed (e.g., hydrophobic core exposed, charged residues buried)
- **Knowledge discovery:** Learn new biological principles from model's learned representations
- **Safety:** Avoid generating toxic, immunogenic, or unstable molecules through interpretable constraints
