## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can generative AI models advance our ability to understand, predict, and design biological systems across multiple modalities (sequences, structures, graphs) and application domains (protein design, drug discovery, biological data modeling)?

### Refined Question

What are the methodological innovations and practical advances needed to effectively apply generative AI models (including diffusion models, large language models, graph neural networks, and geometric deep learning) to biological challenges spanning molecular design, biological data modeling, and AI-guided scientific discovery?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Biomolecule Design Track:** How can we improve rational protein design through better prediction and optimization of protein sequences/structures with constraints and prior knowledge? What advances are needed for small molecule drug design that incorporates biological context? What are the next frontiers in de-novo design for peptides, oligonucleotides, antibodies, and targeted degraders?

2. **Generative Modeling Track:** What are the most effective sequence-based methods for protein and genomic modeling, including large language models? How can graph-based generative learning advance our understanding of biological networks (PPI networks, molecular graphs, GWAS graphs)? What geometric deep learning approaches work best for biological structures as point clouds, surfaces, and geometric objects?

3. **Open Challenges Track:** How can large language models facilitate scientific discovery through literature summarization, information extraction, knowledge gap identification, and hypothesis formulation? What are the systematic barriers between generative AI capabilities and biological experiment design? What pressing biological challenges are difficult to address via traditional means and how can generative algorithms bridge this gap?
