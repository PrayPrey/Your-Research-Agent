## Starting Context

**Background:** While deep learning continues to achieve impressive results on an ever-growing range of tasks, our understanding of the principles underlying these successes remains largely limited. This problem is usually tackled from a mathematical point of view, aiming to prove rigorous theorems about optimization or generalization errors of standard algorithms, but so far they have been limited to overly-simplified settings. The main goal of this workshop is to promote a complementary approach that is centered on the use of the scientific method, which forms hypotheses and designs controlled experiments to test them.

**Source Type:** Workshop Call for Papers (NeurIPS 2024)

**Workshop Focus:** Empirical analyses of deep networks that can validate or falsify existing theories and assumptions, or answer questions about the success or failure of these models.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we use the scientific method to empirically understand the principles and mechanisms underlying deep learning successes through controlled experimentation?

### Refined Question

How can controlled empirical experiments on deep networks validate or falsify existing theories, reveal new phenomena, and advance our understanding of deep learning mechanisms across different architectures and application domains?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Theory Validation/Falsification:** How can we design empirical experiments to validate or falsify existing theories about deep network optimization, generalization, and representation learning?

2. **Phenomenon Discovery:** What empirical regularities and phenomena (e.g., scaling laws, emergent behaviors) can be observed in deep networks that inform theoretical understanding?

3. **Mechanistic Understanding:** How can we empirically investigate the inner workings of deep networks (attention mechanisms, inductive biases, training dynamics) to understand why they succeed or fail?

4. **Domain-Specific Insights:** How do empirical findings about in-context learning in transformers, generalization in generative models, and interpretability methods advance our understanding of specific deep learning architectures?

5. **Methodology Development:** What experimental methodologies and evaluation frameworks are most effective for conducting scientific investigations of deep learning systems?
