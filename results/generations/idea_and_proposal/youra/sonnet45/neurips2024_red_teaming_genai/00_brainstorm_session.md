## Starting Context

**Background:** With the rapid development of Generative AI, ensuring their safety, security, and trustworthiness is paramount. In response, researchers and practitioners have proposed red teaming to identify such risks, enabling their mitigation. Red teaming refers to adversarial tactics employed to identify flaws in GenAI-based systems, such as security vulnerabilities, harmful or discriminating outputs, privacy breaches, and copyright law violations.
