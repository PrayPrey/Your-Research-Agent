## Starting Context

**Background:** Both natural and artificial intelligences form representations of the world that they use to reason, make decisions, and communicate. Despite extensive research across machine learning, neuroscience, and cognitive science, it remains unclear what the most appropriate ways are to compare and align the representations of intelligent systems.

**Auto-Fill Extraction:**

The Workshop CFP already provides well-structured research questions across multiple dimensions:

1. **Core Theme Analysis:** When and why intelligence systems learn aligned representations, and how scientists/engineers can intervene
2. **Computational Strategy Exploration:** Relationship between representational alignment and shared computational strategies
3. **Measurement Methodology:** Current alignment metrics and future measurement approaches

5. **Intervention Methods:** Systematic approaches to increase/decrease representational alignment
6. **Impact Assessment:** Implications of alignment changes on behavioral and value alignment

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we better understand, measure, and control representational alignment between biological and artificial intelligence systems?

### Refined Question

What are the most effective methods for measuring and systematically intervening on representational alignment between biological and artificial intelligence systems, and what are the implications of such interventions for computational strategies, behavioral alignment, and value alignment?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. To what extent does representational alignment indicate shared computational strategies among biological and artificial systems?

2. How have current alignment metrics advanced our understanding of computation, and what measurement approaches should we explore next?

3. How can we develop more robust and generalizable measures of alignment that work across different domains and types of representations?

4. How can we systematically increase (or decrease) representational alignment among biological and artificial systems?

5. What are the implications (positive and negative) of increasing or decreasing representational alignment between systems, on behavioral alignment, value alignment, and beyond?
