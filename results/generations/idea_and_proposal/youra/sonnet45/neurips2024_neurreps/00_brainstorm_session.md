## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can symmetry, geometry, and topology be leveraged to understand and improve neural representations in both biological and artificial systems?

### Refined Question

How do geometric and topological structures in neural representations (both biological and artificial) enable computational efficiency, robustness, and generalization, and what are the unifying mathematical principles that govern these representations across substrates?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Theory and Methods**: What are the mathematical foundations for learning invariant and equivariant representations, and how can statistical learning theory be extended to incorporate topology, geometry, and symmetry?

2. **Biological Neural Systems**: How is representational geometry manifested in neural data, and what group structures exist in biological neural representations?

3. **Geometric Deep Learning**: How can equivariant architectures improve world models for robotics, and what role do dynamics play in shaping neural representations?

4. **Analysis and Applications**: How can topological data analysis and geometric methods be applied to understand generative models and language representations?

5. **Unifying Principles**: What are the connections between symmetries, dynamical systems, and learning that reveal substrate-agnostic computational strategies?
