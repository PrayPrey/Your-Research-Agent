### Initial Question

How can AI accelerate and improve drug discovery and development across multiple phases from molecular design to clinical outcomes?

### Refined Question

What are the key challenges, opportunities, and methodological innovations for applying artificial intelligence throughout the drug discovery and development pipeline, from molecular representation learning to clinical trial optimization?

2. **Drug Design & Optimization:** What AI techniques are most effective for structure-based drug design, molecule optimization, and binding affinity prediction?

4. **Data & Benchmarks:** What datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation frameworks are needed to advance AI for drug discovery research?

5. **Clinical Translation:** How can AI optimize clinical trial design while addressing regulatory requirements for drug development?
