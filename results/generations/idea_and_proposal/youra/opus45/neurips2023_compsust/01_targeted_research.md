## 1. Research Questions

### Primary Research Question

2. **Data Challenges:** How can ML methods be adapted to handle the characteristic challenges of sustainability data - including low signal-to-noise ratios, ever-changing conditions, and biased/imbalanced datasets?

3. **Multi-Stakeholder Collaboration:** What frameworks enable effective collaboration between academia, industry, and non-profits for computational sustainability, and how do different organizational contexts affect solution design and deployment?

4. **Failure Mode Identification:** What are the most common failure modes in computational sustainability projects, and how can researchers better document and share negative results to prevent duplicated effort?

5. **Impact Measurement:** What metrics and evaluation frameworks can accurately quantify the real-world impact of computational sustainability solutions beyond traditional ML benchmarks?
