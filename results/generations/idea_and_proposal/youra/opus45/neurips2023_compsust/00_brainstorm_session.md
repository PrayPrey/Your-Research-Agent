## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can computational methods effectively bridge the gap between theoretical advances and real-world deployment for sustainable development goals, and what are the key failure modes that prevent ML solutions from achieving their intended impact in sustainability domains?

### Refined Question

**Main Research Question:**
What computational approaches and methodological frameworks can successfully navigate the "theory-to-deployment" pipeline for sustainability applications, accounting for the unique challenges of noisy/biased data, complex multi-agent systems, and multi-objective optimization, while identifying and mitigating common failure modes that cause benchmark improvements to fail in practice?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Theory-to-Deployment Gap:** What are the critical transition points where computational sustainability solutions fail when moving from controlled research settings to real-world deployment, and what best practices can bridge these gaps?

2. **Data Challenges:** How can ML methods be adapted to handle the characteristic challenges of sustainability data - including low signal-to-noise ratios, ever-changing conditions, and biased/imbalanced datasets?

3. **Multi-Stakeholder Collaboration:** What frameworks enable effective collaboration between academia, industry, and non-profits for computational sustainability, and how do different organizational contexts affect solution design and deployment?

4. **Failure Mode Identification:** What are the most common failure modes in computational sustainability projects, and how can researchers better document and share negative results to prevent duplicated effort?

5. **Impact Measurement:** What metrics and evaluation frameworks can accurately quantify the real-world impact of computational sustainability solutions beyond traditional ML benchmarks?
