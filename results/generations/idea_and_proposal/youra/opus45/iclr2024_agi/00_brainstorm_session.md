## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How far are current AI systems, particularly Large Language Models, from achieving Artificial General Intelligence, and what are the key frontiers, limitations, and implications of this trajectory?

### Refined Question

**What are the fundamental capabilities, limitations, and developmental pathways that determine the proximity of current Large Language Models and foundation models to Artificial General Intelligence, and how can insights from classic AI, interdisciplinary research, and practical constraints inform this assessment?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Frontiers of AGI Research:** What advances in AI agents, embodied AI, retrieval-augmented LLMs, knowledge-enhanced AI, and multi-agent systems are most promising for AGI progress?

2. **Classic AI Insights:** How can historical methods (expert systems, symbolic AI, Type I/II reasoning) inform and complement current LLM-based approaches toward AGI?

3. **Interdisciplinary Foundations:** What insights from psychology, sociology, and neuroscience can inspire LLM development toward AGI-like capabilities?

4. **Fundamental Limitations:** What intrinsic capabilities (or lack thereof) in LLMs—particularly in reasoning and planning—may impede progression to AGI?

5. **Practical Constraints:** How do system limitations, computational costs, data acquisition barriers, and privacy concerns affect the practical path to AGI?

6. **Safety and Governance:** What moral, safety, and regulatory frameworks are needed to responsibly guide AGI development?

7. **Societal Impact:** What potential changes might AGI initiate in societies, economies, and daily lives, and how should we prepare?
