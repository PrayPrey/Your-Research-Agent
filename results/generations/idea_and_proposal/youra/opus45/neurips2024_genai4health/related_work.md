## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: The imperative for regulatory oversight of large language models (or generative AI) in healthcare (Semantic Scholar ID: 0893549771)
   - **Authors**: Meskó, Topol
   - **Summary**: Establishes theoretical foundation for cautious LLM implementation in healthcare and argues for a regulatory oversight framework for generative AI systems.
   - **Year**: 2023

2. **Title**: SPIRIT-AI Extension: Guidelines for clinical trial protocols for interventions involving AI
   - **Authors**: Cruz Rivera et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces 15 new items for AI clinical trial protocols and provides reporting standards for AI intervention trials in healthcare settings.
   - **Year**: 2020

3. **Title**: Clinical validation of STRATIFYHF: Protocol for prospective, multicentre longitudinal study (Semantic Scholar ID: 48043c1329bd15284bcc98cbfb93dbe84e1fca5c)
   - **Authors**: Charman et al.
   - **Summary**: Describes a protocol involving 1,600 participants with 24-month follow-up that validates the feasibility of multicentre prospective AI validation approaches.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: RWE-LLM Framework
   - **Authors**: Hippocratic AI
   - **Summary**: Presents the largest-scale clinician validation to date with 307,038 AI calls evaluated by 6,234 clinicians, though conducted retrospectively.
   - **Year**: Not specified

5. **Title**: Early prediction of sepsis using NAVOY Sepsis: Prospective RCT validation (Semantic Scholar ID: 10ffe80d68049bb98e874ac971fe510c4fcc6eb9)
   - **Authors**: Persson et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates feasibility of prospective AI validation in ICU settings, achieving 0.79 accuracy and 0.80 sensitivity for sepsis prediction.
   - **Year**: 2024

6. **Title**: A scoping review on generative AI and LLMs in mitigating medication related harm
   - **Authors**: Ong et al.
   - **Summary**: Conducts a comprehensive scoping review of generative AI applications for medication safety, explicitly noting the absence of prospective testing for these models.
   - **Year**: 2025

7. **Title**: Clinical trials informed framework for real world clinical implementation and deployment of AI
   - **Authors**: Not specified
   - **Summary**: Proposes a framework for translating clinical trial methodologies to inform real-world AI deployment in clinical settings.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges**
1. **Lack of Prospective Validation**: No studies have tested generative AI and LLM models prospectively in clinical settings, with existing evaluations being predominantly retrospective.
2. **Regulatory Oversight Gap**: There is an imperative need for regulatory frameworks to govern LLM implementation in healthcare, as current oversight mechanisms are insufficient.
3. **Standardized Reporting for AI Trials**: Clinical trials involving AI interventions require specialized reporting guidelines and protocol items beyond traditional trial standards.
4. **Translation from Retrospective to Real-World Settings**: While large-scale retrospective evaluations exist, frameworks for translating these findings to real-world clinical implementation remain underdeveloped.
