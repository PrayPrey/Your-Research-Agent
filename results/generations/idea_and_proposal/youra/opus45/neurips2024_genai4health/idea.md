# Research Idea

## Title
Phased Prospective Validation Protocol (PPVP) for Safe and Efficient Healthcare GenAI Deployment

## Motivation
Healthcare GenAI systems show transformative potential, yet lack standardized validation pathways that balance safety with deployment efficiency. Current ad-hoc validation approaches either expose patients to insufficiently tested AI or delay beneficial technologies indefinitely. The critical gap: no systematic framework exists to identify safety issues before patient exposure while generating regulatory-acceptable evidence. This research addresses the urgent need for a structured validation methodology that builds trust through graduated risk management.

## Main Idea
We propose PPVP, a four-phase validation protocol inspired by pharmaceutical trials: Phase 0 (retrospective baseline), Phase I (shadow mode—AI runs parallel to clinicians without patient exposure), Phase II (limited pilot with mandatory override), and Phase III (RCT for high-risk applications). The core mechanism: graduated exposure enables >90% of safety-critical issues to be identified in shadow mode before any patient interaction, while incrementally building regulatory evidence.

Key methodology: Compare PPVP against ad-hoc validation across two clinical domains, measuring safety issue identification rates, adverse events, and time-to-deployment. We predict 50% faster regulatory approval with superior safety profiles.

Expected impact: A reusable, policy-compliant framework enabling healthcare institutions to deploy GenAI systems safely, accelerating beneficial AI adoption while maintaining rigorous safety standards aligned with FDA/EMA requirements.
