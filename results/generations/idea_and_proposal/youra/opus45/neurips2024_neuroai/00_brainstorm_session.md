## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can principles from biological neural systems be leveraged to develop more efficient, interpretable, and adaptive artificial intelligence systems?

### Refined Question

How can neuro-inspired mechanisms—including spiking neural networks, predictive coding, and Hebbian plasticity—be integrated into deep learning architectures to achieve (1) improved computational efficiency in small-data regimes, (2) enhanced interpretability through alignment with biological neural processing, and (3) more robust continual learning capabilities?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Neuro-inspired Computation:** How can spiking neural networks and neuromorphic computing principles (e.g., Intel's Loihi architecture) be adapted to improve energy efficiency and real-time learning in deep learning systems?

2. **Explainability through Neuroscience:** How can hierarchical processing structures and neuro-symbolic AI approaches inspired by biological neural mechanisms enhance the interpretability of complex AI models?

3. **Self-supervised Learning:** How can predictive coding and active inference frameworks from neuroscience be implemented in AI systems to enable effective learning from unstructured data without explicit labels?

4. **Reasoning and Decision-Making:** How can distributed representations, adaptive learning, and context-sensitive architectures from cognitive neuroscience inform the development of more flexible and human-like reasoning systems?

5. **Cognitive Function Benchmarking:** What novel methods and benchmarks are needed to evaluate AI systems' emulation of human cognitive processes including language processing, problem-solving, and creativity?
