## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can the rigorous theoretical tools of causality be translated into practical methods for understanding, improving, and trusting large models, especially in the large-scale regime with heterogeneous unstructured data?

### Refined Question

How can causal inference frameworks be integrated with large foundation models to (1) understand why they work so well, (2) systematically verify and enhance their robustness and generalization capabilities, and (3) make them more interpretable and controllable for safety-critical applications?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Causality IN large models:** What causal knowledge is captured by large models, and what are their causal reasoning abilities? Can they perform causal inference implicitly?

2. **Causality FOR large models:** How can causal ideas (intervention, counterfactual reasoning, invariance) be applied to augment and improve large models' robustness and generalization?

3. **Causality WITH large models:** How can large models be leveraged to improve traditional causal inference and causal discovery tasks?

4. **Causality OF large models:** What is the causal structure of how large models work internally, and how can we make them more interpretable and controllable?
