## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

### Refined Question

How can novel information-theoretic methods and computational approaches be developed and validated to create a unified framework for understanding cognitive functions in both biological and artificial systems, enabling better human-AI collaboration?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Novel IT Approaches to Cognition:** What new information-theoretic formulations can better characterize cognitive functions such as perception, decision making, language processing, and social reasoning in both humans and AI systems?

2. **Validation Methods:** How can we develop rigorous methods and approaches for validating information-theoretic formalisms against empirical data from human cognition and artificial systems?

3. **Computation & Estimation:** What novel computational methods can improve the estimation of information-theoretic quantities (e.g., mutual information, transfer entropy) in high-dimensional cognitive data?

4. **Limitations & Challenges:** What are the fundamental limitations of applying information theory to cognitive systems, and how can these challenges be addressed?

5. **Human-AI Alignment:** How can information-theoretic principles guide the development of artificial agents that better communicate and cooperate with humans?
