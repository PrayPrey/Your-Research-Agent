## Starting Context

**Background:** The concept of "World Model" focuses on how intelligent agents can understand and model the external interactive worlds/environments to improve their decision-making and planning abilities. World models were initially focused on modelling low-level physical quantities and interactions by recurrent neural networks (RNNs). Over time, the "World Models" concept has expanded to real-world simulation (e.g. Sora and Genie) and the generation of complex, realistic, and high-dimensional environments.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we advance World Models to better understand, model, and scale predictions across language, vision, and control domains for improved agent decision-making and real-world simulation?

### Refined Question

How can novel architectural innovations (Transformers, SSMs, diffusion models) combined with multi-modal integration improve World Models' ability to capture environment dynamics, causal relationships, and spatial-temporal patterns for scalable real-world prediction and simulation?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Understanding World Rules:** How can World Models better capture environment dynamics, causal understanding, and spatial-temporal patterns? What theoretical foundations enable robust simulation and prediction?

2. **Architecture and Training:** What are the comparative strengths and limitations of Transformers, RNNs, and SSMs for world modeling? How do autoregressive training, diffusion modeling, and reinforcement learning contribute to world model capabilities?

3. **Multi-modal Scaling:** How can integrating visual, auditory, and textual data improve the realism and capability of World Models for generation and prediction?

4. **Domain Applications:** How can World Models be effectively applied to robotics, embodied AI, healthcare, and scientific domains (physics, chemistry, biology) for improved prediction and decision-making?

5. **Evaluation and Benchmarking:** What benchmarks, datasets, and evaluation methodologies are needed to assess World Model performance across diverse applications?
