## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can theories from moral philosophy and moral psychology be systematically applied to develop AI systems that embody ethical values aligned with pluralistic societal needs?

### Refined Question

**How can developmental moral psychology frameworks and pluralistic value theories from moral philosophy inform novel value alignment methodologies that address the limitations of RLHF and ensure diverse, representative values are incorporated into AI systems?**

This question is:
- **Specific:** Targets developmental moral psychology + pluralistic value theories
- **Bounded:** Focuses on value alignment methodology improvement
- **Measurable:** Success = novel methodology that addresses RLHF limitations
- **Significant:** Addresses core workshop themes and field-wide challenges

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Developmental Perspective:** What can theories of developmental moral psychology (e.g., Kohlberg, Gilligan, Turiel) teach us about structuring AI value learning as a progressive, stage-based process rather than static optimization?

2. **Pluralistic Value Integration:** How can we move beyond monolithic value alignment to incorporate diverse voices, views, and values from underrepresented groups into AI systems?

3. **Beyond RLHF:** What are theoretically-grounded alternatives to reinforcement learning from human feedback that better capture the complexity of moral reasoning (e.g., deliberative processes, moral exemplar modeling)?

4. **Value Consistency Audit:** How can we systematically assess whether values embedded in current AI systems are consistent with the pluralistic values of society at large?

5. **Bidirectional Advancement:** How can AI tools and methods contribute back to advancing the fields of moral philosophy and moral psychology themselves?
