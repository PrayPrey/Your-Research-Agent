## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges (arXiv:2104.13478)
   - **Authors**: Bronstein, Bruna, Cohen, Veličković
   - **Summary**: Provides a unified mathematical framework using group theory, fiber bundles, and gauge equivariance for understanding geometric neural networks, establishing the vocabulary for formalizing equivariance.
   - **Year**: 2021

2. **Title**: The Principle of Isomorphism: A Theory of Population Activity in Grid Cells and Beyond (arXiv:2510.02853)
   - **Authors**: Xu, Song, Shao, Si, Qin
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that population activity preserves essential mathematical structures of tasks and proves that toroidal topology emerges from path integration requirements, directly supporting manifold-group correspondence.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: From the fly connectome to exact ring attractor dynamics
   - **Authors**: Biswas, Stanoev, Romani, Fitzgerald
   - **Summary**: Derived ring attractor dynamics from biological connectivity constraints, demonstrating that SO(2) equivariant heading representation emerges from attractor structure.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: The Lie Derivative for Measuring Learned Equivariance
   - **Authors**: Gruver, Finzi, Goldblum, Wilson
   - **Summary**: Provides a principled metric for measuring equivariance with strong mathematical foundations and validated correlation between equivariance and generalization.
   - **Year**: 2022

5. **Title**: e3nn: E(3)-Equivariant Neural Networks
   - **Authors**: Not specified
   - **Summary**: Implements exact equivariance via spherical harmonics for E(3) symmetry groups, serving as a comparison baseline for performance without biological grounding.
   - **Year**: Not specified

6. **Title**: Group Equivariant Convolutional Networks
   - **Authors**: Cohen, Welling
   - **Summary**: Pioneered group equivariance in deep learning for discrete symmetries, establishing foundational architectural approaches for equivariant networks.
   - **Year**: 2016

7. **Title**: Vector-based navigation using grid-like representations
   - **Authors**: Banino et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrated that RNNs trained for path integration develop grid-like patterns, though without theoretical guarantees for the emergence mechanism.
   - **Year**: 2018

8. **Title**: Emergence of grid-like representations by training recurrent neural networks
   - **Authors**: Cueva, Wei
   - **Summary**: Showed that grid cells emerge in trained recurrent networks, though the underlying mechanism remained unclear.
   - **Year**: 2018

9. **Title**: Geometric deep learning and equivariant neural networks
   - **Authors**: Gerken et al.
   - **Summary**: Established mathematical foundations for equivariance in artificial neural networks without addressing connections to biological neural circuits.
   - **Year**: 2021

**Key Challenges**
1. **Unclear Emergence Mechanisms**: Grid cells emerge in trained recurrent neural networks, but the theoretical mechanism explaining this emergence remains unclear.
2. **Disconnect Between Artificial and Biological Systems**: Mathematical foundations for equivariance in artificial networks are well-established, but there is no connection drawn to biological neural circuits.
3. **Lack of Theoretical Guarantees**: RNNs trained for path integration develop grid-like patterns empirically, but lack theoretical guarantees explaining why these representations emerge.
4. **Missing Biological Grounding**: Existing equivariant neural network frameworks (e.g., e3nn) achieve exact equivariance through mathematical constructions but lack biological plausibility or grounding in neuroscience.
