## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can artificial intelligence and machine learning fundamentally transform the methods of scientific discovery across domains ranging from molecular biology to cosmology?

### Refined Question

How can we develop AI/ML methods that incorporate physical insights and domain knowledge to accelerate scientific discovery, specifically focusing on: (1) scaling dynamical system modeling, (2) molecular modeling and drug discovery, or (3) building infrastructure and tools for AI-driven science?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Physical AI Integration:** How can physical laws and constraints be effectively incorporated into AI/ML architectures to improve scientific modeling accuracy and generalization?

2. **Scalability for Scientific Simulation:** What AI/ML approaches can enable efficient scaling of dynamical system modeling to handle millions of particles while maintaining physical accuracy?

3. **Molecular and Biological Discovery:** How can AI/ML methods accelerate drug discovery pipelines and improve molecular modeling including de novo generation of molecules?

4. **Scientific Infrastructure:** What tools, platforms, and benchmarks are needed to democratize AI-driven scientific discovery across different domains?

5. **Cross-Domain Transfer:** How can AI methods developed for one scientific domain (e.g., cosmology) be effectively transferred or adapted to accelerate discovery in other domains (e.g., materials science)?
