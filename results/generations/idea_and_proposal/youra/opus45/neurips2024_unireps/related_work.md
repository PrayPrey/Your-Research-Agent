## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: The Platonic Representation Hypothesis (2024)
   - **Authors**: Huh, Cheung, Wang, Isola
   - **Summary**: Proposes that AI representations converge toward a shared statistical model of reality, providing theoretical foundation for why cross-modal alignment should exist.
   - **Year**: 2024

2. **Title**: Neural Thermodynamics: Entropic Forces in Deep and Universal Representation Learning (2025)
   - **Authors**: Ziyin, Xu, Chuang
   - **Summary**: Provides mathematical proof of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis through SGD-induced entropic forces, establishing mechanistic basis for using entropy as an alignment metric.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Representation Learning with Contrastive Predictive Coding (2018)
   - **Authors**: van den Oord et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces InfoNCE as a tractable lower bound on mutual information, providing foundational methodology for information-theoretic representation learning.
   - **Year**: 2018

4. **Title**: Similarity of Neural Network Representations Revisited (2019)
   - **Authors**: Kornblith et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) is effective for comparing neural network representations across different architectures and layers.
   - **Year**: 2019

5. **Title**: Representational Similarity Analysis
   - **Authors**: Kriegeskorte et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces RSA as a method for comparing representational geometries across different systems and modalities.
   - **Year**: Not specified

6. **Title**: DecAlign: Hierarchical Cross-Modal Alignment (2025)
   - **Authors**: Qian et al.
   - **Summary**: Addresses cross-modal alignment through hierarchical approaches, demonstrating that handling heterogeneity is a key requirement for effective alignment.
   - **Year**: 2025

7. **Title**: Measuring Cross-Modal Interactions in Multimodal Models (2025)
   - **Authors**: Wenderoth et al.
   - **Summary**: Shows that existing explainable AI methods fail to adequately capture cross-modal interactions in multimodal models, highlighting limitations of current approaches.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges**
1. **Fragmented Alignment Methods**: Current cross-modal alignment approaches are fragmented and lack unified frameworks for handling heterogeneity across different modalities.
2. **Inadequate Cross-Modal Metrics**: Existing explainable AI methods fail to capture cross-modal interactions effectively, demonstrating the need for better metrics specifically designed for multimodal settings.
3. **Theoretical-Practical Gap**: While theoretical foundations for representation convergence exist, translating these into practical, tractable alignment metrics remains challenging.
