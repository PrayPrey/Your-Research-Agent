## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

When, how, and why do different neural models (biological and artificial) learn the same representations when subject to similar stimuli?

### Refined Question

What are the underlying mechanisms, conditions, and theoretical principles that cause distinct neural learning systems—both biological (brains) and artificial (deep learning models)—to converge on similar internal representations, and how can this phenomenon be formally characterized and practically leveraged for model alignment, merging, and transfer?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Theoretical Understanding:** What theoretical frameworks (from neuroscience's learning dynamics and AI's identifiability analysis) can explain the emergence of similar representations across different learning systems?

2. **Conditions and Mechanisms:** Under what conditions (stimuli characteristics, architecture constraints, learning objectives) do different neural models converge to similar representations, and what mechanisms drive this convergence?

3. **Practical Applications:** How can the alignment of representations be leveraged for practical applications such as model merging, model stitching, model reuse, and multi-modal learning?

4. **Universal Invariances:** What features and invariances are universally highlighted by different learning processes, and can we identify or enforce these invariances to improve learning systems?

5. **Cross-Domain Insights:** How can insights from neuroscience (biological neural representations) inform artificial intelligence, and vice versa, in understanding representational similarity?
