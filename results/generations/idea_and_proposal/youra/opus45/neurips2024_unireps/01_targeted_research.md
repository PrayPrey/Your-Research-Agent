### Key Findings

**Research Question**: What are the underlying mechanisms, conditions, and theoretical principles that cause distinct neural learning systems to converge on similar internal representations?

**Finding 1 - Theoretical Framework Established**: The Platonic Representation Hypothesis (Huh et al., 2024, 250 citations) provides the foundational theory that AI model representations converge toward a shared statistical model of reality. This has been mathematically proven for deep linear networks via Neural Thermodynamics (Ziyin et al., 2025).

**Finding 2 - Mechanisms Identified**: SGD-induced entropic forces and symmetry breaking are key mechanisms driving representational convergence. The proof identifies 6 specific ways the Platonic convergence can be broken—providing a starting point for understanding divergence conditions.

**Finding 4 - Practical Methods Exist but Lack Theory**: Model merging methods (Representation Surgery, RECALL, Branch-and-Merge) work empirically but are not grounded in the theoretical framework. The gap between theory (PRH) and practice (merging) remains unaddressed.
