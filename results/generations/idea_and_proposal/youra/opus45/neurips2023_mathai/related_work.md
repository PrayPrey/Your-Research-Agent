## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Complex Rule Transfer Recruits Rostral Prefrontal and Ventromedial Prefrontal Neural Networks
   - **Authors**: Seger, Xie, Li, Han, Liu
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that human cognition uses dual-stream parallel processing for abstract rules combined with schema grounding, providing neuroscientific basis for dual-pathway architectures in computational systems.
   - **Year**: 2025

2. **Title**: Adaptive chunking improves effective working memory capacity
   - **Authors**: Soni & Frank
   - **Summary**: Shows that adaptive frequency control in working memory based on reinforcement learning can improve effective capacity, inspiring meta-learned cross-grounding interval mechanisms.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: DeepSeek-Prover-V2: Advancing Formal Mathematical Reasoning
   - **Authors**: Ren et al.
   - **Summary**: Achieves 88.9% on MiniF2F-test using a formal-only approach, establishing state-of-the-art performance for formal mathematical reasoning.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: FIRMA: Bidirectional Formal-Informal Mathematical Language Alignment
   - **Authors**: Not specified
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates 277.8% improvement through bidirectional translation between formal and informal mathematical languages, representing a key comparison point for translation versus maintenance paradigms.
   - **Year**: 2025

5. **Title**: FormalMATH: Benchmarking Formal Mathematical Reasoning
   - **Authors**: Yu et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces a large-scale Lean4 benchmark containing 5,560 problems, providing an evaluation resource for formal mathematical reasoning pathways.
   - **Year**: 2025

6. **Title**: ProofBench
   - **Authors**: Not specified
   - **Summary**: Reveals that foundation models achieve only 50% accuracy on formal proofs, demonstrating a substantial gap between informal reasoning success and formal verification capabilities.
   - **Year**: 2026

7. **Title**: FrontierMath
   - **Authors**: Glazer et al.
   - **Summary**: Shows that state-of-the-art models solve less than 2% of expert-level mathematical problems, highlighting the vast gap between current AI capabilities and human mathematical expertise.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**
1. **Formal-Informal Gap**: Foundation models achieve only 50% on formal proofs despite higher success on informal reasoning, indicating a substantial disconnect between informal mathematical understanding and formal verification capabilities.

2. **Expert-Level Mathematical Reasoning**: State-of-the-art models solve less than 2% of expert-level mathematical problems, revealing a vast capability gap between current AI systems and human mathematical expertise.

3. **Translation vs. Maintenance Paradigm**: Existing approaches focus on bidirectional translation between formal and informal representations rather than maintaining parallel representations, potentially limiting reasoning coherence.

4. **Dual-Stream Processing Integration**: Current systems lack architectures that mirror human cognition's dual-stream parallel processing for combining abstract rules with schema grounding in mathematical reasoning.
