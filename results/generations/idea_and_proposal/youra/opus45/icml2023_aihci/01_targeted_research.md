**4. UI Understanding/Generation Remains Underexplored:**
Despite advances in vision-language models, specific deep learning methods for understanding UI semantics and generating human-centered interfaces in the AI-HCI context are limited.

The field is rapidly evolving (80% of key papers from 2022-2025), indicating opportunity for timely contributions.
