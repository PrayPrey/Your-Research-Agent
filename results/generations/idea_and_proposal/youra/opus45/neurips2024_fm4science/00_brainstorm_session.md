## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can foundation models be effectively developed, adapted, and applied to accelerate scientific discovery while addressing the unique challenges of scientific domains that differ from traditional NLP and vision applications?

### Refined Question

**How can we develop scientific foundation models that:**
1. Scale effectively with scientific data (beyond NLP/vision scaling laws)
2. Enable cross-domain reusability and multi-modal understanding
3. Maintain alignment with scientific facts (avoiding hallucination)
4. Quantify uncertainty in scientific predictions
5. Integrate with classical scientific tools and methodologies

### Detailed Sub-Questions

**Progress-Related Questions:**
1. Is the scaling law and training strategy of scientific foundation models different from counterparts of NLP and vision?
2. Can scientific foundation models be trained once and adopted across different scenarios (reusability)?
3. Can scientific foundation models consistently outperform domain-specific models?

**Opportunity-Related Questions:**
4. How can foundation models understand multi-modal scientific inputs and solve multiple scientific problems simultaneously?
5. How can foundation models accelerate scientific discovery and data collection/assimilation?
6. How can foundation models integrate with classical scientific tools?

**Challenge-Related Questions:**
7. How can we diagnose failure cases or modes where scientific foundation models underperform?
8. How can we align scientific foundation models with scientific facts without hallucination?
9. How can we quantify the scientific uncertainty of foundation model predictions?
