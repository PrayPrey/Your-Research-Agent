## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can Large Language Models and LLM agents achieve effective embodied intelligence in open city environments, overcoming current limitations in outdoor spatial reasoning, navigation, and task execution?

### Refined Question

How can we develop and evaluate LLM-based embodied agents that demonstrate robust spatial intelligence, reasoning, planning, decision-making, and collaborative capabilities in large-scale open urban environments, bridging the gap between current indoor embodied AI achievements and the complexity of real-world outdoor scenarios?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Spatial Intelligence & Embodied Perception:** How can LLM agents develop a sense of space and time in open city environments, and what techniques can integrate spatial intelligence with embodied perception for enhanced outdoor performance?

2. **Reasoning & Planning:** How can LLM agents effectively use reasoning for decision-making in open city environments, and what strategies enable planning of action sequences while addressing inherent biases and limitations?

3. **Decision-Making & Action:** How can LLM agents make context-aware decisions in outdoor environments, and how can large language models be combined with smaller ML models for effective decision-making and action execution?

4. **Multi-Agent & Human-Agent Collaboration:** How can multiple LLM agents collaborate to achieve common goals in outdoor environments, and what strategies enable effective human-agent collaboration in open city scenarios?

5. **Evaluation Infrastructure:** What simulators, testbeds, datasets, and benchmarks are needed to effectively evaluate embodied LLM agents in city environments, and how can we establish standardized evaluation metrics?
