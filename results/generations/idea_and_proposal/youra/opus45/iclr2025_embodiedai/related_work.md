**Key Challenges**
1. **Centralized vs Decentralized Trade-offs**: Existing approaches face fundamental tensions between centralized coordination (which may not scale) and decentralized methods (which may sacrifice coordination quality).

2. **Scalability of Multi-Agent Coordination**: Current multi-agent LLM systems struggle to maintain effective coordination as the number of agents increases in complex environments.

3. **Efficient Spatial Memory for Navigation**: Managing spatial information across multiple agents in large-scale environments requires sophisticated memory architectures that balance detail with computational efficiency.

4. **Bio-Inspired Coordination in AI Systems**: Translating principles from biological swarm intelligence to artificial multi-agent systems with language models remains an open challenge.

5. **Urban-Scale Embodied Reasoning**: Evaluating and enabling effective multi-agent coordination in city-scale outdoor environments presents unique challenges compared to indoor settings.
