## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI technologies be advanced to collaborate with humans for mathematical discoveries, encompassing the full spectrum from natural language to formal proofs?

### Refined Question

**How can we develop neural models that bridge the gap between informal mathematical reasoning and formal theorem proving, enabling bidirectional translation (autoformalization and auto-informalization), automated theorem proving with consistency guarantees, novel theorem generation, and code-augmented mathematical reasoning?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Autoformalization & Auto-informalization:** How can we develop methods that improve the precision of the autoformalization process from natural language proof to formal proof, and conversely, how can we describe formal proofs in natural language effectively?

2. **Automated Theorem Proving:** How do we build consistent theorem proving systems? How do we relieve or solve the intermediate step errors in proving?

3. **Automated Theorem Generation:** Can neural models generate new and practically valid theorems? How do we take full advantage of such generated new theorems?

4. **Code Augmentation for Mathematical Reasoning:** How can the handy and plentiful code data facilitate models to conduct mathematical reasoning?

5. **Formal Verification & Code Generation:** How can progress made in AI for Math help or be directly deployed to the field of formal verification? How can AI systems be able to write provably correct code given any formal specifications?
