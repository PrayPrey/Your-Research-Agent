## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can watermarking technologies be advanced to address the emerging challenges in generative AI, including algorithmic innovation, adversarial robustness, standardized evaluation, industry adoption, and policy compliance?

### Refined Question

What are the key research challenges and opportunities in developing robust, evaluable, and practically deployable watermarking techniques for generative AI systems that satisfy both technical requirements (algorithmic advances, adversarial robustness) and societal needs (industry standards, policy compliance, ethical considerations)?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Algorithmic Advances:** What novel watermarking algorithms and applications can improve the embedding, detection, and persistence of watermarks in AI-generated content across different modalities (text, image, audio, video)?

2. **Adversarial Robustness:** How can watermarking schemes be designed to resist adversarial attacks and maintain integrity under various manipulation scenarios while preserving content quality?

3. **Evaluation & Benchmarks:** What standardized evaluation frameworks, metrics, and benchmarks are needed to fairly compare watermarking techniques and measure their real-world effectiveness?

4. **Industry Requirements:** What are the practical constraints and requirements for deploying watermarking at scale in industry settings, and how can research address these needs?

5. **Policy, Regulations & Ethics:** How should watermarking technologies align with emerging AI regulations, ethical guidelines, and societal expectations for AI transparency and accountability?
