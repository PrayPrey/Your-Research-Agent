## Starting Context

**Background:** Neural fields represent a class of coordinate-based neural networks that map spatial or spatio-temporal coordinates to field values (e.g., color, density, flow). This approach has achieved remarkable progress in visual computing, leading to more accurate, higher fidelity, more expressive, and computationally efficient solutions for problems like 3D scene reconstruction and generative modeling. While highly successful in computer vision, the application of neural fields to other domains (robotics, physics, biology, climate science) remains in early stages.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can neural fields (implicit neural representations) be extended, improved, and applied beyond traditional visual computing domains to address problems across diverse scientific and engineering disciplines?

### Refined Question

**How can we advance the theoretical foundations, architectural designs, and cross-domain applications of neural fields to enable more efficient, generalizable, and interpretable representations for spatio-temporal signals across scientific computing, robotics, and emerging domains?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Architecture & Efficiency:** How can we improve the architectures, optimization methods, and computation/memory efficiency of neural fields for practical deployment?

2. **Evaluation Metrics:** Which metrics and methods should we use to evaluate improvements to neural fields beyond traditional measures like PSNR? When are current metrics insufficient?

3. **Application Boundaries:** When should we avoid using neural fields? Are they appropriate for discrete data such as text and graphs?

4. **Novel Applications:** Which tasks can we tackle with neural fields that haven't yet been explored, particularly in robotics, physics simulation, biology, and climate science?

5. **Representation Learning:** What representations can we extract from neural fields to enable high-level information extraction and solve downstream tasks? What novel architectures are needed?

6. **Cross-Domain Transfer:** How can we facilitate exchange of ideas and collaboration across different research fields (ML, CV, robotics, physics, biology, climate science) to accelerate neural field applications?
