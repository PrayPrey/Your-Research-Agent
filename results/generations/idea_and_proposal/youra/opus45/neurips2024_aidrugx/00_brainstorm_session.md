## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI/ML methods be leveraged to accelerate the development and optimization of new drug modalities, particularly gene, RNA, and cell therapies?

### Refined Question

How can foundation models and AI-driven design approaches be developed and applied to advance new drug modalities (RNA therapeutics, cell/gene therapies, protein engineering) while addressing key challenges in molecular representation, delivery system design, and integration of multimodal biological data?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

**Application Track Questions:**
1. How can AI optimize therapeutic RNA design (e.g., UTR/codon optimization for mRNA vaccines, antisense oligonucleotide stability)?
2. How can AI enable cell and gene therapy advancement (tissue-specific regulatory elements, cell selection, CRISPR design)?
3. How can AI improve protein engineering for therapeutic applications?
4. What novel molecular representations can better capture the properties of new drug modalities?
5. How can AI design effective delivery systems (nanoparticles) for RNA/DNA therapeutics?
6. How can AI advance peptide therapeutics and microbiome-based therapies?
7. How can Foundation Models serve as agents in drug discovery workflows?

**ML Track Questions:**
1. How can Foundation Models bridge the gap between large-scale pretraining and practical drug design/target identification?
2. What new AI-driven design approaches can leverage diverse representations (primary/tertiary structures, RL methods)?
3. How can large-scale predictive/generative models incorporate biological knowledge and multimodal data (DNA/RNA/protein, 2D/3D structures)?
4. How can foundation models be effectively fine-tuned from laboratory feedback?
5. How can interpretability be enhanced in foundation models (knowledge graphs, RAG)?
6. How can foundation models handle multi-modal perturbation and readout data?
