## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can foundation models be designed and adapted to continually learn, efficiently update, and personalize their behavior in response to new information, changing environments, and individual user preferences across vision, language, and multi-modal domains?

### Refined Question

What are the key methodological advances needed to enable foundation models to perform efficient continual learning, personalized adaptation, and knowledge-augmented generation while maintaining performance across vision, language, and multi-modal applications?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Continual Weight Updates:** How can we update model weights continually to incorporate new information without catastrophic forgetting of previously learned knowledge?

2. **Efficient Fine-Tuning:** What strategies enable resource-efficient fine-tuning of large foundation models while preserving or improving task performance?

3. **Token/Prompt Tuning:** How can lightweight prompt and token modifications effectively adapt large models to specific tasks or domains without full parameter updates?

4. **In-Context/Few-Shot Learning:** What mechanisms enable models to learn effectively from limited examples within context, and how can this capability be improved?

5. **Personalized Adaptation:** How can foundation models be customized to individual user preferences, tasks, or domains while maintaining general capabilities?

6. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation:** How can external knowledge sources be optimally integrated to enhance generation quality and factual accuracy?

7. **Multimodal Learning:** What techniques enable effective fusion and transfer of knowledge across text, images, and other modalities in adaptive settings?
