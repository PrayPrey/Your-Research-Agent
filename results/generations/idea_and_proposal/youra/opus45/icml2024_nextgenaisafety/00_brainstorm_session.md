## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we ensure AI systems meet trustworthiness standards as they evolve across five emerging trends: agentic AI, multimodal systems, personalized interactions, sensitive applications, and dangerous capabilities?

### Refined Question

What are the key safety challenges and mitigation strategies for ensuring trustworthy deployment of next-generation AI systems across autonomous agents, multimodal processing, personalized interactions, high-stakes applications, and potentially dangerous capabilities?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Agentic AI Safety:** How can we ensure autonomous AI agents respect privacy, adhere to safety protocols, and prevent unintended consequences, ethical issues, and adversary exploitation?

2. **Multimodal AI Safety:** What robust guidelines and security measures can address content appropriateness, privacy, bias, and misinformation concerns in AI systems that process and generate diverse modalities (audio, video, images)?

3. **Personalized Interaction Safety:** How do we balance tailored conversational experiences with user safety, preventing data privacy breaches and echo chambers in social and personal AI interactions?

4. **Sensitive Application Safety:** How can we ensure AI systems in high-risk domains (legal, medical, mental health) enhance decision-making without compromising human expertise, preventing overreliance on automation and catastrophic errors?

5. **Dangerous Capabilities Prevention:** What safeguards can prevent AI systems from being misused to extract or generate harmful information (bioweapons, cyber attacks) while still enabling beneficial research and innovation?
