## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

Why don't deep learning approaches always deliver as expected in real-world applications, and what are the systematic patterns underlying these failures across domains?

### Refined Question

**How can we systematically identify, categorize, and understand the failure modes of deep learning models when deployed in real-world applications, and what common principles or underlying causes connect similar failures across different domains (healthcare, robotics, scientific discovery, etc.)?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Data-Related Failures:** How do data-related issues (distribution shift, bias, label quality, noisy measurements, simulated data quality) contribute to the gap between benchmark performance and real-world deployment success in deep learning systems?

2. **Model Limitations:** What model-related factors (assumption violations, robustness, interpretability, scalability, representation misalignment) most commonly cause deep learning approaches to underperform in applied settings compared to controlled experiments?

3. **Deployment Challenges:** How do deployment-specific challenges (computational demands, hardware constraints, integration complexity) affect the practical viability of deep learning solutions that showed promise in research settings?

4. **Cross-Domain Patterns:** Are there common failure patterns that emerge across different application domains (healthcare, robotics, scientific discovery, education, social sciences), and what underlying mechanisms connect these failures?

5. **Safety and Reliability:** What safety and reliability concerns emerge specifically when deploying deep learning in unpredictable real-world applications, and how do these differ from issues observed in benchmark evaluations?
