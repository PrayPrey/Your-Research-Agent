**Key Challenges**
1. **Scalability**: Current spatio-temporal graph neural network methods struggle to scale efficiently to large graphs and long temporal sequences, necessitating linear complexity solutions.
2. **Long-Range Dependency Modeling**: Existing approaches have difficulty capturing long-range temporal and spatial dependencies, limiting their effectiveness on tasks requiring extended context.
3. **Bottleneck Identification**: Graph structures contain information bottlenecks that impede long-range interactions, requiring geometric methods like curvature analysis to identify and address.
4. **Temporal Compression**: Effective mechanisms for selectively compressing temporal information while preserving important long-term patterns remain an open problem.
