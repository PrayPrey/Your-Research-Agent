## Starting Context

**Background:** The advanced generative artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as large language models and large multimodal models, are transforming many aspects of educational assessment. The integration of AI into education has the potential to revolutionize not only test development and evaluation but also the way students can learn. While some successful adoptions exist (NLP for automated scoring, collaborative filtering for predicting student responses), the rapid advances of large foundation models (ChatGPT, GPT-4, Llama, Gemini) demonstrate potential for intelligent assessment with data-driven AI systems.

**Source Type:** Workshop CFP (NeurIPS 2024 - Workshop on Large Foundation Models for Educational Assessment)

**Key Research Challenges Identified:**
- Explainability and accountability of foundation models inadequate for educational stakeholders
- Limited adoption in large-scale assessments due to trust issues
- Unclear whether LFMs can assist complex assessment tasks requiring creative thinking or high-order reasoning
- Need for collaborative efforts from AI and educational assessment researchers

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can large foundation models (LLMs and multimodal models) be effectively applied to educational assessment tasks while addressing challenges of explainability, accountability, and capability for complex reasoning?

### Refined Question

How can large foundation models be adapted and evaluated for educational assessment applications, specifically addressing: (1) their capability for complex assessment tasks requiring creative thinking and high-order reasoning, and (2) the explainability and accountability requirements of educational stakeholders?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Automated Scoring & Generation:** How can large foundation models improve automated scoring accuracy and generate high-quality assessment items while maintaining construct validity?

2. **Adaptive Assessment:** How can large foundation models enhance computerized adaptive testing and knowledge tracing to provide more personalized learning experiences?

3. **Trustworthy AI for Education:** What techniques (fairness, explainability, privacy) are needed to make large foundation models trustworthy for high-stakes educational assessments?

4. **Knowledge Augmentation & Editing:** How can external knowledge be integrated into foundation models, and how can model knowledge be edited to improve educational assessment performance?

5. **Assessment Security:** How can generative AI be leveraged to improve assessment security and accountability while mitigating risks of misuse?
