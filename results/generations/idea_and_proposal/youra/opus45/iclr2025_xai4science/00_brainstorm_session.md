## Starting Context

**Background:** Machine learning models are widely adopted despite limited understanding of why they work well or fail. The XAI4Science workshop aims to bridge the gap between understanding model behavior and using that understanding to discover new human knowledge, focusing on both theoretical interpretability methods and practical applications in weather/climate, healthcare, and material science.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can explainable AI and interpretability methods be leveraged not just to understand model behavior, but to actively discover new scientific knowledge across domains like weather/climate, healthcare, and material science?

### Refined Question

How can we develop and apply XAI methods that transform machine learning models from opaque prediction tools into instruments of scientific discovery, enabling researchers to extract actionable domain knowledge from model behavior in weather/climate, healthcare, and material science applications?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Ante-hoc Interpretability:** How can we design a-priori (ante-hoc) interpretable and self-explainable models that inherently reveal the scientific principles driving their predictions?

2. **Post-hoc Attribution Methods:** How can a-posteriori (post-hoc) interpretability and attribution methods be developed and validated to accurately explain model behavior, and how do we evaluate the accuracy of these explanations?

3. **Weather/Climate Knowledge Discovery:** How can XAI techniques be applied to climate and weather models to reveal previously unknown atmospheric/oceanic patterns or mechanisms?

4. **Healthcare Knowledge Discovery:** How can interpretability methods extract novel biomedical insights from healthcare ML models while ensuring clinical validity and trust?

5. **Material Science Knowledge Discovery:** How can XAI approaches uncover new structure-property relationships or design principles from materials informatics models?

### So What Test

**Significance:**
- **Scientific Impact:** Bridging XAI and scientific discovery addresses a fundamental challenge - making ML models tools for advancing human knowledge rather than black-box predictors
- **Practical Impact:** Climate change, disease understanding, and new materials development are pressing societal challenges where XAI-driven insights could accelerate breakthroughs
- **Community Impact:** Workshop venue (ICLR 2025) validates research relevance; brings together interpretability researchers and domain scientists
- **Methodological Impact:** Developing XAI methods that produce scientifically meaningful explanations requires new evaluation frameworks beyond standard ML metrics

### Feasibility Check

**Assessment:**
- **Data Availability:** Public climate datasets (ERA5, CMIP6), healthcare datasets (MIMIC, imaging benchmarks), and materials databases (Materials Project) are available
- **Method Maturity:** Core XAI techniques exist (SHAP, attention, concept models) but need adaptation for scientific discovery contexts
- **Evaluation Challenge:** Validating that XAI-extracted insights represent genuine scientific knowledge is non-trivial but addressable through domain expert collaboration
- **Scope:** Focusing on one application domain initially (e.g., climate science) while developing transferable methodology is realistic

**Feasibility Rating:** HIGH - Well-defined problem space with available resources and clear research directions
