## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI/deep learning methods advance nucleic acid (RNA/DNA) research across structure prediction, functional analysis, therapeutic design, and genomic data analysis?

### Refined Question

How can novel deep learning architectures and foundation models improve the accuracy and efficiency of RNA tertiary structure prediction, nucleic acid interaction modeling, and therapeutic nucleic acid design?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Structure & Function:** How can we advance RNA secondary and tertiary structure prediction using deep learning, and what novel architectures can capture the complex folding dynamics of nucleic acids?

2. **Foundation Models:** How can we develop effective (multimodal) foundation models for nucleic acids that capture sequence-structure-function relationships, and what pre-training strategies are most suitable for NA data?

3. **Generative Design:** How can generative models (diffusion models, VAEs, autoregressive models) be adapted or developed to design novel RNA/DNA sequences with specific therapeutic properties or desired structures?

4. **NA Interactions:** How can AI methods better model and predict nucleic acid interactions (RNA-RNA, RNA-protein, DNA-protein) to understand cellular processes and enable drug discovery?

5. **Genomic Analysis:** How can deep learning improve genome reconstruction, variant calling, gene expression analysis, and single-cell transcriptomics/genomics workflows?
