## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can AI and machine learning technologies be designed, developed, and deployed specifically for children's unique needs across healthcare, psychology, and education domains?

### Refined Question

How can we develop novel AI methodologies (including deep learning, LLMs, foundation models, and embodied AI) that are specifically tailored to address children's developmental, health, and educational needs while ensuring safety, appropriateness, and effectiveness for pediatric populations?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **AI for Pediatric Healthcare:** How can AI/ML methods improve early diagnosis, prediction, and intervention for childhood diseases, and what unique challenges arise when applying these technologies to pediatric data?

2. **AI for Child Psychology & Development:** How can LLMs and generative AI support children's mental health and developmental milestones while ensuring psychological safety and age-appropriate interactions?

3. **AI for Education:** How can AI-powered educational tools be designed to adapt to individual children's learning styles, developmental stages, and cognitive abilities?

4. **AI Safety & Ethics for Children:** What frameworks and methodologies are needed to assess and mitigate risks associated with AI applications targeting children?

5. **AI for Low-Resource Settings:** How can AI technologies be leveraged to bridge gaps in healthcare, education, and developmental support for children in resource-constrained environments?
