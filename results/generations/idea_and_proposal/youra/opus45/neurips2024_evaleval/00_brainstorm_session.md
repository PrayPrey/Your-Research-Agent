## Starting Context

**Background:** Generative AI systems are becoming increasingly prevalent in society, producing text, images, audio, and video content with far-reaching implications. While the NeurIPS Broader Impact statement has notably shifted norms for AI publications to consider negative societal impact, no standard exists for approaching these impact assessments. This workshop addresses this critical gap by bringing together experts on evaluation science and practitioners who develop and analyze technical systems.

## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we develop standardized, community-driven evaluation frameworks for measuring the broader societal impacts of Generative AI systems?

### Refined Question

How can we establish comprehensive, participatory frameworks for evaluating the broader societal impacts of Generative AI systems that incorporate diverse stakeholder perspectives beyond traditional ML/AI expertise, while creating standardized practices and policy recommendations for social impact assessment?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Methodology Sharing:** What existing methodologies and findings from evaluation science can be effectively applied to assessing Generative AI societal impacts, and how can these be shared with the NeurIPS community?

2. **Community-Built Evaluations:** How can we develop effective community-driven evaluation approaches that leverage collective expertise for more comprehensive AI impact assessments?

3. **Adoption Barriers:** What are the primary barriers preventing broader adoption of social impact evaluation practices for Generative AI systems, and how can these be addressed?

4. **Policy Development:** What policy recommendations should guide future investment and development of social impact evaluation frameworks for Generative AI?

5. **Standardization Framework:** How can we create a documentation and standardization framework for evaluation practices that ensures consistency, reproducibility, and broad applicability across different Generative AI applications?
