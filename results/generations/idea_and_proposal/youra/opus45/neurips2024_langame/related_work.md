## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Compositionality and Generalization in Emergent Languages (2020)
   - **Authors**: Chaabouni, Kharitonov, Bouchacourt, Dupoux, Baroni
   - **Summary**: Introduced topological similarity metrics to quantify compositionality in emergent languages and demonstrated that compositional languages exhibit better generalization capabilities.
   - **Year**: 2020

2. **Title**: Internal and External Pressures on Language Emergence (2020)
   - **Authors**: Rodriguez Luna, Ponti, Hupkes, Bruni
   - **Summary**: Investigated how functional pressures such as least effort and object constancy influence emergent languages, finding that these pressures produce languages with improved generalization capabilities.
   - **Year**: 2020

3. **Title**: Accelerating language emergence by functional pressures (2023)
   - **Authors**: Vithanage et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrated that sparse grounding pressures expedite the emergence of structured language and introduced differentiable functional pressure losses for training.
   - **Year**: 2023

4. **Title**: Measuring and Narrowing the Compositionality Gap in Language Models (2023)
   - **Authors**: Press, Zhang, Min, Schmidt, Smith, Lewis
   - **Summary**: Identified a compositionality gap in LLMs where models fail to combine known concepts, and showed that elicitive prompting can partially address this limitation.
   - **Year**: 2023

5. **Title**: Emergent Communication Survey (2020)
   - **Authors**: Lazaridou, Baroni
   - **Summary**: Provided a comprehensive survey of emergent communication research, demonstrating that existing metrics work for small agents while noting scalability challenges when applying them to LLMs.
   - **Year**: 2020

6. **Title**: SPIN (2024)
   - **Authors**: Chen et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrated that self-play mechanisms can improve LLM performance, though the approach lacks metrics for measuring language properties.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**
1. **Scalability of Compositionality Metrics**: Existing metrics for measuring compositionality in emergent communication work well for small agents but face significant challenges when scaling to large language models.
2. **Lack of Language Property Metrics in Self-Play**: Current self-play approaches for improving LLMs do not incorporate metrics to measure or track changes in language properties during training.
3. **Compositionality Gap in LLMs**: Large language models exhibit a compositionality gap where they fail to combine known concepts effectively, indicating limitations in their compositional reasoning abilities.
4. **Measuring Representation Change**: Standard task performance metrics capture behavioral accuracy but do not measure underlying changes in model representations.
