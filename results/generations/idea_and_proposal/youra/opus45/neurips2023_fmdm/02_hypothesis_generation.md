## 1. Clarified Hypothesis

### 1.1 Core Statement

**Hypothesis ID:** H-DPVLA-v1
**Confidence Level:** 0.78

**Main Hypothesis:**
Under vision-language decision-making tasks (robotics, games, dialogue), if we employ a dual-process architecture separating VLM intuition (System 1 proposer) from RL-optimized deliberation (System 2), then task success rate, sample efficiency, and interpretability will improve compared to end-to-end monolithic approaches, because the explicit separation enables better credit assignment and leverages complementary strengths of pretrained knowledge and learned reasoning.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H0):**
There is no significant difference in task success rate, sample efficiency, or interpretability between dual-process architectures and end-to-end monolithic VLM-RL approaches; any observed improvements are attributable to increased model capacity or training compute rather than architectural separation.

### 1.3 Causal Mechanism

**Causal Chain (N=4 steps):**

```
Step 1: VLM System 1 generates K diverse proposals
    ↓ (because VLM's broad pretraining enables pattern recognition)
Step 2: Proposal diversity enables better exploration
    ↓ (because multiple candidates allow comparison and ranking)
Step 3: RL deliberator learns to reason about proposals via CoT
    ↓ (because explicit reasoning enables intermediate step optimization)
Step 4: Better action selection leads to task success
    ↓ (because correct decisions compound over episode steps)
[Outcome]: Improved success rate, sample efficiency, interpretability
```

**Evidence for Causal Links:**

| Link | Evidence Source | Key Finding | Strength |
|------|-----------------|-------------|----------|
| Step 1 → Step 2 | Zhai et al. 2024 | 7B VLMs outperform GPT-4V on decision tasks with CoT | Strong |
| Step 2 → Step 3 | iRe-VLA (ICRA 2025) | Iterative RL+SL stabilizes VLA training | Strong |
| Step 3 → Step 4 | Zhai et al. 2024 | Removing CoT significantly degrades performance | Strong |
| Step 4 → Outcome | VLA-R1 (2025) | RLVR with CoT achieves superior generalization | Medium |

**Key Tension:**
- **Tension:** Zhai et al. 2024 demonstrates end-to-end VLM-RL with CoT works well, suggesting monolithic approaches may suffice. However, iRe-VLA 2025 shows training instability with direct RL on large VLAs.
- **Resolution:** This verification plan tests whether explicit architectural separation (dual-process) provides stability and interpretability benefits beyond what end-to-end approaches achieve.

### 1.4 Key Assumptions

### 1.5 Scope & Boundaries

**Where Hypothesis Applies:**
- Vision-language decision tasks with discrete or discretizable actions
- Domains: Robotics manipulation (RoboCasa), text-based games (ALFWorld), open-world games (Minecraft)
- Model scale: 7B+ parameter VLMs with sufficient reasoning capability

**Where Hypothesis Does NOT Apply:**
- Pure language tasks (use RLHF instead)
- Pure vision tasks without language grounding
- Continuous control requiring high-frequency actions (>10Hz)

**Known Limitations:**
- Sequential System 1→2 differs from parallel human cognition (engineering simplification)
- Proposal quality bounded by VLM's pretrained knowledge distribution

### 1.6 Testable Predictions

**Primary Prediction:**
**P1 (Task Success Rate):**
DP-VLA will achieve task success rate > 75% on ALFWorld benchmark, outperforming end-to-end VLM-RL baseline by ≥10 percentage points.

*Measurement:*
- Success rate measured over 100 held-out evaluation episodes
- Statistical test: Two-proportion z-test, p < 0.05

*Success Criteria for Phase 2B:*
- Primary: Success rate > 75% AND improvement ≥ 10pp over baseline (p < 0.05)
- Falsification: Success rate ≤ 65% OR no significant improvement triggers rejection

**Secondary Predictions:**

**P2 (Sample Efficiency):**
DP-VLA will require ≤50% of environment interactions to reach 70% success rate compared to end-to-end baseline.

**P3 (Interpretability):**
Human evaluators will rate DP-VLA's CoT reasoning ≥4.0/5.0 on relevance and coherence.

**Falsification Criteria:**
The hypothesis will be **REJECTED** if any occur:
1. **Primary Failure:** Success rate ≤ 65%
2. **Mechanism Failure:** No significant difference in sample efficiency
3. **Comparative Failure:** DP-VLA performs equivalent to or worse than baseline on all metrics
4. **Interpretability Failure:** CoT reasoning rated ≤3.0/5.0

### 1.8 Statistical Verification Design

**Sample Size:** n ≥ 100 episodes per condition
**Statistical Tests:** Two-proportion z-test, paired t-test, Mann-Whitney U
**Significance:** α = 0.05 (two-tailed)
**Report Format:** Effect size, 95% CI, p-value
