## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Deep learning for knowledge tracing in learning analytics: an overview
   - **Authors**: Casalino, G., Grilli, L., Limone, P., et al.
   - **Summary**: Provides a comprehensive overview of Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) architectures and validates DKT as an effective approach for predicting student learning outcomes.
   - **Year**: 2021

2. **Title**: Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques
   - **Authors**: Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K.A., Marsh, E.J., et al.
   - **Summary**: Meta-analysis that rates distributed practice as a HIGH utility learning technique, establishing the theoretical foundation for the spacing effect in educational contexts.
   - **Year**: 2013

3. **Title**: Optimizing song retention through the spacing effect
   - **Authors**: Katz, J.J., Ando, M., Wiseheart, M.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that the spacing effect persists at 3-week retention intervals and generalizes beyond simple word lists to more complex learning tasks.
   - **Year**: 2021

4. **Title**: Problems With LLMs for Learner Modelling
   - **Authors**: Hooshyar, D., et al.
   - **Summary**: Shows that DKT outperforms LLMs for learner modeling (AUC=0.83 vs. lower) and recommends hybrid frameworks that incorporate dedicated learner modeling components.
   - **Year**: 2025

5. **Title**: From MOOC to MAIC
   - **Authors**: Yu, J., Zhang, Z., et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates large-scale LLM tutoring deployment with over 100K records, providing a baseline for non-spaced LLM tutoring systems.
   - **Year**: 2024

6. **Title**: New Era of Artificial Intelligence in Education
   - **Authors**: Kamalov, F., Calonge, D.S., Gurrib, I.
   - **Summary**: Identifies the lack of longitudinal effectiveness studies as a critical research gap in AI-based educational systems.
   - **Year**: 2023

7. **Title**: Training LLM-based Tutors for Student Learning Outcomes
   - **Authors**: Scarlatos, A., et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that LLMs can be optimized for learning outcomes but focuses primarily on short-term dialogue interactions rather than long-term retention.
   - **Year**: 2025

**Key Challenges**
1. **LLM Limitations in Learner Modeling**: LLMs underperform compared to dedicated knowledge tracing models (DKT) for accurately modeling learner knowledge states, necessitating hybrid approaches.
2. **Lack of Longitudinal Effectiveness Studies**: Current AI education research lacks studies examining long-term learning outcomes and retention, with most work focusing on immediate performance.
3. **Short-term Focus of LLM Tutoring**: Existing LLM-based tutoring systems are optimized for short-term dialogue interactions rather than sustained learning and knowledge retention over time.
4. **Integration of Spacing Effect**: Despite strong evidence for the effectiveness of distributed practice, current LLM tutoring systems do not systematically incorporate spaced repetition principles.
