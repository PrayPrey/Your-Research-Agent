## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Gaze-Based Intention Estimation: Principles, Methodologies, and Applications in HRI
   - **Authors**: Belardinelli
   - **Summary**: Comprehensive review establishing the empirical basis for gaze-intention relationships, demonstrating that eye movements are highly anticipatory of task steps (300-800ms ahead).
   - **Year**: 2023

2. **Title**: Automated construction of cognitive maps with visual predictive coding
   - **Authors**: Gornet & Thomson
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that predictive coding with self-attention effectively processes sequential sensory input, with prediction errors driving representation updates.
   - **Year**: 2024

3. **Title**: CogDPM: Diffusion Probabilistic Models via Cognitive Predictive Coding (arXiv:2405.02384)
   - **Authors**: Chen et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces a precision weighting mechanism that allocates attention based on prediction confidence, demonstrating the application of predictive coding principles in deep learning.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: GazeIntent: Adapting dwell-time selection in VR interaction with real-time intent modeling (arXiv:2404.13829)
   - **Authors**: Narkar et al.
   - **Summary**: Achieved F1=0.94 for gaze-based intent prediction, validating the feasibility of ML-based gaze intention inference in VR environments.
   - **Year**: 2024

5. **Title**: Manual-Free Gaze Interaction via Bayesian-Based Implicit Intention Prediction
   - **Authors**: Jo et al.
   - **Summary**: Presents a state-of-the-art Bayesian approach for gaze-based intention prediction in XR environments.
   - **Year**: 2025

6. **Title**: A Practical, Robust, Accurate Gaze-Based Intention Inference Method for Everyday HRI
   - **Authors**: Xu et al.
   - **Summary**: Proposes a classification-based approach for gaze intention inference in human-robot interaction scenarios.
   - **Year**: 2023

7. **Title**: Attend-and-Excite
   - **Authors**: Not specified
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates attention manipulation in vision models without gaze integration, highlighting opportunities for gaze-guided attention mechanisms.
   - **Year**: Not specified

**Key Challenges**
1. **Classification-Based Limitations**: Current gaze-based intention systems are primarily classification-based, lacking continuous prediction approaches that could provide more nuanced and temporally-aware intention estimation.
2. **Absence of Gaze Integration in Attention Models**: Existing attention manipulation methods in vision models do not incorporate gaze information, representing a missed opportunity for leveraging natural human attention signals to guide model attention.
