## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can eye gaze data be effectively integrated into machine learning systems to bridge human cognition and artificial intelligence?

### Refined Question

How can eye gaze signals be leveraged as cost-efficient supervision mechanisms in machine learning to improve human-AI interaction, enhance model interpretability, and enable AI systems to predict and align with human attentional patterns and intentions?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Attention Correlation:** What is the relationship between computational attention mechanisms (e.g., transformer attention) and biological eye-gaze patterns, and how can this correlation be exploited to improve model design?

2. **Gaze-Based Supervision:** How can eye-tracking data be used as weak supervision signals for training machine learning models, particularly in annotation-expensive domains like medical imaging and autonomous driving?

3. **Human Intention Inference:** What methods can effectively infer human intentions, goals, and cognitive states from eye gaze patterns in real-time human-AI collaborative environments?

4. **Feature Importance via Gaze:** How can unsupervised learning techniques leverage eye gaze information to identify feature importance and perform feature selection without explicit labels?

5. **Ethical Gaze AI:** What are the privacy implications and ethical considerations when deploying eye-tracking technology in AI systems, and how can these be addressed while maintaining utility?

### So What Test

**Significance:** This research direction addresses a fundamental challenge in AI development - making AI systems that understand and align with human cognitive processes. Eye gaze provides a unique window into human attention and intention that is:
- Non-invasive and increasingly accessible (modern devices include eye tracking)
- Rich in information about cognitive states
- Applicable across diverse domains (healthcare, transportation, entertainment)
- Critical for trustworthy and interpretable AI systems

The emergence of immersive technologies (AR/VR) makes this research timely and impactful, as these platforms inherently capture gaze data at scale.

### Feasibility Check

**Assessment:** This research direction is highly feasible:
- **Data Availability:** Large-scale eye-tracking datasets exist; new devices make collection easier

- **Clear Metrics:** Performance can be measured through prediction accuracy, annotation efficiency, and human evaluation

**Potential Challenges:**
- Cross-domain generalization of gaze patterns
- Individual differences in gaze behavior
