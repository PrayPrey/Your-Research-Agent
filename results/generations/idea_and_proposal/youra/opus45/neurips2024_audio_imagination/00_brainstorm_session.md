## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can generative AI advance audio generation capabilities across speech, music, and sound modalities while addressing the unique challenges of audio signals, human perception, and cross-modal relationships?

### Refined Question

What novel architectures, training paradigms, and evaluation methods can push the boundaries of audio generation in terms of quality, controllability, multimodal coherence, and responsible deployment?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Text-to-Audio Generation:** How can text-to-speech, text-to-music, and text-to-sound systems be improved for higher fidelity, better controllability, and more natural outputs?

2. **Audio in Large Language Models:** What are effective approaches for integrating audio/speech capabilities into LLMs and Multimodal LLMs, and how do audio generation patterns relate to text generation?

3. **Cross-Modal Audio Generation:** How can video-to-audio, multimodal inputs, and synchronized audio-visual generation be achieved with high coherence and temporal alignment?

4. **Audio Data and Evaluation:** What datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation metrics are needed to properly assess generated audio quality, including perceptual and semantic aspects?

5. **Generative Methods for Audio Tasks:** How can generative approaches enhance established audio tasks such as speech enhancement, source separation, voice conversion, and speech-to-speech translation?

6. **Spatial and Immersive Audio:** What methods enable generation of spatial audio experiences for VR/AR applications with proper acoustic modeling?

7. **Responsibility and Interpretability:** How can we ensure responsible development of audio generation technologies with appropriate interpretability, bias mitigation, and misuse prevention?
