## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we close the gap between advances in machine learning and the practical needs of practitioners and policy makers working in global public health, particularly learning from the COVID-19 pandemic experience?

### Refined Question

**What methodological, data, and translational barriers prevented machine learning from achieving greater impact during the COVID-19 pandemic, and how can these barriers be systematically addressed to improve ML's effectiveness in future global health challenges?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Lessons from COVID-19:** What specific machine learning approaches succeeded or failed during the pandemic, and what factors determined their real-world applicability?

2. **ML Suitability Assessment:** For which types of global health questions is machine learning well-suited, and for which questions are alternative approaches more appropriate?

3. **Current Limitations & Solutions:** What are the primary barriers (technical, data, institutional) limiting ML applications in global health, and what evidence-based solutions exist?

4. **Proactive Health Applications:** How can machine learning be leveraged to promote public health, prepare for future pandemics, and address health inequalities?

5. **Data Infrastructure Requirements:** What data collection and sharing practices would enable more effective machine learning applications in global health contexts?
