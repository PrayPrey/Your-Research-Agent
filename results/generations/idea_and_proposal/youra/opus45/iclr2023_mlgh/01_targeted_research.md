## 9. Conclusion

### Key Findings

**Research Question**: What barriers prevented ML from achieving greater impact during COVID-19, and how can they be addressed?

**Finding 1: Translation Gap is the Core Barrier**
The primary barrier is not technical capability but the translation gap between ML development and public health deployment. Zhang et al. (2022, 321 citations) document how the field focused on model development while neglecting deployment infrastructure, data pipelines, and clinical integration pathways.

**Finding 2: Critical Data Factors Were Systematically Ignored**
COVID-19 prediction models failed to incorporate crucial contextual factors. Santosh (2020, 129 citations) identifies six unexploited factors: hospital capacity, test rates, demographics, population density, vulnerable populations, and poverty. This represents a fundamental methodological gap.

**Finding 3: Equity and Bias Remain Unaddressed**
ML systems trained on Global North data show algorithmic bias when deployed in Global South contexts (Ugar, 2025). Fairness frameworks exist (Raza, 2023) but were not systematically applied during pandemic response.
