**Key Challenges**
1. **Unexploited Contextual Factors**: COVID-19 prediction models have failed to incorporate critical factors such as hospital capacity, test rates, demographics, population density, vulnerable populations, and poverty levels.
2. **Data Scarcity in Low-Resource Regions**: Behavioral and health prediction in data-scarce contexts requires novel approaches like transfer learning and spatial embeddings to overcome limited local data availability.
3. **Algorithmic Bias in Health ML**: Machine learning systems in healthcare exhibit systematic biases that undermine public health equity goals.
4. **Global North Data Bias**: Healthcare AI systems are predominantly trained on Global North data, creating significant performance gaps when deployed in African and other underrepresented regions, necessitating transfer learning solutions.
