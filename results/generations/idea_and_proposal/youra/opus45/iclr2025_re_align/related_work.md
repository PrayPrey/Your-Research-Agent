## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: Getting aligned on representational alignment
   - **Authors**: Sucholutsky et al.
   - **Summary**: Provides a foundational framework for representational alignment research, identifying intervention as an open problem in the field.
   - **Year**: 2023

2. **Title**: Predictive Coding algorithms induce brain-like responses in Artificial Neural Networks
   - **Authors**: Gütlin & Auksztulewicz
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that predictive coding objectives create brain-like mismatch responses in artificial neural networks, establishing the viability of PC in modern deep networks.
   - **Year**: 2025

3. **Title**: Towards the Training of Deeper Predictive Coding Neural Networks
   - **Authors**: Qi et al.
   - **Summary**: Shows that precision-weighted optimization enables predictive coding training in deep networks, addressing scalability challenges.
   - **Year**: 2025

4. **Title**: Predictive coding is a consequence of energy efficiency in recurrent neural networks
   - **Authors**: Ali et al.
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that predictive coding emerges from energy efficiency optimization, providing a biological basis for principle-based approaches to neural network design.
   - **Year**: 2021

5. **Title**: Privileged representational axes in biological and artificial neural networks
   - **Authors**: Khosla et al.
   - **Summary**: Shows that training on natural inputs creates privileged axes in neural representations, serving as a baseline for understanding how training affects alignment.
   - **Year**: 2024

6. **Title**: Equivalence between RSA, CKA, and CCA
   - **Authors**: Williams
   - **Summary**: Provides mathematical unification of alignment metrics, establishing a methodological foundation for measuring representational similarity.
   - **Year**: 2024

7. **Title**: Correcting Biased CKA Measures in Biological and Artificial Neural Networks
   - **Authors**: Murphy et al.
   - **Summary**: Introduces debiased CKA methods required for valid brain-AI comparisons, addressing measurement reliability concerns.
   - **Year**: 2024

8. **Title**: Conclusions about Neural Network to Brain Alignment are Profoundly Impacted by the Similarity Measure
   - **Authors**: Soni & Khosla
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates that metric choice significantly affects conclusions about neural alignment, motivating the use of multiple metrics such as CKA and RSA.
   - **Year**: 2024

**Key Challenges**
1. **Intervention as an Open Problem**: Current representational alignment research lacks established methods for actively intervening to improve alignment, with most work focusing on measurement rather than modification.

2. **Scalability of Predictive Coding**: Training predictive coding networks at depth comparable to modern deep learning architectures has been challenging, requiring specialized techniques like precision-weighted optimization.

3. **Measurement Reliability**: Standard alignment metrics like CKA exhibit biases that can invalidate brain-AI comparisons, necessitating debiased measurement approaches.

4. **Metric Sensitivity**: Conclusions about neural network to brain alignment vary substantially depending on the similarity measure used, requiring multi-metric approaches to ensure robust findings.
