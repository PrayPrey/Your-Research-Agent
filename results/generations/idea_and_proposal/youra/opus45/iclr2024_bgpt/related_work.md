## Related Work

**Related Papers**
1. **Title**: In-context Learning and Induction Heads
   - **Authors**: Olsson et al.
   - **Summary**: Identifies induction heads as a key mechanism underlying in-context learning and documents their emergence at specific training points during model development.
   - **Year**: 2022

2. **Title**: The Evolution of Statistical Induction Heads (arXiv:2402.11004)
   - **Authors**: Edelman, Goel, Malach, Tsilivis
   - **Summary**: Demonstrates multi-phase learning dynamics progressing from uniform to unigram to bigram distributions, with rapid phase transitions to in-context learning solutions.
   - **Year**: 2024

3. **Title**: Differential learning kinetics govern the transition from memorization to generalization (arXiv:2412.00104)
   - **Authors**: Nguyen & Reddy
   - **Summary**: Establishes a memorization scaling law that determines the task diversity threshold required for models to transition from memorization to generalization.
   - **Year**: 2024

4. **Title**: Unified theoretical framework for wide neural network learning dynamics
   - **Authors**: Avidan, Li, Sompolinsky
   - **Summary**: Proposes a theoretical framework identifying two-phase learning dynamics consisting of gradient-driven and diffusive phases in wide neural networks.
   - **Year**: 2025

5. **Title**: New Evidence of Two-Phase Learning Dynamics
   - **Authors**: Zhou, Yang, Sugiyama, Yan
   - **Summary**: Introduces the Chaos Effect (CSR) and Cone Effect (NCA) as indicators of phase transitions in finite-width neural networks.
   - **Year**: 2025

6. **Title**: Understanding Emergent Abilities from Loss Perspective
   - **Authors**: Du et al.
   - **Summary**: Proposes loss-based methods for predicting the emergence of capabilities in language models.
   - **Year**: 2024

7. **Title**: Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models
   - **Authors**: Kaplan et al.
   - **Summary**: Establishes foundational scaling laws relating model performance to compute, data, and parameter count (Chinchilla approach).
   - **Year**: 2020

8. **Title**: Attention entropy dynamics during training
   - **Authors**: Zhai et al.
   - **Summary**: Investigates attention entropy collapse phenomena during transformer training.
   - **Year**: 2023

9. **Title**: Attention entropy dynamics
   - **Authors**: Hong & Lee
   - **Summary**: Studies entropy dynamics in attention mechanisms during the training process.
   - **Year**: 2025

10. **Title**: TransformerLens documentation
    - **Authors**: Not specified
    - **Summary**: Provides mechanistic interpretability tools for analyzing transformer models.
    - **Year**: Not specified

**Key Challenges**
1. **Descriptive vs Predictive Gap**: Existing work such as Olsson et al. describes when induction heads emerge but does not provide methods to predict their emergence in advance.
2. **Lack of Unified Prediction Framework**: Current approaches including loss-based prediction and scaling laws offer separate perspectives but lack integration into a comprehensive predictive framework for capability emergence.
3. **Understanding Phase Transition Mechanisms**: While multiple works identify phase transitions in learning dynamics, the precise mechanisms governing these transitions and their relationship to emergent capabilities remain incompletely understood.
