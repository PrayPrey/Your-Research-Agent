**Key Challenges**
1. **Unexplained Alignment Phenomenon**: While empirical universality of representation alignment has been observed across neural networks, the underlying theoretical explanation for why this alignment occurs remains unclear.

2. **CKA Sensitivity Limitations**: CKA, despite being widely used for measuring representational similarity, exhibits sensitivity to transformations that preserve linear separability, requiring careful consideration when using it as a similarity metric.

3. **Gap Between Observation and Theory**: Current work documents representation alignment empirically but lacks a unified theoretical framework connecting loss landscape structure (star-shaped connectivity) with the emergence of universal representations.
