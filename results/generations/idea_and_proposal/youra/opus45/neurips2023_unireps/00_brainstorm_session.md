## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How and why do different neural models (biological and artificial) converge on similar internal representations when exposed to similar stimuli, and what are the theoretical foundations and practical implications of this representational convergence?

### Refined Question

What are the underlying mechanisms, conditions, and extent of representational similarity across distinct neural models, and how can we leverage this understanding to develop unified frameworks for model merging, knowledge transfer, and cross-modal learning?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **When (Emergence Patterns):** Under what conditions do representational similarities emerge across different neural models, and what metrics can reliably measure these similarities?

2. **Why (Underlying Causes):** What are the theoretical foundations explaining why neural representations converge, considering both learning dynamics in biological systems and identifiability constraints in artificial systems?

3. **What For (Applications):** How can representational alignment be exploited for practical applications including model merging, stitching, reuse, efficient fine-tuning, and knowledge transfer across modalities?

4. **Theoretical Foundations:** How do concepts like symmetry, equivariance, linear mode connectivity, and disentanglement relate to representational similarity?

5. **Cross-Domain Bridge:** What insights from neuroscience can inform AI representation learning, and vice versa?
