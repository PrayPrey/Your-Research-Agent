## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can theoretical tools from statistics, information theory, optimization, and learning theory be applied to understand, improve, and responsibly deploy foundation models?

### Refined Question

**What are the fundamental theoretical principles underlying the success of foundation models, and how can these principles be leveraged to develop more efficient, responsible, and interpretable large-scale AI systems?**

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Efficiency:** What theoretical frameworks can guide the development of computationally efficient training, fine-tuning, and inference algorithms for foundation models while preserving model capabilities?

2. **Compression & Distillation:** What are the information-theoretic limits of model compression, pruning, and knowledge distillation, and how can we approach these limits in practice?

3. **Emergent Capabilities:** What theoretical explanations account for emergent capabilities in LLMs such as in-context learning, and can these be predicted or controlled?

4. **Architecture Understanding:** From a principled perspective, what makes transformer-based architectures successful, and under what conditions might alternative architectures (e.g., state-space models) be comparable or superior?

5. **Responsible AI Foundations:** What theoretical principles should govern fairness, privacy, alignment, and safety in the pre-training and fine-tuning paradigm of foundation models?
