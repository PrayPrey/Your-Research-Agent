## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we advance video-language models and video foundation models to effectively process, understand, and evaluate multimodal video data at scale?

### Refined Question

How can we develop video foundation models that overcome the challenges of (1) limited high-quality annotated video data, (2) efficient processing of large-scale video content, (3) coherent multimodal integration, and (4) standardized evaluation benchmarks for video-language alignment?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Data Annotation Gap:** How can we address the scarcity of high-quality, annotated video data that limits the development of advanced video-language models, given that video data typically lacks the detailed annotations available for text and image data?

2. **Scalable Processing:** What advancements in data processing techniques are needed to efficiently handle the large volume of video data (hundreds to thousands of frames per video) while maintaining detailed information capture and analysis quality?

3. **Multimodal Architecture:** How can we design sophisticated model architectures that coherently integrate audio, visual, temporal, and textual information from video data in a unified and effective manner?

4. **Evaluation Standards:** How can we develop robust video-language alignment benchmarks that enable fair evaluation and comparison of video-language model capabilities across different approaches and applications?
