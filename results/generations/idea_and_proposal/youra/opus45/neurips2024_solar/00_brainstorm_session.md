## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

How can we develop, deploy, and evaluate language models in a way that is socially responsible, addressing risks and harms related to security, privacy, bias, safety, transparency, and societal impact?

### Refined Question

What novel methods, frameworks, and evaluation approaches can advance socially responsible language modeling research, specifically addressing: (1) the identification and mitigation of security/privacy risks, (2) bias detection and fairness improvement, (3) safety and alignment techniques, and (4) transparency and interpretability mechanisms?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Security and Privacy:** How can we protect against security vulnerabilities and privacy breaches in language models during both training and deployment phases?

2. **Bias and Exclusion:** What methods can effectively detect, measure, and mitigate bias and exclusionary patterns in language models across diverse demographic groups and use cases?

3. **Development and Deployment Analysis:** How should responsible development practices, including crowdwork ethics and deployment protocols, be structured to minimize societal harms?

4. **Safety, Robustness, and Alignment:** What techniques can ensure language models remain aligned with human values and robust against adversarial attacks or misuse?

5. **Auditing and Evaluation:** How can we develop comprehensive red-teaming, auditing, and evaluation frameworks that reliably assess LM risks before and after deployment?

6. **Multimodal Risks:** What unique risks and harms emerge from novel input/output modalities in language models, and how can they be addressed?

7. **Transparency and Interpretability:** How can we make language model decision-making processes more transparent, explainable, and interpretable to various stakeholders?

8. **Social Good Applications:** How can language models be effectively deployed for social good, including applications for low-resource languages and underserved communities?
