## Research Question Development

### Initial Question

To what extent can machine learning models comprehend mathematics, and what applications could arise from this capability?

### Refined Question

How can deep learning approaches, particularly large language models, be advanced to achieve genuine mathematical comprehension—encompassing problem-solving, theorem proving, and reasoning—and what novel applications in science, engineering, education, and mathematics itself can emerge from these capabilities?

### Detailed Sub-Questions

1. **Human-Machine Comparison:** How do human-level mathematical reasoning processes differ from, complement, or intersect with current AI techniques? What can we learn from this comparison to improve AI mathematical capabilities?

2. **Benchmark Design:** How can we design benchmarks that accurately evaluate mathematical reasoning abilities in the era of large language models, avoiding memorization artifacts and testing genuine comprehension?

3. **Capability Advancement:** What novel techniques and architectures can move beyond current limitations in AI mathematical reasoning? How can we achieve deeper mathematical understanding rather than pattern matching?

4. **Educational Applications:** What role can deep learning models play in mathematics education, particularly in contexts with limited educational resources? How can AI tutoring systems adapt to individual learner needs?

5. **Domain Applications:** What near-term and long-term applications can AI mathematical reasoning enable across domains including software verification, scientific discovery, engineering optimization, finance, and advancing mathematical research itself?
